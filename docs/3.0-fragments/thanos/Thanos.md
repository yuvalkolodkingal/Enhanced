# Thanos

The Mad Titan of *Avengers: Infinity War*: a giant in golden armour, a Double-Edged Sword in one hand and the Infinity Gauntlet on the other. In the mod he is the **hardest boss**, and the way to get the **Infinity Gauntlet**: whoever holds the Gauntlet for three seconds with no power becomes Thanos.

Hebrew: [עברית](Thanos-HE)

This page is about fighting him. The first thing to know: the fight is on a **clock**. He starts with one Infinity Stone, and **every 90 seconds of fighting he takes another**, up to five. Every stone gives him new moves, and he never gets the sixth, the Mind Stone: it is not in his hand.

## Calling him

Hold **the Tesseract in one hand and Loki's Scepter in the other** (either hand) and **hold right-click for five seconds**. A spiral of the Space Stone's blue and another of the Mind Stone's gold rise round you, a bell tolls every second and the action bar counts the stones. Let go, swap an item out of a hand, or die, and it is cancelled. **Neither item is used up**, ever.

When the five seconds are over, a portal opens in the sky, a column of blue light marks where he will land (14 to 20 blocks from you, where there is room), and **six seconds later he falls through it**. For his first three seconds he is invulnerable (he rises from the landing). The fight is round the spot where you called him: a circle of 40 blocks, and the fight counts everyone within 64.

The call is turned down, with a red message in the action bar and nothing else happening (the Scepter does not explode and the Tesseract does not take you anywhere), when:

| Why | What it says |
|---|---|
| The server turned the call off, or you are a Spectator | "The stones do not answer on this server" |
| The difficulty is Peaceful | "The Titan will not come in Peaceful" |
| Mjolnir lies on you | "Mjolnir holds you down: you can't go anywhere" |
| He is already here | "He is already here" |
| Another boss is fighting | who it is, and where |
| The last fight was less than ten minutes ago | "The stones are quiet. Wait 7:42" |
| Not the Overworld | "The Titan answers only in the Overworld" |
| You are not on solid ground, or there are blocks over your head | "The Titan needs open sky above you" |
| Not enough open ground round you (fewer than 60 percent of 24 spots round you) | "The Titan needs open ground around you" |

An operator can call him anywhere with `/enhanced boss start thanos force` (see [Commands](Commands)): it skips the site and the rest, but not Peaceful or the one-fight-at-a-time rule.

## His body

* **3,000 health for one fighter**, and half as much again for each extra one (up to four fighters counted: 7,500). Creative and Spectator players do not count. The bar is **purple with one stone, blue with two, red, yellow, then green** (the colour of his newest stone); at five it says **"Only the Mind Stone remains"**, and when his damage starts to grow it says he is **enraged**.
* **Four armour plates**: his right pauldron breaks below 75 percent of his health, the left below 55, the breastplate below 35 and the helmet below 20. Each plate takes **10 percent** off the damage he takes. Mjolnir breaks his helmet at once, 3 seconds of the Scarlet Witch's Chaos Beam tear a plate away, and a Telekinetic Slam tears one (at most once in 6 seconds). His Rewind puts them back.
* **No hit takes more than 6 percent of his health, and no second more than 15 percent**, so there is no way to kill him quickly. A creature that is not a player (a golem, a wolf) does a quarter, and an Iron Golem that dares is swatted away with the back of his hand. **Stormbreaker does two and a half times** what it does to anything else.
* **He cannot be pushed, burned or poisoned**: no knockback, no fire, lava, drowning or falls, no Wither, Poison, Hunger, Nausea, Levitation, Mining Fatigue or Weakness, and no Slowness above level II. The Hex bounces him, Mjolnir cannot pin him, telekinesis cannot lift him and nothing can shapeshift him or confuse him.

## The clock of stones

| Stone | When (of combat) | Colour | New moves |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Power** | at the start | purple | Power Blast, Overload, Titan Shockwave, Power Pulse |
| **Space** | 1:30 | blue | Portal Step, Meteor Hail, Gravity Pull, Space Barrier |
| **Reality** | 3:00 | red | Reality Veil, Phantoms, Petrify Orb |
| **Soul** | 4:30 | orange | Soul Sight, Soul Drain, Soul World |
| **Time** | 6:00 | green | Rewind, Time Snare, **the Infinity Beam** |

* The clock only runs while **a Survival or Adventure player is in the arena**; nobody there, and it stands still (and he slowly heals).
* Ten seconds before a stone, its name appears and its voice hums. When it arrives he **kneels for three seconds**: a "sudden and painful surge", in which he does not attack and takes **half as much again** damage. That is your opening.
* After the fifth stone, if the fight drags on for another **three minutes**, his damage starts to grow, **4 percent each minute, up to 40 percent**.
* Every stone after the first makes everything he does **6 percent** stronger, and he rests a little less between moves.

## His moves

Every move has a **tell**: a ring on the ground, a line from his hand, a glow. Watch for it. The numbers are damage at the default settings.

**The sword and the fist** (always, no stone):

| Move | What it does | How to beat it |
|---|---|---|
| **Sword Sweep** | A wide arc ahead, 30. It breaks a raised Shield for 5 seconds and throws a rider off. | Be behind him, or out of the gold ring |
| **Overhead Smash** | The ground ahead, 26, then a ring of 12 spreading out. | Jump the ring |
| **Gauntlet Jab** | Two quick blows, 14 each. | Back away |
| **Sword Throw** | The sword flies 28 blocks and comes back, 22 each way. While it is away he has no Sweep or Smash. | A Chaos Shield turns it home |
| **Choke** | Lifts one player in front of him, 16 over two seconds and 8 more where he is thrown. He picks a Loki or a Vision first. | **60 damage from another fighter** frees you and staggers him |
| **Leap** | Crosses the arena and lands where you stood, 12. | Move |

**Power Stone**: **Power Blast** (a beam, 20, and 8 round where it ends; a block or a Chaos Shield stops it), **Overload** (purple sigils under up to three players, 16 each after a second: leave the circle), **Titan Shockwave** (a ring, 12, jumpable) and the **Power Pulse** (a fighter who hides where he can neither see nor reach is found after 8 seconds: 12 through the walls).

**Space Stone**: **Portal Step** (he appears behind you and strikes at once; a Hex wall refuses it), **Meteor Hail** (a ring under every player and a few more, 16 each, at most ten: leave your ring), **Gravity Pull** (draws in everyone he can see, then crushes, 10; cover breaks it) and the **Space Barrier** (a dome for 3 seconds that stops arrows, daggers and Thor's lightning, but not the Chaos Beam, Vision's beam or Stormbreaker).

**Reality Stone**: the **Reality Veil** (turns arrows and bolts within 12 blocks to bubbles for 6 seconds; Mjolnir and Stormbreaker are not stopped), **Phantoms** (three illusions of one health that jab for 3.5, while the real one is hidden for 4 seconds; any hit on him shows him) and the **Petrify Orb** (turns the first player it meets to stone for 3 seconds: they cannot move and take half damage; a Chaos Shield stops it).

**Soul Stone**: **Soul Sight** (dissolves Loki's duplicates, ends his invisibility and shapeshifting), **Soul Drain** (chains to up to two players, 3 every half second that armour cannot lessen, healing him 40 percent of it; a block, 28 blocks, or **60 poise** of blows breaks it) and the **Soul World** (a pillar of orange light: whoever is in it at the end is taken for 4 seconds and nothing can hurt them, and he heals 3 percent for each).

**Time Stone**: the **Rewind** (undoes the last six seconds of damage: up to 8 percent of his health, the plates and his place; it fails if he is staggered), the **Time Snare** (mandalas under two players: whoever is inside is slowed to 40 percent for 4 seconds) and **the Infinity Beam** (only with all five stones: a huge beam that sweeps across the arena, 40; only a block or the edge stops it, a Chaos Shield breaks, and Stormbreaker cuts it).

## How the fight is beaten

* **Clenching.** Every stone move starts with him closing the Gauntlet. **100 poise of blows** during the wind-up (a point of damage is about two poise, and the stones' own damage counts three times) breaks the clench: he is **staggered** for two seconds, takes a quarter more damage, and **his stones are dark for four seconds**: only the sword and the fist. Spider-Man's web does the same. The sword moves cannot be interrupted.
* **Stormbreaker** thrown into a beam cuts it and staggers him.
* **A Vision** is what he hunts first. Then whoever hurt him most in the last ten seconds. Then the nearest.
* **A Chaos Shield** stops his beams, meteors, orb and sword, and pays for each (the sword costs double). It is not enough against the Infinity Beam.
* **Quicksilver Time** slows him to half, and not at all once he has the Time Stone. Magneto's strip takes nothing from him.
* Nobody is forced to stay: there are no walls round the arena, and **the world is never changed** (no block is ever broken or placed, unless the server turned *blockDamage* on).

## When it ends

* **He is beaten.** He kneels, says his last words and turns to dust (five seconds, invulnerable). **Everyone who did 3 percent of his health** (and was there for the shared minimum, a minute) **and whoever struck the last blow** gets **an Infinity Gauntlet**, straight into the inventory; if it is full it drops for them alone; if they are dead or away it waits and arrives when they are back. Each of them also gets 1,500 experience points, and **12,000 more fall where he stood**. Creative players are never rewarded. He cannot be called again for **ten minutes**.
* **Every fighter is dead for 15 seconds**, or **nobody is near him for 90 seconds** ("Thanos has lost interest and left"): he steps through a ring of Space and is gone. Nobody is rewarded, and the rest is five minutes.
* The fight is never saved: if the server stops, he is gone.

## The Infinity Gauntlet

The reward. **Hold right-click for three seconds with no power** to become Thanos. It is used up; a hero who tries is told that their body already holds a power, and nothing is used up. In Creative it is kept. Playing Thanos has its own section below.
