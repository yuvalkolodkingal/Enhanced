# Witchcraft

Witchcraft (3.17) is magic of its own, for players with no hero power, after *Agatha All Along*'s coven: a witch has one **path**, an affinity with one element, and the spells that go with it. Billy has spells of his own, the grimoires teach theirs, and the hero sorcerers (the Scarlet Witch, Agatha, Doctor Strange, Loki) cast what their grimoires taught them.

## The paths

| Path | Element | What it is for |
|---|---|---|
| **Green Witch** | earth | Vines and herbs that trap, healing by nature and the moon (stronger under the night sky), and the Witches' Road opened for a whole coven |
| **Potions Witch** | water | Strong combat elixirs brewed in seconds, a truth serum, antidotes and healing draughts |
| **Protection Witch** | fire | Protective circles that keep dark magic out, reversal spells that turn magic back, shields for her friends |
| **Divination Witch** | air | Dodging blows (Foresight), enemies seen through walls (True Sight), attacks foreseen (Premonition), watching sigils |
| **Spirit Witch** | chaos | The rare all-rounder (like Agatha, Wanda and Billy): she learns every path's spells |
| **Sorcerer** | the mystic arts | Eldritch bolts, the Mandala Shield, the Crimson Bands of Cyttorak, sling portals |

A witch has a **level**, 1 to 5: each path's spells open by level, and the higher the level the stronger the spells and the larger the mana pool. Mana comes back by itself, twice as fast with another caster near (a **coven**).

### Becoming a witch

* **The Demiurge** (Billy, with [Ultimate Mode](Ultimate-Mode) on) gives witchcraft to any player with no hero power on his **Grant Magic** screen: he chooses the player, the path (or a sorcerer's art) and the level, and he can take it back.
* **The Coven's Road:** at the end of the Witches' Road walked together, someone with no power may choose witchcraft as their prize (a first-level path).
* Operators: `/enhanced witch grant <player> <path> [level]` (see [Commands](Commands#witchcraft-enhanced-witch)).

## Casting

* the **Spells key** (`;` unless you change it) casts the chosen spell. **Sneak + the Spells key** opens the **Spell Wheel**: rings of glowing seals round the crosshair, one per spell you know, each in its colour; point at one and let go of the key (or click) to choose it. The game goes on behind it.
* A witch's **R G V B N** (and the same with **Sneak**) cast her first ten spells directly.
* Most spells are **chanted** first: the words are spoken aloud to everyone near (Billy's are *"I want ..."*), runes circle the caster's hands and a circle forms at the feet. **Holding the key chants twice as fast.** A heavy blow (4 damage or more) **breaks the incantation off** and loses half its mana. The Demiurge in his star form needs no incantation.
* The panel to the right of the hotbar shows your mana, the chosen spell, the incantation as you speak it, and the Darkhold's corruption.

## The grimoires

The first time a witch, a sorcerer, Billy or a hero sorcerer uses a grimoire, they **read** it and its spells are theirs; after that the book does what it always did.

* **The Darkhold** (Agatha's cottage): forbidden spells: Dreamwalk (your spirit walks free while your body sleeps), Power Siphon, Soul Binding (what hurts you hurts them), Necromancy. Their light is darker, they hit harder, and **every one corrupts**: corruption darkens your magic, makes it hurt more and drains your own life with every spell. The Light of the Vishanti, an Antidote, the Unbinding and the Coven's Road cleanse it.
* **The Book of the Vishanti** (the Sanctum's library): defensive and cleansing light: the Light of the Vishanti, the Shield of the Vishanti (a dome no harm or dark magic enters), Banishment (every summoned thing sent back).
* **Cagliostro's Grimoire** (new, the Sanctum's library and relics; Creative): the spells of time: the Chrono Hold, the Time Loop (cast again within twenty seconds, or take a deadly blow, and you are back in the marked moment), the Dark Dimension.

## Billy's spells

**A hero casts spells only with [Ultimate Mode](Ultimate-Mode) on** (with it off every hero is exactly the hero of before 3.17: no mana, no spells). Then Billy is the Demiurge and has every spell, his own among them: the **Unbinding** (*"Dimitte eam..."*: frees you and your friends of mind control, stuns, curses and Wanda's hold; it breaks a Hex's runes), the **broomstick**, sigils and the **Rune of Protection**, ghosts raised and banished, the **Dream Binding**, the **Siphon** and the **Coven Circle**, and his powers over space and reality (the table below lists them all); the hero sorcerers (the Scarlet Witch, Agatha, Doctor Strange, Loki) cast the coven's first spells and what the grimoires taught them; Cosmic Spider-Man has matter and portals.

## The Coven's Road and the Mindscape

* **The Coven's Road:** a Green Witch (or Billy) opens a portal to the Witches' Road; **everyone who steps in** while it is open walks it together, to the Road's End, a round hall with an amethyst dais. There each one chooses **a prize of their own**: to be **unbound** (nothing holds you until you die), to **find a player**, to **bring back the dead** (a hardcore world's fallen players), **witchcraft** (someone with no power), or **the Road's blessing**. A prize you already have is never offered.
* **The Witches' Mindscape** (**Billy, and only the witches and sorcerers whose magic is Billy's own gift**, given on the Demiurge's Grant Magic screen; a witch made by a command or by the Road does not find the way in, nor does a hero sorcerer): your mind goes to one round hall shared by all of them, Billy's place of meditation, where what you say in chat is **heard only there**. Cast it again to come back.

### The Demiurge

| Spell | Level | Billy's rank | Mana | Incantation | What it does |

| **Grant Magic** | - | - | 0 | at once: *Accipe.* | The Demiurge gives witchcraft or a sorcerer's art to a player with no power, or takes it back. |

Mana is the spell's cost; the incantation is how long it is chanted (half as long while the Spells key is held). Level is the witch's level that opens it; Billy's rank is the rank that opens it for Billy. The Demiurge knows every spell.

## Settings

The `[witchcraft]` section of the server config: whether witchcraft is in the game at all, mana regeneration and the coven's reach, the spells' damage, what interrupts a chant, how much each Darkhold spell corrupts, whether spells touch other players, how long conjured blocks and the Infinite Hallway last, whether the Coven's Road may bring back the dead and offer witchcraft, and whether the Mindscape's chat is private.
