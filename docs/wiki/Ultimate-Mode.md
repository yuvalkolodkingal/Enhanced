# Ultimate Mode

Ultimate Mode (3.17) is one server setting that gives **every hero an ultimate version**. It is **off by default**. Turn it on with `[ultimate] enabled = true` in the server config, or for one world with **`/enhanced ultimate on`** (`off`, `info`, `default`; operators; saved with the world in `serverconfig/enhanced-ultimate.json`, which then wins over the config). Every player is told at once.

It replaced the separate switches of before: 3.16's `vision.solar_jewel.enabled` is gone, and a 3.16 server config that had it on turns Ultimate Mode on.

**With Ultimate Mode off, nothing of it exists.** Every hero looks, plays and is keyed exactly as before 3.17: the same skins and crowns, the same powers and numbers, the same keys. No hero has mana or spells, the Ultimate Form key does nothing, Source Code Vision does not burn, Billy is Billy, and the Darkhold and the Book of the Vishanti do what they always did. (Only the witches and sorcerers of [Witchcraft](Witchcraft), players with no hero, are there with the mode off.)

## The ultimate forms (the Ultimate Form key)

With Ultimate Mode on, the **Ultimate Form key** (`'` unless you change it) awakens your hero's ultimate form: you rise in a column of light with your form's aura forming round you, and then for 45 seconds you have its strengths (status effects, a quarter more damage) and its aura. **the key again** while it lasts fires its **signature move** (every 10 seconds). After it ends, 3 minutes pass before it can be awakened again. All of it is tunable in `[ultimate]`.

Six heroes change more than that:

### Source Code Vision: the Solar Jewel

With Ultimate Mode on, Vision in his Source Code look runs on the **Solar Jewel** in his brow (the Solar Beam, Jewel Overload and the infrared eye beams; see [Vision](Vision#solar-jewel-vision)); any Vision has it while his ultimate form lasts. (Off, the Source Code has Vision's own powers, stronger.)

**He burns.** While he flies or uses a power, his **whole body is fire**, like the Human Torch: a figure of molten red, bright yellow-white cracks of fire running over every part of it (head, body, arms, legs), a sheath of flame hugging his silhouette, long tongues of flame and a trail of fire streaming behind him, embers and sparks. It is only light: nothing is set on fire and he never burns. With Ultimate Mode off he does not burn at all.

### Billy: the Demiurge

With Ultimate Mode on, **Billy is the Demiurge all the time**:

* **twenty hearts** (40 health), **protection like a full set of Netherite** on top of what he wears, and he heals faster;
* his **barrier wraps his whole body**: Sneak + B raises a shell of bright blue round him, small stars turning inside it;
* his abilities are a **much brighter blue with small stars in them**, and hit much harder;
* **Magic types (the Magic Types key, `,`):** he takes on another magic user's magic: **the Scarlet Witch, Doctor Strange, Agatha, Loki**, and back to his own. While he has it, **all his keys work exactly as on that character**: R G V B N and their Sneak variants are that hero's abilities (the powers panel shows that hero's list under "Billy ✦ the hero"), Doctor Strange's Y is his too, in the Demiurge's blue with stars. One key more, the Magic Types key, moves on to the next magic;
* he knows **every spell** of [Witchcraft](Witchcraft), and in his star form (the Ultimate Form key) needs no incantation;
* **Grant Magic:** on his Grant Magic screen he gives witchcraft (a path and its strength) or a sorcerer's art to any player with no hero power, and takes it back.

**His look:** at rest, his own face (the Teen's), the starry cosmic suit under orange shoulder plates, a **red scarf** wound round his neck and a **red cape** down his back. Flying or using a power, the **body of stars** with hands and feet of light, white hair and eyes of light, in the **great red cloak**: real red cloth (never light), long and wide, flaring out behind and to both sides with its hem torn into long waving tongues, its **hood** up over his head; rings of blue energy at his hands. The cloak grows and falls back as he changes. **His crown** is the crown Billy always had (it forms as he flies and after his spells), only **brighter and bluer, with small stars of energy alive in it**. Nobody else has a crown from Ultimate Mode.

### The Scarlet Witch: the Nexus Being

With Ultimate Mode on, Wanda's ultimate form is the **Nexus Being**: her own face of the films, in a **sleek red suit with touches of black** (the red and black of her newest comics: an hourglass of red between black flanks, long red gauntlets, black leggings into tall red boots) and a red cape. She wears no new crown: **her crown is her own crown**, shown when she wears it, exactly as before. While she is the Nexus Being:

* **every hurt heals back within two seconds** (as long as she lives through it);
* her abilities have **no cooldowns**, are a much brighter red and hit far harder;
* **the whole world she stands in is hers**, not a Hex's walls: its sky turns red and **runes cover it**, and those runes **strip every other hero in it of his powers**; each world she goes to while the form lasts is hers too (**she holds several at once**);
* she grows **stronger (faster, tougher, her chaos magic harder) for each hero** her runes strip;
* **the Chaos Burst:** every two seconds **all the ground and all the sky within 50 blocks** of her throw bright red energy in every direction, up out of the ground and down out of the sky, and it strikes **everyone within 50 blocks except her** (15 damage each; `ultimate.nexusFlashRange`, `nexusFlashDamage`). Her signature move is the same burst at once, twice as hard;
* **nobody can step inside her Chaos Shield**, and whatever is shot into it falls;
* the form has **no time limit and no cooldown**: **Sneak + O** ends it.

### Spider-Man: Cosmic Spider-Man

Captain Universe: the Uni-Power in Spider-Man, in the starry black, blue and white suit.

* **Strength three times the Hulk's** at full rage, **protection like Netherite**, and he heals twice as fast as a man;
* he **flies at great speed**, energy streaming behind him;
* the Ultimate Form key fires an **energy blast**: from his hands, then from his eyes (thinner and longer), turn about; **Sneak + the Ultimate Form key leaves the form early** (the Uni-Power he has left stays, and climbs back);
* **matter and portals** are his as spells on the Spells key (conjured weapons, food, armour, bridges, walls and platforms of light, transmutation; the sling portal, combat portals and the Dimension Gate), at about two thirds of Billy's strength;
* a **sharper spider-sense**: whatever means him harm within 40 blocks shows through walls;
* **the Uni-Power is a meter of 170.** It only runs down while the form lasts (a little over four minutes; each blast costs some) and does not come back in the form. Empty, he is Spider-Man again and it climbs back; at 170 he can awaken the form again. The form has no other time limit;
* it is life itself: **dark magic hurts him half as much again**.

### Iron Man: the Godkiller

The Godkiller armour, a giant of gold.

* **65 blocks tall.** His whole body is that size (the mod raises the game's limit on a body's size for it): he can be struck anywhere on it, from his boots to his helm, and he reaches and strikes what stands right under his feet, 65 blocks below his eyes;
* **more than Netherite's protection**: blows and energy do 30% less to it, magic 10% more;
* his suit's **energy lasts as 230 would**; great strength; every move of his suit hits two and a half times as hard;
* the Ultimate Form key fires the **giant repulsor cannons of the chest and both hands**; with Sneak, the **stamp**: everything under him, all the way down.

### Tommy: Ultimate Speed

* While his super speed or his slow time is on, **every other player in his world is slowed**, three times as much as his slow time slows those beside him, **and their flight is a third as fast**;
* **protection like Netherite**;
* the Ultimate Form key raises the **great tornado**: he spins on the spot and a whirlwind nine blocks wide stands for ten seconds, throwing his enemies round and out and **stopping whatever is shot or cast into it, energy too**; with Sneak, **time quickened** on himself or a friend he looks at: a great healing, for some hunger;
* **spells hurt him less** (40% less), and **no power of time touches him** (a Time Stone's slow, another speedster's slow time);
* his blows go **through magic shields** (the Chaos Shield, Billy's barrier, Agatha's shield, Doctor Strange's Shield of the Seraphim, a circle of protection), and he **runs through the wall of a Hex**, in or out;
* **his look** is the comics' Speed (the dark green suit with the lighter green bolt, pale sleeves, spiky white hair, silver goggles with yellow lenses), and his speed's light, his trail and his tornado run **green and light pink-purple**;
* the **cosmic battery**: the Scarlet Witch and Billy within 16 blocks of him are fed (strength, healing, their magic refilled);
* his **running costs no hunger**.
