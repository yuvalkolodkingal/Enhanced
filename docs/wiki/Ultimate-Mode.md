# Ultimate Mode

Ultimate Mode (3.17) is one server setting that gives **every hero an ultimate version**. It is **off by default**. Turn it on with `[ultimate] enabled = true` in the server config, or for one world with **`/enhanced ultimate on`** (`off`, `info`, `default`; operators; saved with the world in `serverconfig/enhanced-ultimate.json`, which then wins over the config). Every player is told at once.

It replaced the separate switches of before: 3.16's `vision.solar_jewel.enabled` is gone, and a 3.16 server config that had it on turns Ultimate Mode on.

## The ultimate forms (O)

With Ultimate Mode on, **O** awakens your hero's ultimate form: you rise in a column of light with your form's aura forming round you, and then for 45 seconds you have its strengths (status effects, a quarter more damage) and its aura. **O again** while it lasts fires its **signature move** (every 10 seconds). After it ends, 3 minutes pass before it can be awakened again. All of it is tunable in `[ultimate]`.

Three heroes change more than that:

### Source Code Vision: the Solar Jewel

With Ultimate Mode on, Vision in his Source Code look runs on the **Solar Jewel** in his brow (the Solar Beam, Jewel Overload and the infrared eye beams; see [Vision](Vision#solar-jewel-vision)); any Vision has it while his ultimate form lasts. (Off, the Source Code has Vision's own powers, stronger.)

### Billy: the Demiurge

With Ultimate Mode on, **Billy is the Demiurge all the time**:

* **twenty hearts** (40 health), **protection like a full set of Netherite** on top of what he wears, and he heals faster;
* his **barrier wraps his whole body**: Sneak + B raises a shell of bright blue round him, small stars turning inside it;
* his abilities are a **much brighter blue with small stars in them**, and hit much harder;
* **Magic types (Sneak + O):** his keys take on another magic user's abilities, in his blue with stars: **the Scarlet Witch, Doctor Strange, Agatha, Loki**, and back to his own (the panel shows which);
* he knows **every spell** of [Witchcraft](Witchcraft), and in his star form (O) needs no incantation;
* **Grant Magic:** on his Grant Magic screen he gives witchcraft (a path and its strength) or a sorcerer's art to any player with no hero power, and takes it back.

**His look:** at rest, the starry cosmic suit with the red scarf and cape and his own face, a crown of bright blue starry energy over his head; flying or using a power, the **star body in the great red hooded cloak** flaring out round him, white hair, his face in shadow, rings of blue energy at his hands. He changes between them with a burst of stars.

### The Scarlet Witch: the Nexus Being

With Ultimate Mode on, Wanda's ultimate form is the **Nexus Being**: Wanda of the films in the cosmic Nexus suit, deep scarlet with a star field, red chaos curling off her hands. While she is the Nexus Being:

* **every hurt heals back within two seconds** (as long as she lives through it);
* her abilities have **no cooldowns**, are a much brighter red and hit far harder;
* **the whole world she stands in is hers**, not a Hex's walls: its sky turns red and **runes cover it**, and those runes **strip every other hero in it of his powers**; each world she goes to while the form lasts is hers too (**she holds several at once**);
* she grows **stronger (faster, tougher, her chaos magic harder) for each hero** her runes strip;
* **chaos flashes** out of everything within **50 blocks** of her at her enemies, **15 damage** each; they never touch her;
* **nobody can step inside her Chaos Shield**, and whatever is shot into it falls;
* the form has **no time limit and no cooldown**: **Sneak + O** ends it.
