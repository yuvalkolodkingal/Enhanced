# Becoming a Hero

Every hero has one way in. Recipes are on [Items and Crafting](Items-and-Crafting), which also lists where the rare items are found.

## The rules

| Rule | Detail |
|---|---|
| One power per player | A player is exactly one of: no powers, Magneto, Scarlet Witch, Quicksilver, Vision, Tommy, Billy, Loki, Thor, the Phoenix, Spider-Man, Thanos, Ultron or Apocalypse. Powers never stack. |
| Permanent | Once unlocked, a power survives death, logging out and changing dimension. Only an operator command removes it: `/enhanced power set <player> none` (see [Commands](Commands)). |
| Creative and Spectator | Creative and Spectator players are never killed by the Mind Stone or the Hex wall. |

## The ways in

| Hero | How |
|---|---|
| [Magneto](Magneto) | Craft **Magneto's Helmet** from 5 Netherite Blocks (helmet shape) and put it on. |
| [Scarlet Witch](Scarlet-Witch) | Find the very rare **Scarlet Witch Gene**. Hold it in one hand and use **Loki's Scepter** with the other to touch the Mind Stone (**without the gene, you explode**), or use the **Tesseract** with the other hand instead. |
| [Quicksilver](Quicksilver) | Craft **Quicksilver's Goggles** (sugar, iron ingots, glass panes and a Block of Diamond) and put them on. |
| [Vision](Vision) | Craft a **Synthezoid Core** (Blocks of Redstone, Netherite Ingots and a Nether Star). Hold it in one hand and use **Loki's Scepter** with the other. |
| [Tommy](Tommy) or [Billy](Billy-and-the-Witches-Road) | Craft the **Witches' Road Door** (amethyst shards, soul lanterns, dark oak trapdoors and an Eye of Ender) and place it in the floor. Open it holding a **Scarlet Witch Gene**, walk the three trials of the Road, and choose which of Wanda's sons you become. |
| [Loki](Loki) | Find the **Tesseract** and use it: you vanish in a blue flash and reappear far away, as Loki. (With a **Scarlet Witch Gene** in your other hand it makes you the Scarlet Witch instead.) |
| [Thor](Thor) | Forge **Mjolnir** (Netherite Ingots, a Nether Star, a Heavy Core and a Breeze Rod). Only the worthy can lift it, and the first worthy player who lifts it claims it for good. Lift it, and lightning makes you Thor. |
| [Phoenix](Phoenix) | Find a very rare **Phoenix Ember** in a chest (End City, Bastion, Ancient City, Stronghold library, Nether Fortress and Woodland Mansion chests), hold it with no power, and **hold right-click for 3 seconds**. The Ember burns into you and you awaken as Jean Grey. |
| [Spider-Man](Spider-Man) | Craft a **Radioactive Spider** (Fermented Spider Eyes, Glow Ink Sacs and a Slime Block), or find one in a mineshaft, dungeon or Ancient City chest, a Trial Chambers reward chest (or, rarely, from a Cave Spider you kill). With no power, **right-click with it**: it bites you, you are sick for 7 seconds, and then you awaken as Peter Parker. |
| [Thanos](Thanos) | Hold the **Tesseract** in one hand and **Loki's Scepter** in the other and **hold right-click for five seconds** to call the **Mad Titan**. Beat him, and hold the **Infinity Gauntlet** he rewards for three seconds with no power. |
| [Ultron](Ultron) | Beat the **Ultron** boss (woken with the **Ultron Program** and Loki's Scepter) and take his **Core**. Hold the Core in one hand and use **Loki's Scepter** with the other. |
| [Apocalypse](Apocalypse) | Craft a **Capstone** (a Nether Star, five Gold Blocks and a Lapis Block), plant it in sand by day with a Scarlet Witch or a Phoenix near, beat **En Sabah Nur**, and hold the **Essence** he leaves for three seconds with no power. |

## Step by step

### Magneto

Craft the helmet and wear it. The first time you wear it, all of Magneto's abilities unlock for good (if you have no other power). While you wear the helmet, your skin turns into Magneto's classic suit.

### Scarlet Witch

1. Find a **Scarlet Witch Gene**: a very rare item in the chests of woodland mansions, strongholds, Nether fortresses, bastions, ancient cities, End cities, jungle temples and desert pyramids, and dropped by witches and evokers. On its own it does nothing at all.
2. Find **Loki's Scepter**, a relic found in End City treasure chests, stronghold libraries, bastion treasure chests, ancient cities and woodland mansions. The Mind Stone sits in its blue gem.
3. Hold the gene in one hand and **right-click** with the scepter in the other. The gene and the stone's energy fuse, the gene is used up, and you awaken as the Scarlet Witch. Wanda's Crown appears in your inventory: wear it to transform.

The **Tesseract** works too: hold the gene in one hand and **right-click** with the Tesseract in the other, instead of the scepter, and you awaken as the Scarlet Witch in exactly the same way. The gene is used up and Wanda's Crown appears, the Tesseract is not used up, and you are not taken anywhere. Without the gene the Tesseract makes you Loki, as always.

If you touch the Mind Stone **without the gene, it tears you apart**: you explode and die instantly. (Creative and Spectator players survive.) If you already are the Scarlet Witch or Vision, touching the stone is safe. If you have a different power, the gene can't bond and nothing happens.

### Quicksilver

Craft the goggles and wear them. Like Magneto's Helmet, the first time you wear them you become Quicksilver for good, if you have no other power.

### Vision

Craft the **Synthezoid Core**, hold it in one hand and use **Loki's Scepter** with the other. The Mind Stone moves into the body: the core is used up and you become Vision for good. Holding the core also protects you from the stone, exactly like the gene does for Wanda.

### Tommy and Billy

1. Craft a **Witches' Road Door** and place it in the floor.
2. As a player with no power, in Survival mode, right-click it while holding a **Scarlet Witch Gene**. A ring of blue-green light runs around the door, the doors open onto a spiral stair, and the Road asks: **"Seekest thou the Road?"** Press **Walk the Road**.
3. Walk the **three trials** of [The Witches' Road](Witches-Road), alone, in a place of your own: a Parkour over the void, a structure to copy block by block, and a long ice-boat race. Each success opens the next; falling never hurts, and you can leave at any time with your Gene.
4. At the end of the Road, choose **Billy: Wiccan** or **Tommy: Speed**. One gene is used up, and you are taken back to the place where you came in. <!-- ROAD-ENDING: planned ending, see Witches-Road -->

Without a gene the door won't open ("The Road only opens for witch blood"). A hero already "walks their own road". (A server can switch the trials off with `witches_road.enabled`; the door then gives the instant choice of 3.1.) See [Billy and the Witches' Road](Billy-and-the-Witches-Road).

### Loki

Find the **Tesseract**. It is not crafted: it turns up in End City treasure chests, stronghold libraries, woodland mansions and ancient cities (chances are on [Items and Crafting](Items-and-Crafting)). Use it (right-click) as a player with no power: the Space Stone swirls round you, you vanish in a blue flash and reappear 100 to 250 blocks away, on safe ground in the same dimension, as Loki. The cube is never used up. If no safe spot is found, you stay where you are and still become Loki.

Holding a **Scarlet Witch Gene** in your other hand changes that: you awaken as the [Scarlet Witch](Scarlet-Witch) instead, the same way as with Loki's Scepter, and you are not taken anywhere.

A hero who uses the Tesseract does not become anyone else: they are simply taken to the block they look at, even with a gene in the other hand. See [Loki](Loki).

### Thor

1. Craft **Mjolnir**. A Mjolnir nobody has claimed yet belongs to nobody.
2. Be **worthy**: Thor himself, **Vision**, anyone who has proven themselves a hero by **saving a village from a raid** (the *Hero of the Village* advancement), a player in Creative or Spectator mode, or everyone, if the server option `thor.anyoneWorthy` is on.
3. **Lift it.** The first worthy player who lifts a Mjolnir **claims it** (a worthy player who forges one claims it at once): from then on only its owner can lift it, and its tooltip says "Bound to" followed by the owner's name.
4. If you have no other power, lightning strikes and you become **Thor**. A Vision who claims Mjolnir fights with it but stays Vision.

Everyone else finds Mjolnir far too heavy: it slips out of their hands. See [Thor](Thor) for the details, including the server option that turns ownership off.

### Phoenix

1. Find a **Phoenix Ember**. It is not crafted: it turns up, very rarely, in End City and bastion treasure chests, Ancient City chests, stronghold libraries, Nether fortresses and woodland mansions (chances are on [Items and Crafting](Items-and-Crafting)).
2. With **no power**, hold the Ember in either hand and **hold right-click for 3 seconds**. Gold and orange flames spiral up around you and you hear the Phoenix gather. Let go early and the flames sputter out; nothing is used up.
3. At the end the Ember burns into you and is used up (not in Creative), a burst of fire goes out that hurts nobody, and you are **the Phoenix**: you can't be burned, and you have five abilities on **R G V B N**.

A hero who tries is told "Your body already holds a power" and keeps the Ember. Loki's Scepter and the Scarlet Witch Gene have nothing to do with the Phoenix: the Ember is her way in. See [Phoenix](Phoenix).

### Spider-Man

1. Get a **Radioactive Spider**. Craft it from 4 Fermented Spider Eyes, 4 Glow Ink Sacs and a Slime Block (the recipe is on [Items and Crafting](Items-and-Crafting#radioactive-spider); it unlocks when you pick up a Glow Ink Sac), or find it, in a chest of an abandoned mineshaft, a dungeon or an Ancient City, in the reward chest of a Trial Chambers, or as a very rare drop of a Cave Spider that you kill.
2. With **no power**, hold it and **right-click**. You hear the bite, green motes rise from your hand, one spider is used up (not in Creative) and you take 1 damage.
3. **For 7 seconds you are sick** (Nausea, Weakness, Slowness, and Poison at the end, which never kills). Drink milk and the bite is cured: nothing happens. Otherwise, when the 7 seconds are over, you awaken as **Spider-Man** in the Homemade Suit, and everyone on the server is told.

A hero who tries is told "The spider's bite changes nothing: your power does not mix" and keeps the spider. Press **H** to put on the Iron Spider or the Brand New Day suit. See [Spider-Man](Spider-Man).

### Thanos

Thanos is the end of a long road, and, like Ultron and Apocalypse, a hero you cannot simply craft or find: you win him from his boss. Hold **the Tesseract in one hand and Loki's Scepter in the other** and hold right-click for five seconds, in open country with the sky above you, to **call the Mad Titan**: a portal opens in the sky and he falls through it. Beat him (he takes a new Infinity Stone every 90 seconds of fighting, so do it fast and do it together) and he rewards **an Infinity Gauntlet** to everyone who did 3 percent of his health and to whoever struck the last blow. **Hold the Gauntlet for three seconds with no power** and you become Thanos. Neither the Tesseract nor the Scepter is ever used up. The fight is on [Thanos](Thanos).

Once you hold the Gauntlet you are Thanos: the five stones of the Power Blast, the Portal Step, the Transmute, the Soul Drain and the Rewind are on **R, G, V, B and N** (Sneak for the other five), and you are a tank with a toolbox. You can also give the Gauntlet to a friend who has no power, and take the fight again later. See [Playing Thanos](Thanos#playing-thanos).

### Ultron

Like Thanos and Apocalypse, Ultron is a hero you cannot become without a fight: you must beat him first.

1. Craft the **Ultron Program** (a Nether Star, four Blocks of Iron, two Echo Shards and two Blocks of Redstone: you need a Wither and an Ancient City). See [Items and Crafting](Items-and-Crafting#the-ultron-program).
2. Find **Loki's Scepter** (End City treasure chests, stronghold libraries, bastion treasure chests, ancient cities and woodland mansions). Hold the Program in one hand and the Scepter in the other, look at open, level, dry ground with room above, and **Sneak + right-click**. The Program is used up, scrap rises out of the ground, and in six seconds Ultron stands there with six Sentries.
3. **Beat him**, with friends: dodge the tells, clear the Sentries, keep your feet inside the ring when Sokovia rises, and destroy his body again and again until no Sentry is left. Everyone who fought for a minute and hurt him or his Sentries gets one **Ultron's Core**, and so does whoever lands the last blow. See [Ultron](Ultron).
4. With **no power**, hold the **Core** in one hand and use **Loki's Scepter** with the other. The Mind Stone gives the Core a mind: the Core is used up and you become Ultron for good. (With a power already, nothing is used up: keep the Core for later or give it to a friend. Holding the Core also keeps the Mind Stone from killing you, like the gene.)

A server can switch Ultron off. Operators can start the fight without the Program: [Commands](Commands).

### Apocalypse

Apocalypse is the end of a fight, and the only hero who needs a friend with a particular power. Craft the **Capstone** and **Sneak + right-click it on sand** (or sandstone) in daylight under the open sky: a wall of sand rises round a circle of 24 blocks, a violet portal opens and **En Sabah Nur steps out**, inside a golden shield that **stops everything except mind power**. The ritual refuses unless a **Scarlet Witch or a Phoenix** is within 64 blocks, because only they can break the shield; bring them, and bring a team for the twelve-second windows when it shatters. Beat him (three shields, three phases, his Horsemen, the fall of his armour) and **every fighter who stayed a minute and hurt him or his shield** gets the **Essence of En Sabah Nur**, and so does whoever struck the last blow. **Hold the Essence for three seconds with no power** and you become Apocalypse. The Capstone is used up when the ritual starts and given back if the fight fails for a reason that was not yours. The fight is on [Apocalypse](Apocalypse).

Once you hold the Essence you are Apocalypse: **sand waves and entombing** on **R**, **levitation** on **G**, a violet **portal** on **V**, a **golden shield** that stops everything but mind power on **B**, and **power bestowal** (and, with Sneak, a **sandstorm**) on **N**. You can give the Essence to a friend who has no power. See [the hero](Apocalypse#becoming-apocalypse-the-hero).
