# Items and Crafting

Every item you can use is in the **Enhanced** creative tab. Thirteen are crafted, five more are found in chests (or dropped), three more are the rewards of the bosses, and one, the [Hero Creator](#hero-creator), is for Creative mode only. The Radioactive Spider and Professor X's Wheelchair are both: you can craft them and find them. Since 3.6 there are also [the Infinity Stones and their vessels](#the-infinity-stones-and-their-vessels-36), Doctor Strange's [Eye of Agamotto](#eye-of-agamotto-36) and [Sling Ring](#sling-ring-36), the Hulk's [Gamma Emitter](#gamma-emitter-36) and [Hydra's gear](#hydras-gear-36). Since 3.7: the [Mutant Cure](#mutant-cure-331) (it is older, from 3.3.1, but now has a home), Deadpool's [chimichanga](#chimichanga-37), the [Stone Locator](#stone-locator-37), and the [Adamantium Injection](#adamantium-injection-37), found in the X-Men's lab. Since 3.8: [the Avengers' gear](#the-avengers-gear-38), found in the Avengers Tower (the Super Soldier Serum, the Vibranium Shield, the Falcon Exo-Wings, the two Arc Reactors and Iron Man's six suits). In the recipe grids below, `.` is an empty slot, and the recipes are **shaped**: the items must sit exactly as drawn.

### Magneto's Helmet

```
N N N
N . N

N = Block of Netherite
```

Worn, it makes you [Magneto](Magneto). It protects like a full Netherite set (20 armor, 12 toughness, 40% knockback resistance), blocks Nausea, and never breaks.

### Wanda's Crown

```
G R G
G . G

G = Gold Ingot
R = Block of Redstone
```

Given to the Scarlet Witch when she awakens, and craftable if lost. On her: 20 hearts and Diamond-level protection. It never breaks. On anyone else it is just a crown.

I = Iron Ingot
G = Glass Pane

```

Worn, they make you [Quicksilver](Quicksilver). They protect like an iron helmet and never break.

N S N
R N R

A synthetic body waiting for a mind. Hold it in one hand and use Loki's Scepter with the other, and you become [Vision](Vision).

### Witches' Road Door

```

T E T
A L A

A = Amethyst Shard
L = Soul Lantern
T = Dark Oak Trapdoor

```

A door that is placed flat in the floor. Open it holding a Scarlet Witch Gene, with no power, to walk the three trials of [The Witches' Road](Witches-Road) and become Billy or Tommy: see [Billy and the Witches' Road](Billy-and-the-Witches-Road). An axe breaks it fastest, pistons can't push it, and breaking it drops it again.

### Mjolnir

```
N S N
N H N
. R .

N = Netherite Ingot
S = Nether Star
H = Heavy Core
R = Breeze Rod
```

Only the worthy can lift it, and it is bound to the first worthy player who does (its tooltip says "Unclaimed" until then, and "Bound to" and the owner's name after). 10 attack damage (slow, like an axe), never breaks, fire and lava can't hurt it. In Thor's hands it shatters into fragments that strike his enemies (Sneak + R), and its owner can pin someone down with it (Sneak + right-click on a creature or player). **3.3: it is also found** lying in the middle of a [Mjolnir's crater](Structures#mjolnirs-crater) (deserts, badlands, savannas, plains), owned by nobody until the first worthy player lifts it. See [Thor](Thor).

### Stormbreaker

```
N N
N S
. L

N = Netherite Ingot
S = Nether Star
L = any log
```

An axe that hits harder than any other (12 attack damage: a fifth more than Mjolnir), never breaks, can't burn. Anyone can wield it, and whoever holds it can call the Bifrost. See [Thor](Thor), where Mjolnir and Stormbreaker are compared.

### Radioactive Spider

```
F G F
G B G
F G F

F = Fermented Spider Eye
G = Glow Ink Sac
B = Slime Block
```

A small spider in a jar, glowing sickly green (an epic item, up to 16 to a stack). It does nothing until a player with **no power** right-clicks with it: the bite takes **one** spider (not in Creative) and **1 damage**, makes you sick for 7 seconds (the *Radioactive Bite* effect: Nausea, Weakness, Slowness, then Poison, which never kills), and then you awaken as [Spider-Man](Spider-Man). Milk cures the bite. A hero is turned away ("The spider's bite changes nothing: your power does not mix") and keeps it. Besides the recipe, it is found in chests and, very rarely, dropped by a Cave Spider that a player kills. An operator can change how long the bite takes (`spider_man.biteDelayTicks`, 7 seconds by default).

### The Ultron Program

```
I E I
R S R
I E I

I = Block of Iron
E = Echo Shard
R = Block of Redstone
S = Nether Star
```

Stark's peacekeeping program, waiting for a mind: a dark steel data core with a red scanline that runs down it. It does nothing alone. Hold it in one hand and use Loki's Scepter on open, level ground with Sneak and Ultron wakes: see [Ultron](Ultron). It is used up only when the ritual starts, and is given back if the fight is stopped, the server stops or the world goes Peaceful. Fireproof, does not stack. To make one you need a Wither, an Ancient City and thirty-six iron.

### Capstone

```
. S .
G L G
G G G

S = Nether Star
G = Gold Block
L = Lapis Block
```

"The golden tip of the pyramid. The sun feeds it." A golden pyramid tip with a sapphire pulse running down its edge. **Sneak + right-click on sand (or sandstone) by day, under the open sky, with a Scarlet Witch or a Phoenix within 64 blocks**, and En Sabah Nur wakes: see [Apocalypse](Apocalypse#waking-him). It is used up only when the ritual starts, and is given back if the fight is stopped, the server stops, the world goes Peaceful or he withdraws because nobody could break his shield (not after a wipe, an abandoned fight or the time limit). Fireproof, does not stack. To make one you need a Wither and forty-five gold ingots.

### Book of the Vishanti

```
A E A
G B G
A L A

A = Amethyst Shard
E = Eye of Ender
G = Gold Ingot
B = Book
L = Lapis Lazuli
```

"The old book of the sorcerers: the spells of Oshtur, Hoggoth and Agamotto." Only a **sorcerer** (Loki, the Scarlet Witch, Billy or Agatha) can read it. **Use** opens the book (save places and teleport to them, in any world); **Sneak + use** goes to your base; **Save as base** raises a **ward** round it that keeps out every monster and boss, what they shoot, and dark magic, as big as the sorcerer is strong. An epic item, fireproof, does not stack. See [The Sorcerers' Books](Sorcerers-Books#the-book-of-the-vishanti).

### Darkhold

```
N R N
E B E
N S N

N = Netherite Scrap
R = Wither Rose
E = Echo Shard
B = Book
S = Wither Skeleton Skull
```

"Agatha's book of dark magic: it corrupts whoever reads it." Held by a **sorcerer**, it gives Agatha's **purple beam** (hold use), a **revenge** (whoever uses magic on you is poisoned for 10 seconds), a **hex mark** and a **mind glimpse** (Sneak + use). **A player with no power who holds use on it for 3 seconds becomes [Agatha](Agatha)** (3.3), and keeps the book. It is rare on purpose: the rose needs a Wither, the skull a Nether Fortress, the echo shards an Ancient City. An epic item, fireproof, does not stack. See [The Sorcerers' Books](Sorcerers-Books#the-darkhold).

```

"Polished steel and black leather. It waits for a great mind." ### Infinity Gauntlet (3.6)

```
G N G
G C G
G G G

G = Block of Gold
N = Netherite Ingot
C = Nether Star
```

The **crafted** Infinity Gauntlet has **six empty sockets**: right-click [Infinity Stones](Infinity-Stones#the-infinity-gauntlet) onto it in your inventory, choose one with **J** and use it. Only a god, the Scarlet Witch, the Phoenix, Thanos or the Hulk can wield it with stones in it; with all six, it **Snaps**. A server can switch the recipe off (`gauntlet.craftable`). It is not **Thanos's Gauntlet**, the Mad Titan's reward (below), which still makes its holder Thanos.

### Eye of Agamotto (3.6)

```
G G G
G T G
G G G

G = Block of Gold
T = Time Stone
```

"The Time Stone's vessel. Only a sorcerer can open it." A gold amulet with two closed lids; it opens, green, when it is used. A player with **no power** who **holds right-click on it for three seconds** becomes [Doctor Strange](Doctor-Strange#becoming-doctor-strange-the-eye-of-agamotto), and keeps the Eye (it is bound to him: "The Eye of Steve"). A **sorcerer** uses it for the Time Stone's **Slow Time** (use) and **Rewind** (Sneak + use). Anyone may carry it: it never burns. Crafted alone, it gives the Time Stone back (the gold is lost). Does not stack, fireproof.

### Sling Ring (3.6)

```
. G .
G A G
. G .

G = Gold Ingot
A = Amethyst Shard
```

"A sorcerer's ring: portals to saved places." Only a **sorcerer** (Doctor Strange, Billy, Agatha, Loki, the Scarlet Witch, a custom sorcerer) can use it: **Sneak + use** remembers the place you stand on (up to 8), **use** opens its screen, and a place you choose opens a pair of portals to it, in any world but Asgard. For anyone else, "The ring is just a ring for you." Also found in the Sanctum's library chests (30%). See [Doctor Strange](Doctor-Strange#the-sling-ring).

### Gamma Emitter (3.6)

```
I G I
G E G
I R I

I = Block of Iron
G = Lime Stained Glass
E = Block of Emerald
R = Block of Redstone
```

A lab block, not an item you hold. Place it, then right-click it or give it a redstone signal: it warms up for 3 seconds, shines a **green gamma beam** 6 blocks long out of its face for 3 seconds, and rests for 10. A player with **no power** who stands in the whole beam becomes [the Hulk](Hulk#becoming-the-hulk-the-gamma-emitter); a Hulk in it is healed. It drops itself when broken. A server can switch the recipe off (`gamma.emitterCraftable`).

**The Gamma Inhibitor** (3.6) is Bruce Banner's wrist band: while it is in his inventory, his anger never turns him into the Hulk. It has no recipe and is never found: it is in the creative tab.

### Mutant Cure (3.3.1)

Shapeless: the four in any order.

```
Glass Bottle + Milk Bucket + Echo Shard + Iron Nugget
```

"An injector of the cure. There is no going back." **Hold right-click for 2 seconds** and your power is gone for good, exactly as an operator's `/enhanced power set <player> none` (your items, Mjolnir too, stay items). On **another player**: right-click him and hold, while he stays near: an operator always, anyone else only while PvP is on (`mutant_cure.onOthers`). Someone with no power is turned away and nothing is used. Also found: the **Cure Station** of the [X-Men lower lab](Mutant-School-Lab#the-cure-station) fills one for each glass bottle (one a minute per player), the lab's medical chest (30%), and rarely a Hydra outpost's lab chest. It is how a hero clears the way for the [Adamantium Injection](#adamantium-injection-37). Section `[mutant_cure]`.

### Chimichanga (3.7)

Shapeless: the three in any order. It makes **two**.

```
Bread + Cooked Beef (or Cooked Porkchop, or Cooked Chicken) + Carrot
```

"Crunchy, warm, and Deadpool's favourite." Anyone can eat it: 4 drumsticks of food. [Deadpool](Deadpool)'s **Chimichanga Break** (Sneak + B) eats one from his bag for a bigger heal (all his health, and Absorption II for 30 seconds). Also in the kitchen chest of [Agatha's Cottage](Agathas-Cottage) (10%). **Choose a Hero → Deadpool** gives three. The advancement *Maximum Snacks* is for eating one.

### Stone Locator (3.7)

```
N K N
D S D
N D N

N = Block of Netherite
K = Wither Skeleton Skull
D = Block of Diamond
S = an Infinity Stone, or a stone's vessel
```

The middle takes **any Infinity Stone** or a vessel of one (Loki's Scepter, the Tesseract, the Eye of Agamotto, the Orb, a crafted Infinity Gauntlet with a stone; never Thanos's Gauntlet). **The stone comes back**: it stays in the middle slot, exactly as it was. The Locator is tuned to it ("It hums with the Time Stone's energy") and glows in its colour. Hold it: its needle and a line over the hotbar point to the nearest site of a stone you haven't got; use it for a spark trail, Sneak + use to choose the stone. Anyone may use it. Epic, doesn't stack, fireproof. See [Stone Locator](Stone-Locator). A server can switch the recipe off (`stone_locator.craftable`).

### Loki's Scepter

The Mind Stone sits in the blue gem of its golden blade. **Since 3.6** it is the Mind Stone's vessel: **Sneak + hold right-click** with nothing in your other hand fires the **Mind Beam**, whoever you are; **crafted alone**, it gives the [Mind Stone](Infinity-Stones) and an Empty Scepter; and a hit on a [Hydra soldier](Hydra-Soldiers) turns him to your side. Touching it with the gene in your other hand makes you the Scarlet Witch, with a Synthezoid Core makes you Vision, with Ultron's Core makes you [Ultron](Ultron), and with none of them **kills you** (with the [Ultron Program](#the-ultron-program) in the other hand, Sneak + right-click on the ground wakes the Ultron boss, and with the Tesseract it calls [Thanos](Thanos)). It is also a strong melee weapon (8 attack damage, never breaks), and a hit confuses: a creature forgets its target and stumbles about for 6 seconds, a player gets Nausea and Slowness (see [Scarlet Witch](Scarlet-Witch)). The stone has a voice of its own, a bright, shimmering, slightly eerie chime-hum: it sings when the stone is touched, softly under the chime of a confusing hit, and hums quietly for as long as the scepter is in someone's hand.

**3.3: it is kept in the vault of a [Hydra outpost](Structures#a-hydra-outpost)**, and nowhere else: the chest on the pedestal behind the iron bars always holds exactly one. (It is no longer in End City, stronghold, bastion, Ancient City or woodland mansion chests.) `/locate structure enhanced:hydra_outpost` finds the nearest outpost.

### Tesseract

The Space Stone in its cube. **Since 3.6** it is the Space Stone's vessel: **Sneak + right-click** opens the **Realm Picker** for anyone (the Overworld, the Nether, the End, Asgard); **crafted alone**, it gives the [Space Stone](Infinity-Stones) and an Empty Tesseract. One always waits on the roof of every [Avengers Tower](Structures#avengers-tower), in its cradle. Using it as a player with no power makes you [Loki](Loki), far away, unless you hold a Scarlet Witch Gene in your other hand: then you awaken as the [Scarlet Witch](Scarlet-Witch) instead. A hero who uses it jumps to the block they look at. Held together with Loki's Scepter, right-click held for five seconds, it calls [Thanos](Thanos) instead. It is never used up. The Space Stone has a voice of its own, a deep, swelling cosmic hum: it swells under the sound of the portal, and hums softly for as long as the cube is in someone's hand.

### Phoenix Ember

A spark of the cosmic fire that remembers a host, very rare. They awaken as the [Phoenix](Phoenix), Jean Grey. A hero is turned away ("Your body already holds a power") and keeps the Ember. It doesn't stack, can't burn, and an operator can change how long the ritual takes (`phoenix.emberRitualTicks`, 3 seconds by default). The Phoenix's gathering roar plays while it is held, and the Burst's at the end, softly.

### Ultron's Core

The heart Wanda tore out, in a cage of three gunmetal bars: a glowing red-orange sphere that pulses. It is the reward of the Ultron fight: every fighter who was there for a minute and hurt him or his Sentries gets one, and so does whoever lands the last blow; it is never crafted and never found in a chest. On its own it does nothing. A player with no power who holds it in one hand and uses Loki's Scepter with the other becomes [Ultron](Ultron) (the Core is used up). Fireproof, does not stack.

### Essence of En Sabah Nur

"A sapphire soul in gold veins. It looks for a body." A pale-blue wisp swirling in a gold-veined crystal. It is the reward of the Apocalypse fight: every fighter who was there for a minute and hurt him **or his shield** gets one, and so does whoever lands the last blow; it is never crafted and never found in a chest. A player with **no power** who **holds right-click for three seconds** with it is moved into the Essence and becomes [Apocalypse](Apocalypse#becoming-apocalypse-the-hero) (the Essence is used up; let go early and nothing is). Someone who already has a power is told "Your body already holds a power", and nothing is used up: they can keep it or give it to a friend. Fireproof, does not stack.

### Hela's Headdress

"Hold it with no power to become Hela." The antlered headdress of the Goddess of Death, dark green and shining. It is the reward of the [Hela](Hela#rewards) fight; it is never crafted and never found in a chest. A player with **no power** who **holds right-click for three seconds** with it becomes [Hela](Hela#becoming-hela-the-hero) (the Headdress is used up; let go early and nothing is). A Headdress of the reward remembers whom it was given to, and gives him the right to take [Gungnir](#gungnir) when it reaches him. Fireproof, does not stack, and never lost in Asgard.

## The Infinity Stones and their vessels (3.6)

![The Space Stone in the inventory](images/113-ultron-inventory-space-stone.png)

The six [Infinity Stones](Infinity-Stones) are items: the **Space**, **Mind**, **Reality**, **Power**, **Time** and **Soul Stones**. Epic, they don't stack, never burn, never despawn and can't be blown up. Only a god, the Scarlet Witch or the Phoenix may hold a bare stone (a sorcerer the Time Stone, Vision the Mind Stone); in anyone else's inventory it burns after 10 seconds and jumps out.

| Item | Where it comes from |
|---|---|
| **Reality Stone** | Its pedestal in Odin's vault in [Asgard](Asgard) (after Hela's first defeat) |
| **Mind Stone** | Craft **Loki's Scepter** alone: the Mind Stone and an **Empty Scepter** |
| **Space Stone** | Craft the **Tesseract** alone: the Space Stone and an **Empty Tesseract** |

## Hydra's gear (3.6)

* **Hydra Blaster**: "Fires blue energy bolts." Anyone can fire it: right-click, a bolt of 5, once a second, 96 shots. An energy cell repairs it in an anvil. Dropped by a [Hydra officer](Hydra-Soldiers#drops-and-loot) (10%) and found in a Hydra outpost's supply crates (5%).

## The items of Asgard (3.5)

![Loki in Asgard](images/023-loki-asgard.png)

None of these can be crafted. [Asgard](Asgard) keeps all three weapons for you if you die there.

### Hofund

Heimdall's sword, a long golden broadsword: 10 attack damage, sweeps, never breaks, fireproof. **Whoever holds a Hofund, anyone's, can open the Bifrost** (right-click: the Bifrost screen; Sneak + right-click: to where you look), unless the server turns that off (`asgard.hofundOpensBifrost`). It is won in [Heimdall's](Heimdall#becoming-heimdall-the-guardians-watch) **Guardian's Watch**, bound to the winner ("Bound to" on the tooltip), and with no power **your own Hofund in your hand makes you Heimdall**. A lost one is replaced (Heimdall calls it back; the old copy crumbles to golden dust).

### Gungnir

The spear of the Allfather: 9 attack damage, a block more reach, never breaks, fireproof. Anyone can fight with it, but **only its owner opens the Bifrost** with it (right-click; Sneak: to where you look). Each player who beat [Hela](Hela) takes **his own** from Gungnir's Stand in Odin's vault; with no power, **sitting on the Throne of Asgard with your own Gungnir in your hand makes you [Odin](Odin)**. A lost one is replaced at the stand (or by Odin's R); the old copy crumbles.

### Sif's Token, Asgardian Mead and Valkyrie's horse

* **Sif's Token**: a shining gold nugget, the prize for beating Sif in her training duel ([Asgardians](Asgardians#training-with-sif)).
* **Asgardian Mead**: honey, from Volstagg's trades.
* **Valkyrie's Horse**: a lead from Valkyrie's trades; a tamed, saddled horse waits beside her.

### The blocks of Asgard

Asgardian Gold Plating, Gold Trim, Gold Pillar and Gold Lattice; Realm Stone, Realm Stone Bricks (with stairs, slab and wall), Polished Realm Stone and Realm Stone Pillar; the Rainbow Bridge; the Cosmic Falls; the Mural of Asgard; the Asgardian Brazier; Golden Leaves of Asgard; the Asgardian Bench. They are in the Creative tab, with no recipe. In Asgard's wild land the stone and the leaves can be mined like any block; the city itself can't be broken ([Asgard](Asgard#the-rules-of-asgard)). The Throne of Asgard, Hela's Seal, Gungnir's Stand, Hofund's Socket, the Bifrost Mechanism and the Bifrost Rune Stone are the city's own: they can't be broken and have no item.

## Hero Creator

"Become any hero, or make your own." A gold medallion, only in the **creative tab**: it has no recipe and is never found. It works for a player in Creative mode or an operator. Use it to open the Hero Creator: become any hero of the mod in any look, or make a hero of your own with powers from a catalogue of thirty and (since 3.4) the abilities of the heroes (150 since 3.8) and the six Infinity Stones, your keys, your moves, your colours, your skin and its extras, and save it to a file. Since 3.4, choosing a hero also gives you his items: Magneto's Helmet, Quicksilver's Goggles, the Ruby-Quartz Glasses, Mjolnir (claimed by you) or Stormbreaker, Loki's Scepter, Thanos's Gauntlet, Ultron's Core, the wheelchair, the Darkhold, or (3.6) Doctor Strange's Eye of Agamotto and Sling Ring, or (3.7) Deadpool's three chimichangas, or (3.8) Captain America's Vibranium Shield, Iron Man's Arc Reactor and Mark 3, Falcon's Exo-Wings (on his chest if it is free) or Love's Stormbreaker, never a second one (`custom_hero.chooseGivesItems`). See [Hero Creator](Hero-Creator).

## Spider Web (a block, not an item)

The webs that [Spider-Man](Spider-Man) shoots leave temporary **Spider Web** blocks. You can't craft, find or pick one up. It is like a cobweb, but it holds everyone **except Spider-Man**, it **vanishes by itself after 10 seconds** (15 for organic webs), and a hand or a sword clears it at once. It only appears in free air, never where spawn protection or a claim says no, and never inside a boss arena. An operator can turn the blocks off (`spider_man.webBlocks`) or change how long they last (`spider_man.webBlockTicks`).

## Changing the loot

The chances above are NeoForge *global loot modifiers* in the mod's data (`data/enhanced/loot_modifiers/`), so a datapack can change them. The chests of the [structures](Structures) of 3.3 are ordinary loot tables (`data/enhanced/loot_table/chests/hydra_outpost/` and `chests/mutant_school/`), and so are those of 3.6 (`chests/sanctum/`, `chests/avengers_tower/`, `chests/end_tunnel/`, `chests/vormir/`) and what the Hydra soldiers drop (`entities/hydra_soldier`, `hydra_soldier_enforcer`, `hydra_soldier_sniper`, `hydra_officer`), and those of 3.7 (`chests/school_lab/medical`, `lockers`, `weapon_x`; `chests/agatha_cottage/kitchen`, `cellar`, `nicky`, `study`). The recipes are ordinary recipe files (`data/enhanced/recipe/`).
