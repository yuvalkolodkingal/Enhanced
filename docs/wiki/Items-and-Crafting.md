# Items and Crafting

Every item you can use is in the **Enhanced** creative tab. Thirteen are crafted, five more are found in chests (or dropped), three more are the rewards of the bosses, and one, the [Hero Creator](#hero-creator), is for Creative mode only. The Radioactive Spider and Professor X's Wheelchair are both: you can craft them and find them. In the recipe grids below, `.` is an empty slot, and the recipes are **shaped**: the items must sit exactly as drawn.

### Magneto's Helmet

```
N N N
N . N

N = Block of Netherite
```

Worn, it makes you [Magneto](Magneto). It protects like a full Netherite set (20 armor, 12 toughness, 40% knockback resistance), blocks Nausea, and never breaks.

### Wanda's Crown

```
G R G
G . G

G = Gold Ingot
R = Block of Redstone
```

Given to the Scarlet Witch when she awakens, and craftable if lost. On her: 20 hearts and Diamond-level protection. It never breaks. On anyone else it is just a crown.

### Quicksilver's Goggles

```
S I S
G D G

S = Sugar
I = Iron Ingot
G = Glass Pane
D = Block of Diamond
```

Worn, they make you [Quicksilver](Quicksilver). They protect like an iron helmet and never break.

### Synthezoid Core

```
R N R
N S N
R N R

R = Block of Redstone
N = Netherite Ingot
S = Nether Star
```

A synthetic body waiting for a mind. Hold it in one hand and use Loki's Scepter with the other, and you become [Vision](Vision).

### Witches' Road Door

```
A L A
T E T
A L A

A = Amethyst Shard
L = Soul Lantern
T = Dark Oak Trapdoor
E = Eye of Ender
```

A door that is placed flat in the floor. Open it holding a Scarlet Witch Gene, with no power, to walk the three trials of [The Witches' Road](Witches-Road) and become Billy or Tommy: see [Billy and the Witches' Road](Billy-and-the-Witches-Road). An axe breaks it fastest, pistons can't push it, and breaking it drops it again.

### Mjolnir

```
N S N
N H N
. R .

N = Netherite Ingot
S = Nether Star
H = Heavy Core
R = Breeze Rod
```

Only the worthy can lift it, and it is bound to the first worthy player who does (its tooltip says "Unclaimed" until then, and "Bound to" and the owner's name after). 10 attack damage (slow, like an axe), never breaks, fire and lava can't hurt it. In Thor's hands it shatters into fragments that strike his enemies (Sneak + R), and its owner can pin someone down with it (Sneak + right-click on a creature or player). **3.3: it is also found** lying in the middle of a [Mjolnir's crater](Structures#mjolnirs-crater) (deserts, badlands, savannas, plains), owned by nobody until the first worthy player lifts it. See [Thor](Thor).

### Stormbreaker

```
N N
N S
. L

N = Netherite Ingot
S = Nether Star
L = any log
```

An axe that hits harder than any other (12 attack damage: a fifth more than Mjolnir), never breaks, can't burn. Anyone can wield it, and whoever holds it can call the Bifrost. See [Thor](Thor), where Mjolnir and Stormbreaker are compared.

### Radioactive Spider

```
F G F
G B G
F G F

F = Fermented Spider Eye
G = Glow Ink Sac
B = Slime Block
```

A small spider in a jar, glowing sickly green (an epic item, up to 16 to a stack). It does nothing until a player with **no power** right-clicks with it: the bite takes **one** spider (not in Creative) and **1 damage**, makes you sick for 7 seconds (the *Radioactive Bite* effect: Nausea, Weakness, Slowness, then Poison, which never kills), and then you awaken as [Spider-Man](Spider-Man). Milk cures the bite. A hero is turned away ("The spider's bite changes nothing: your power does not mix") and keeps it. Besides the recipe, it is found in chests and, very rarely, dropped by a Cave Spider that a player kills. An operator can change how long the bite takes (`spider_man.biteDelayTicks`, 7 seconds by default).

### The Ultron Program

```
I E I
R S R
I E I

I = Block of Iron
E = Echo Shard
R = Block of Redstone
S = Nether Star
```

Stark's peacekeeping program, waiting for a mind: a dark steel data core with a red scanline that runs down it. It does nothing alone. Hold it in one hand and use Loki's Scepter on open, level ground with Sneak and Ultron wakes: see [Ultron](Ultron). It is used up only when the ritual starts, and is given back if the fight is stopped, the server stops or the world goes Peaceful. Fireproof, does not stack. To make one you need a Wither, an Ancient City and thirty-six iron.

### Capstone

```
. S .
G L G
G G G

S = Nether Star
G = Gold Block
L = Lapis Block
```

"The golden tip of the pyramid. The sun feeds it." A golden pyramid tip with a sapphire pulse running down its edge. **Sneak + right-click on sand (or sandstone) by day, under the open sky, with a Scarlet Witch or a Phoenix within 64 blocks**, and En Sabah Nur wakes: see [Apocalypse](Apocalypse#waking-him). It is used up only when the ritual starts, and is given back if the fight is stopped, the server stops, the world goes Peaceful or he withdraws because nobody could break his shield (not after a wipe, an abandoned fight or the time limit). Fireproof, does not stack. To make one you need a Wither and forty-five gold ingots.

### Book of the Vishanti

```
A E A
G B G
A L A

A = Amethyst Shard
E = Eye of Ender
G = Gold Ingot
B = Book
L = Lapis Lazuli
```

"The old book of the sorcerers: the spells of Oshtur, Hoggoth and Agamotto." Only a **sorcerer** (Loki, the Scarlet Witch, Billy or Agatha) can read it. **Use** opens the book (save places and teleport to them, in any world); **Sneak + use** goes to your base; **Save as base** raises a **ward** round it that keeps out every monster and boss, what they shoot, and dark magic, as big as the sorcerer is strong. An epic item, fireproof, does not stack. See [The Sorcerers' Books](Sorcerers-Books#the-book-of-the-vishanti).

### Darkhold

```
N R N
E B E
N S N

N = Netherite Scrap
R = Wither Rose
E = Echo Shard
B = Book
S = Wither Skeleton Skull
```

"Agatha's book of dark magic: it corrupts whoever reads it." Held by a **sorcerer**, it gives Agatha's **purple beam** (hold use), a **revenge** (whoever uses magic on you is poisoned for 10 seconds), a **hex mark** and a **mind glimpse** (Sneak + use). **A player with no power who holds use on it for 3 seconds becomes [Agatha](Agatha)** (3.3), and keeps the book. It is rare on purpose: the rose needs a Wither, the skull a Nether Fortress, the echo shards an Ancient City. An epic item, fireproof, does not stack. See [The Sorcerers' Books](Sorcerers-Books#the-darkhold).

### Professor X's Wheelchair

```
L . .
L L E
I . I

L = Leather
E = Eye of Ender
I = Iron Ingot
```

"Polished steel and black leather. It waits for a great mind." A player with **no power** who **holds right-click on it for three seconds** becomes [Professor X](Professor-X) and chooses the young or the old Charles (the chair is used up, not in Creative; a hero is turned away and keeps it). It is also found, always one, in the Professor's study of the [Mutant School](Structures#the-mutant-school). Does not stack.

## Items found in the world

These five are never crafted. Each chance is **per chest generated** (or per kill, for the mobs): a chest of that kind has that chance to hold the item.

### Scarlet Witch Gene

Very rare. On its own it does nothing at all: it is held while touching the Mind Stone or using the Tesseract ([Scarlet Witch](Scarlet-Witch)) or while opening the Witches' Road Door ([Billy and the Witches' Road](Billy-and-the-Witches-Road)).

### Loki's Scepter

The Mind Stone sits in the blue gem of its golden blade. Touching it with the gene in your other hand makes you the Scarlet Witch, with a Synthezoid Core makes you Vision, with Ultron's Core makes you [Ultron](Ultron), and with none of them **kills you** (with the [Ultron Program](#the-ultron-program) in the other hand, Sneak + right-click on the ground wakes the Ultron boss, and with the Tesseract it calls [Thanos](Thanos)). It is also a strong melee weapon (8 attack damage, never breaks), and a hit confuses: a creature forgets its target and stumbles about for 6 seconds, a player gets Nausea and Slowness (see [Scarlet Witch](Scarlet-Witch)). The stone has a voice of its own, a bright, shimmering, slightly eerie chime-hum: it sings when the stone is touched, softly under the chime of a confusing hit, and hums quietly for as long as the scepter is in someone's hand.

**3.3: it is kept in the vault of a [Hydra outpost](Structures#a-hydra-outpost)**, and nowhere else: the chest on the pedestal behind the iron bars always holds exactly one. (It is no longer in End City, stronghold, bastion, Ancient City or woodland mansion chests.) `/locate structure enhanced:hydra_outpost` finds the nearest outpost.

### Tesseract

The Space Stone in its cube. Using it as a player with no power makes you [Loki](Loki), far away, unless you hold a Scarlet Witch Gene in your other hand: then you awaken as the [Scarlet Witch](Scarlet-Witch) instead. A hero who uses it jumps to the block they look at. Held together with Loki's Scepter, right-click held for five seconds, it calls [Thanos](Thanos) instead. It is never used up. The Space Stone has a voice of its own, a deep, swelling cosmic hum: it swells under the sound of the portal, and hums softly for as long as the cube is in someone's hand.

| Stronghold library chest | 6% |

### Phoenix Ember

A spark of the cosmic fire that remembers a host, very rare. It has no recipe and does nothing on its own, until a player with **no power** **holds right-click on it for 3 seconds**: flames spiral up around them, and the Ember burns into them and is used up (not in Creative). They awaken as the [Phoenix](Phoenix), Jean Grey. A hero is turned away ("Your body already holds a power") and keeps the Ember. It doesn't stack, can't burn, and an operator can change how long the ritual takes (`phoenix.emberRitualTicks`, 3 seconds by default). The Phoenix's gathering roar plays while it is held, and the Burst's at the end, softly.

### Ruby-Quartz Glasses

"The only thing that holds back his eyes." Scott Summers' glasses: worn in the head slot by a player with **no power**, they make him [Cyclops](Cyclops); on any other hero they are only glasses. 1 point of armour, never break, fireproof, do not stack. Taken off by Cyclops, they let loose his giant beam until he puts them back on.

| Source | Chance |
|---|---|

| Stronghold library chest | 2% |

The **Radioactive Spider** ([above](#radioactive-spider)) is found too, in five places listed with its recipe, and **Professor X's Wheelchair** ([above](#professor-xs-wheelchair)) in the Mutant School.

## Rewards of the bosses

These three are never crafted and never found in a chest: each is the reward of a boss fight, and the only way in to its hero.

| Item | Becomes | Where it comes from |
|---|---|---|
| [Infinity Gauntlet](Thanos#the-infinity-gauntlet) | [Thanos](Thanos) (hold right-click for three seconds with no power) | The reward for beating the Mad Titan: one for each fighter who did 3 percent of his health, and one for whoever struck the last blow. Held with no power it lights its five stones in turn and makes you [Thanos](Thanos#playing-thanos); it can be given to a friend |
| [Ultron's Core](#ultrons-core) | [Ultron](Ultron) (with Loki's Scepter, with no power) | The reward for beating Ultron: one for each fighter who was there for a minute and hurt him or his Sentries, and one for whoever struck the last blow |
| [Essence of En Sabah Nur](#essence-of-en-sabah-nur) | [Apocalypse](Apocalypse#becoming-apocalypse-the-hero) (hold right-click for three seconds with no power) | The reward for beating Apocalypse: one for each fighter who was there for a minute and hurt him or his shield, and one for whoever struck the last blow |

### Ultron's Core

The heart Wanda tore out, in a cage of three gunmetal bars: a glowing red-orange sphere that pulses. It is the reward of the Ultron fight: every fighter who was there for a minute and hurt him or his Sentries gets one, and so does whoever lands the last blow; it is never crafted and never found in a chest. On its own it does nothing. A player with no power who holds it in one hand and uses Loki's Scepter with the other becomes [Ultron](Ultron) (the Core is used up). Fireproof, does not stack.

### Essence of En Sabah Nur

"A sapphire soul in gold veins. It looks for a body." A pale-blue wisp swirling in a gold-veined crystal. It is the reward of the Apocalypse fight: every fighter who was there for a minute and hurt him **or his shield** gets one, and so does whoever lands the last blow; it is never crafted and never found in a chest. A player with **no power** who **holds right-click for three seconds** with it is moved into the Essence and becomes [Apocalypse](Apocalypse#becoming-apocalypse-the-hero) (the Essence is used up; let go early and nothing is). Someone who already has a power is told "Your body already holds a power", and nothing is used up: they can keep it or give it to a friend. Fireproof, does not stack.

## Hero Creator

"Become any hero, or make your own." A gold medallion, only in the **creative tab**: it has no recipe and is never found. It works for a player in Creative mode or an operator. Use it to open the Hero Creator: become any hero of the mod in any look, or make a hero of your own with powers from a catalogue of thirty and (since 3.4) the 81 abilities of the heroes, your keys, your moves, your colours, your skin and its extras, and save it to a file. Since 3.4, choosing a hero also gives you his items: Magneto's Helmet, Quicksilver's Goggles, the Ruby-Quartz Glasses, Mjolnir (claimed by you) or Stormbreaker, Loki's Scepter, the Infinity Gauntlet, Ultron's Core, the wheelchair or the Darkhold, never a second one (`custom_hero.chooseGivesItems`). See [Hero Creator](Hero-Creator).

## Spider Web (a block, not an item)

The webs that [Spider-Man](Spider-Man) shoots leave temporary **Spider Web** blocks. You can't craft, find or pick one up. It is like a cobweb, but it holds everyone **except Spider-Man**, it **vanishes by itself after 10 seconds** (15 for organic webs), and a hand or a sword clears it at once. It only appears in free air, never where spawn protection or a claim says no, and never inside a boss arena. An operator can turn the blocks off (`spider_man.webBlocks`) or change how long they last (`spider_man.webBlockTicks`).

## Changing the loot

The chances above are NeoForge *global loot modifiers* in the mod's data (`data/enhanced/loot_modifiers/`), so a datapack can change them. The chests of the [structures](Structures) of 3.3 are ordinary loot tables (`data/enhanced/loot_table/chests/hydra_outpost/` and `chests/mutant_school/`). The recipes are ordinary recipe files (`data/enhanced/recipe/`).
