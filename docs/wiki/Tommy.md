# Tommy

Tommy Maximoff (Speed), Wanda's speedster son from *WandaVision*. His speed showed up on Halloween night ("Chip off the old Maximoff block! You've got super speed!"), and in the Battle of Westview he crossed the street as a teal-green ghost and disarmed a whole S.W.O.R.D. squad.

## Becoming Tommy

Through the Witches' Road, with a Scarlet Witch Gene: craft and place a **Witches' Road Door**, open it holding the gene, walk the three trials of [The Witches' Road](Witches-Road), and choose **Tommy: Speed** at the end of the Road. The whole ritual is on [Billy and the Witches' Road](Billy-and-the-Witches-Road), and the first steps are on [Becoming a Hero](Becoming-a-Hero).

## Super speed

Tommy's speed doesn't slow the world down (that is [Quicksilver's](Quicksilver) trick). Instead he leaves a translucent teal-green ghost trail and afterimages, low along the ground, like in the Battle of Westview.

## Abilities

| # | Key | Ability | How it works |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **R** (toggle) | Super Speed | On from the start: sprinting is super speed, three and a half times as fast, stepping up whole blocks, with no fall damage while running. It burns hunger slowly; with an empty hunger bar he only runs normally. Turn it off to sprint normally. |
| 2 | **G** | Disarm Blitz | In a blink he circles every armed creature within 12 blocks (up to 8 of them) and is back before they notice, their weapons in his arms: creatures lose whatever they hold, players only their weapon (swords, axes, bows, crossbows, tridents, maces, Stormbreaker). Everyone disarmed is slowed for 2 seconds. Mjolnir is far too heavy to snatch. Cooldown 8 seconds. |
| 3 | **V** | Whirlwind | For 3 seconds he runs circles around himself: everything within 8 blocks (creatures, players, arrows in flight) is pulled onto a spinning ring, lifted up to 3 blocks, and flung away when the whirlwind ends. Ghosts of Tommy race round the ring. Cooldown 10 seconds. |
| 4 | **B** | Vibrate Through Walls | He vibrates his molecules and passes straight through the wall in front of him, up to 4 blocks thick (from the *VisionQuest* trailer and the comics). Not through bedrock or other unbreakable blocks, and not through a Hex he isn't allowed into. Cooldown 2 seconds. |
| 5 | **N** | Molecular Acceleration | From the comics: he vibrates whatever he touches (up to 5 blocks away) until it shatters. A block breaks and drops its item (anything up to the hardness of obsidian, never bedrock; it respects spawn protection and claims), and a creature takes 6 damage and is blasted back. Cooldown 1 second. |

Tommy does not fly.

## Looks

*Halloween* (his WandaVision costume, a mini-Quicksilver: silver hair teased up, a blue tee with a silver lightning bolt), *Battle of Westview* (the green track jacket), *Speed* (the comics costume, green and white with amber goggles), and *Your own skin*. Press **H** to switch. See [Looks](Looks).

## Numbers you can change

Tommy's speed bonus (2.5, which is three and a half times as fast) and the hunger cost of running are the `tommy.*` options on [Server Configuration](Server-Configuration).
