# Love

Thor's daughter (3.8), from the end of *Thor: Love and Thunder*: the girl Eternity brought back, who fights beside Thor with Eternity's gifts. In Enhanced you become Love by **[wishing for it](Eternity#become-love)** in Eternity's realm. She throws **Stormbreaker** and it flies back to her hand, flies while she holds it, calls lightning down where she looks, pours Thor's lightning from her hand, opens the **Bifrost**, and fires a **purple laser from her eyes** that burns. She has **16 hearts** and is **worthy of Mjolnir**.

Hebrew: [עברית](Love-HE)

![Love at the Shrine of Eternity](images/101-love-eternity-shrine.png)

## The quick version

* **How:** reach [Eternity](Eternity) (a key of the Bifrost on the altar of the Shrine of the Cosmic Entities, in the End) and choose **Become Love**. You get a Stormbreaker too.
* **R** Axe Throw, **G** Flight, **V** Lightning (hold), **B** Eye Laser (hold), **N** Bifrost. **Sneak** changes two of them: Thunder Strike, and the Bifrost to where you look.
* **Most of her powers need Stormbreaker in her hand:** the throw, flight and the Bifrost ("Love needs her axe."). Her lightning and her eyes don't.

## Becoming Love

* In [Eternity's realm](Eternity#the-wish), choose **Become Love**: your old hero is replaced, the old way of `/enhanced power set`, and you get a **[Stormbreaker](Thor#stormbreaker)** if you carry none. Everyone is told: "Eilon made a wish to Eternity and became Love, Thor's daughter!" It is your one wish. The advancement **Thunder runs in the family** is for it.
* Or **Choose a Hero** in the [Hero Creator](Hero-Creator) (with a Stormbreaker), or `/enhanced power set <player> love` (and a look: `love` or `own`).

**Losing it:** the Mutant Cure, `/enhanced power set <player> none`, the [Super Soldier Serum](Super-Soldier-Serum), an [Arc Reactor](Iron-Man#becoming-iron-man-the-arc-reactors), being sacrificed at Vormir, or choosing another hero. Stormbreaker stays an item (anyone can wield it).

## The body

* **32 health** (16 hearts), **+4 armour**, every blow **+3**, a little harder to push, and 6 more blocks of fall before it hurts.
* **Worthy of Mjolnir**: she is Thor's daughter. She can lift a Mjolnir nobody owns and claim it (a server can take it away: `love.worthy`). See [Thor](Thor#the-worthy).

## Abilities

![Love at the Shrine, second view](images/102-love-eternity-shrine-2.png)

| # | Key | Ability | How it works |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **R** | Axe Throw | **Only with Stormbreaker in hand.** She throws it as Thor does: it smashes what it meets on its way, turns at a wall or at its range, and **flies back to her hand**. Press **R** again while it flies and it turns home at once; with the axe in her bag, **R** brings it to her hand. Its blow is Stormbreaker's. **Sneak + R: Thunder Strike**: a bolt of lightning comes down on the block she looks at (up to 48 blocks): **8** to every creature within 2 blocks of it. Blocks don't catch fire (unless a server allows it: `love.strikeSetsFire` with `mobGriefing`). Look at the ground or a wall: "Look at the ground or a wall to call the lightning." It has its own cooldown of 5 seconds, so the axe throw stays quick. |
| 2 | **G** | Flight | **While she holds Stormbreaker** (or it is flying back to her), as Thor. Speed 0.10. |
| 3 | **V** (hold) | Lightning | **Thor's lightning beam**, in her lilac and gold, from her hand, or from Stormbreaker's head when she holds it (then it reaches much further and hits harder, as Thor's does). It uses a little food. |
| 4 | **B** (hold) | Eye Laser | A **purple beam from her eyes**, 24 blocks, **through every creature** in its way to the first block: **6** (3 hearts) to each creature it touches, **once a second** each, and sets it **burning for 4 seconds**. Blocks are not set on fire. Her eyes glow violet. Up to 5 seconds, then her eyes rest 3 seconds ("Love's eyes need a rest."). |
| 5 | **N** | Bifrost | **With Stormbreaker in hand**: the Bifrost screen opens, as Thor's: [Asgard](Asgard), home, your saved places. **Sneak + N** (with the axe too): the Bifrost to where she looks. |

With PvP off, her abilities don't hurt players. Everything she pushes at the edge of the [Vormir Cliff](Vormir) counts as her push.

## The HUD

Next to the usual panel: Stormbreaker ("Stormbreaker: in hand", "flying" or "not with you"; "needs the axe" on the keys that need it), the eye laser's seconds, and the cooldowns.

## Against the others

* She is **worthy** and holds Stormbreaker, so she is a **key of the Bifrost** for [Eternity's altar](Eternity#the-way-in-the-altar) too, and the Bifrost takes her to [Asgard](Asgard).
* Her lightning charges an [Iron Man's](Iron-Man) Mark 85 **Lightning Refocuser**.
* Her powers don't keep [Wolverine](Wolverine) or [Deadpool](Deadpool) down: only the Allfather's power does, not his granddaughter's.

## Love in the Hero Creator

Her abilities are in the [Hero Creator](Hero-Creator) (`love.axe`, `love.lightning`, `love.eye_laser`, `love.bifrost`, `love.body`), and her two Sneak variants are cards of their own (`love.thunder_strike`, `love.bifrost_look`). **Daughter of Thor** (her body) gives 16 hearts and makes a custom hero worthy of Mjolnir. A custom hero's Axe Throw takes **Mjolnir or Stormbreaker**, as Thor's hammer card does; his Bifrost needs the axe in hand. See [Heroes' Abilities](Hero-Abilities#love-5). The ready-made hero is *Love-like*.

## Numbers you can change

Every number of her body and her abilities is a `love.*` option. See [Server Configuration](Server-Configuration#eternity-and-love).
