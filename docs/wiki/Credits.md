## Skins, music and libraries

* **Scarlet Witch skin:** the community skin ["Scarlet Witch"](https://www.minecraftskins.com/skin/18465369/scarlet-witch/) from minecraftskins.com.
* ***Sweet Dreams (Are Made of This)*:** Eurythmics. Bundled for personal use only (since 3.5 it plays only if a player chooses it): the recording is copyrighted, so do not share the jar publicly.
* **The Walkman themes** (3.5): Quicksilver's *Silver Hours* and Speed's *Westview Blur*, original synth-pop written for the mod in the spirit of the 80s (the style only; no melody, bass line or chords of any song).
* **The music of the Witches' Road** (3.3): an original ballad written for the mod in the spirit of *Agatha All Along*, synthesised in five arrangements, and a sixth in 3.5 for the choice; not the show's song.
* **The music and sounds of Asgard** (3.5): Asgard's three pieces, the Guardian's Watch, Hela's three-layer battle theme, and every sound of Heimdall, Odin, Hela and the Asgardians, all synthesised for the mod; no film audio.
* **The sounds of 3.6**: the six stones' voices, Doctor Strange's spells, the Sanctum's clocks, the End tunnel's drone, Vormir's wind, the Hulk's roars and the Hydra soldiers' blasters, siren and radio, all synthesised for the mod; no film audio. The looks of the new heroes, the Stonekeeper and the Hydra soldiers are drawn for the mod (the Hydra emblem is our own drawing; Avengers Tower has no logo), and every line they say is our own.
* **The sounds of 3.7**: Wolverine's *snikt* (our own metal scrape) and wordless growls and roar, Deadpool's cartoon pistols, katanas, teleport belt and his own little whistled tune and fanfare, Dogpool's barks, the healing factor's heartbeats and chimes, the lab's lift, doors, pod and machinery, Cerebro's chord, the Locator's pings, and the cottage's birds, kettle, Rio's harp and Señor Scratchy's jingle: 63 sounds, all synthesised for the mod; no film audio or music, no words. Wolverine's and Deadpool's suits, Dogpool, Agatha, Nicky and Rio, the lab, the cottage and the Locator are drawn for the mod (no X-Men, Weapon X or Deadpool logo), and every line Deadpool and the family say is our own.
* **JLayer** (javazoom), the MP3 decoder bundled inside the jar, so Quicksilver's Walkman can play an `.mp3` you put in `config/enhanced/music/`.
* **NeoForge**, the mod loader, and the Mojang and Parchment mappings the mod is built with.
