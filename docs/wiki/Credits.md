## Skins, music and libraries

* **Scarlet Witch skin:** the community skin ["Scarlet Witch"](https://www.minecraftskins.com/skin/18465369/scarlet-witch/) from minecraftskins.com.
* ***Sweet Dreams (Are Made of This)*:** Eurythmics. Bundled for personal use only: the recording is copyrighted, so do not share the jar publicly.
* **JLayer** (javazoom), the MP3 decoder bundled inside the jar, so Quicksilver's Walkman can play an `.mp3` you put in `config/enhanced/music/`.
* **NeoForge**, the mod loader, and the Mojang and Parchment mappings the mod is built with.
