# Enhanced

**Enhanced** is a Minecraft mod (NeoForge, Minecraft 1.21.1) where you can become one of the *Enhanced*: a hero from the Marvel films and shows, with the powers, the look and the colours you know from the screen. You can be **Magneto**, the Master of Magnetism; **Wanda Maximoff, the Scarlet Witch**; **Quicksilver**, who slows the world down around him (to *Sweet Dreams*) or just outruns it; **Vision**, the Mind Stone in a synthetic body (or **White Vision**); Wanda's sons **Tommy (Speed)** and **Billy (Wiccan)**; **Loki**, the God of Mischief; **Jean Grey, the Phoenix**, who reads minds and burns with cosmic fire; **Peter Parker, Spider-Man**, who goes everywhere on a web; or, if you are worthy, **Thor**, with **Mjolnir** and **Stormbreaker**; **Agatha Harkness**, whom the Darkhold makes; **Cyclops**, whose eyes only his ruby-quartz glasses hold back; **Professor X**, who hears every mind and takes over bodies; **Doctor Strange**, the Sorcerer Supreme, whom the Eye of Agamotto makes; **the Hulk**, whom a beam of gamma rays makes; or, from the X-Men's secret lab under the Mutant School, **Wolverine**, with adamantium claws, and **Deadpool**, with two katanas, two pistols and a lot of jokes, who both get back up from anything but the strongest powers. In **Asgard**, the golden realm the Bifrost reaches, you can become **Heimdall**, who sees everything and keeps the Bifrost, the Allfather **Odin**, or **Hela**, the Goddess of Death, once you have beaten her. The six **Infinity Stones** wait in the worlds, for the gods to hold and to set into the Infinity Gauntlet, and the **Stone Locator** points the way to them. In the woods, **Agatha**, her son **Nicky** and **Rio, the Green Witch** live happily in their cottage; and when you die, Rio lets you choose where to start again. From the **Avengers Tower**, the **Super Soldier Serum** makes you **Captain America**, with his Vibranium Shield, **Red Hulk**, who burns hotter the angrier he gets, or **Falcon**, with his Exo-Wings and his drone Redwing; and an **Arc Reactor** makes you **Iron Man**, with six suits to put on, from the cave's Mark 1 to the nanotech Mark 85 and the giant Hulkbuster. Far out in the End, the **Shrine of the Cosmic Entities** leads to **Eternity**, who grants one wish: anything in the world, or to become **Love**, Thor's daughter. In Creative mode the **Hero Creator** makes any hero, or a hero of your own. And three villains are bosses whose powers are won only by defeating them: **Thanos**, the Mad Titan, who gathers the Infinity Stones as he fights; **Ultron**, the AI of the Mind Stone, who lifts the ground into the sky; and En Sabah Nur, the first mutant, **Apocalypse**, whose golden shield only mind power breaks.

Original hero concepts by Eilon Kolodkin-Gal. This wiki is the player's guide. The full design, with every rule and number, is in [docs/DESIGN.md](https://github.com/yuvalkolodkingal/enhanced/blob/main/docs/DESIGN.md).

## What's new in 3.8.3

Eilon asked: "Redesign the drones to look like that" (the Mark 85's wings in *Endgame*).

* **[The Mark 85's Nanotech Drones](Iron-Man#mark-85-the-strongest-suit) are the *Endgame* wings now** (Sneak + N): **four big curved blades**, like crescent moons, grow out of Iron Man's arms nanite by nanite and fan out beside him, **two on each side**: dark-red metal and gold around a bright **cyan** glowing panel full of circuit lines, with a little thruster pod on the back. The inner blade floats just past his hand, the outer one further out and a little lower; they bob and sway, turn with him and stay with him in flight. Their **cyan lasers** leave from the inner edge of a blade. In first person they stay at the edges of the screen; when their time is up they fold back into his arms. Same damage, time, energy and cooldown as before.
* Keep the server and the players on the same version (network protocol **13**, unchanged: 3.8.1, 3.8.2 and 3.8.3 play together).

## What's new in 3.8.2

Eilon's last two fixes: "Wanda's shield doesn't protect against Iron Man's laser beam: fix it so it doesn't go through the shield", and "Death, in her suit, has flowers on her, and in the series she had no flowers".

* **[Wanda's Chaos Shield stops every beam of Iron Man](Scarlet-Witch#4-chaos-shield)**, as it stops Thor's lightning beam: the repulsors of every suit (and the Double-Hand Blast and the Mark 50's nano cannon), the Unibeam, the Mark 85's giant chest laser, the Hulkbuster's heavy Unibeam, the lasers of the Laser Burst and of the drones, and the Lightning Refocuser's chain lightning. The beam ends on the shield's surface with a flash, Wanda isn't touched, nothing behind or inside the shield is hit, and the hit costs the shield what it would have done (with the old shield rules). Fired from inside the shield, his beams still never touch her. **Redwing's** laser and **Love's** eye laser stop there too.
* **[Death](Meeting-Death) wears no flowers.** Her crown of branches had pale tips that looked like little white blossoms: now it is dark dead twigs with a few dead leaves, and her gown has no pale specks. The clearing still has its little flowers and falling petals, but never on her: the grass at her feet has none, and the petals fade away before they reach her. At home in the cottage Rio is still the gardener she was.
* Keep the server and the players on the same version (network protocol **13**, unchanged: 3.8.1 and 3.8.2 play together).

* **Only the Bifrost goes there.** Choose **Asgard** at the top of the Bifrost screen. The Bifrost answers Mjolnir, Stormbreaker, **Hofund** (anyone's), your own **Gungnir**, and Heimdall and Odin. With no power, right-click a **Bifrost Rune Stone** in the Overworld.
* **The Bifrost takes everyone in its circle**, Thor's too: friends, pets, horses. Step out to stay behind.
* **Three new heroes:** [Heimdall](Heimdall) (win his Guardian's Watch), [Hela](Hela) (a boss: beat her in the throne room and wear her Headdress) and [Odin](Odin) (take Gungnir from his vault and sit on his throne).
* **[The Asgardians](Asgardians):** Heimdall, Odin, Frigga, Sif, Valkyrie, the Warriors Three, the Einherjar and the citizens live in the city, talk to you in English or Hebrew, give hints, trade, train with you, and fight Hela's army beside you.
* **[Music](Music):** Quicksilver's Walkman plays his own new theme, *Silver Hours* (*Sweet Dreams* is one option away), and Speed has a Walkman with his own, *Westview Blur*; the Witches' Road has a new version of its ballad for the choice; and a server can put its own songs in the world's music folder, which every player downloads by himself.
* **The Hero Creator:** every ability can have **its own colour**, picked with a colour picker; **every Sneak variant** of the heroes is a card of its own (Billy's Hex Barrier, Thor's Shatter Mjolnir, Spider-Man's Spider-Sense...), and so is the Scarlet Witch's Build Magic; Heimdall's, Odin's and Hela's abilities are there too; the editor asks before it throws away unsaved changes.
* **Fixes:** an old Mutant School's door can be mended (`/enhanced structure repair_school`); Loki is tougher (30 health, 6 armour); Agatha's Power Drain takes only a witch's or sorcerer's power; Spider-Man's *Brand New Day* suit is redrawn to match the film; becoming another hero puts the old hero's helmet, goggles, glasses or crown in your bag.
* **Update the server and every player together** (network protocol 9). Worlds from 3.4 open in 3.5 with Asgard in them (its city is built as you explore it), and the rune stones appear in land not explored yet.

## What's new in 3.4

Eilon asked for a Hero Creator that makes heroes as good as the mod's own.

* **Every hero's abilities, for your own hero.** All **81 abilities** of the sixteen heroes are powers you can pick in the [Hero Creator](Hero-Creator), each with its Sneak variant, its effects, sounds and animations, exactly as the hero has them: Thor's Lightning Beam, Quicksilver's Super Speed, Agatha's Witch's Shield and Ultron's Sentry Army on one hero, if you like. A **strength** slider makes each one weaker or stronger. The list is on [Heroes' Abilities](Hero-Abilities).
* **Ten keys.** Put your powers on **R G V B N**, alone or with **Sneak**, like the real heroes; a hero's ability brings its Sneak variant with it.
* **A bigger budget**: 90 points and up to 10 powers (was 60 and 8). Two powers of different heroes that drive the same thing (one shield, one grip, one beam...) cannot go together, and the editor says why.
* **Moves as good as the heroes'.** Every power plays a gesture (the hero's own, or one you choose), held and switched-on powers have a stance, and you choose how your hero flies, runs, runs at super speed and stands. Everyone sees it, in first person too.
* **Looks:** a cape, a hat, a mask, glowing eyes and an aura on top of the skin.
* **A new editor** with a 3D preview that moves, 22 ready-made heroes to start from ("Thor-like", "Speedster"...), categories and a search box in English and Hebrew.
* **Choose a Hero gives the hero's items**: Thor gets a Mjolnir that is already his, Agatha the Darkhold, Cyclops his glasses; never a second one.
* **Hero files of 3.3 still load:** they turn into the new format by themselves.
* **Update the server and every player together.** A 3.4 server and a 3.3 player (or the other way round) cannot play together (network protocol 8). Worlds from 3.3 open in 3.4 as they were; a custom hero saved with a player comes back as he was. A 3.3 server file keeps its Hero Creator budget of 60 and 8 powers until you change them.

## What's new in 3.3

Eilon asked for these.

* **Three new heroes.** **[Agatha Harkness](Agatha)**: a player with no power who reads the **Darkhold** becomes her, with the book's purple beam, flight, 40 health, the **Power Drain** that takes another hero's power away for 10 seconds, a purple **Witch's Shield** and a **Rune Trap**; two looks, *WandaVision* and *Agatha All Along*. **[Cyclops](Cyclops)**: put on his **ruby-quartz glasses** and fire the **Optic Blast** from your eyes, a Ricochet Shot, a Concussion Blast, an Optic Leap and the Rally; take the glasses off and a **giant red beam** pours out until you put them back on. **[Professor X](Professor-X)**: craft his **wheelchair**, choose the **young** or the **old** Charles, **hear the thoughts** of everyone around you, **take over a body** (a creature, or a player with PvP on, unless they are a telepath; Wanda, the Phoenix and Billy throw him out), freeze minds, and find everyone with **Cerebro**.
* **The Hero Creator.** A Creative-mode item: become any hero at once, or **make your own** from a catalogue of **thirty powers**, each with a strength from 1 to 10 within a budget, on the keys you choose, in your colours and **your own skin** (everyone sees it). Save it to a JSON file and load it on any server. See [Hero Creator](Hero-Creator).
* **New places in the world.** **[Hydra outposts](Structures#a-hydra-outpost)**, where **Loki's Scepter** is now kept (and nowhere else); **[Mjolnir's craters](Structures#mjolnirs-crater)**, with an unclaimed Mjolnir in the middle; and the **[Mutant School](Structures#the-mutant-school)**, with Professor X's wheelchair and Cyclops's glasses. `/locate structure` finds them, and they appear in the new chunks of old worlds. See [Structures](Structures).
* **Walls that really keep things out.** Billy's **Hex Barrier** stops everything from the front, has **60 health** and **no time limit**, mends while it is down, and **only chaos and dark magic wear it down** (3.3.1). Wanda's **Hex** lets in only those she approves, however they try, and **no power crosses its wall** from outside; her new **Hex Runes** take the powers of every other sorcerer inside. The **ward** of the Book of the Vishanti keeps out every monster and boss, what they shoot, and dark magic, and nothing else. See [Billy](Billy-and-the-Witches-Road#the-hex-barrier-sneak--b-held), [Scarlet Witch](Scarlet-Witch#the-wall-keeps-powers-out) and [The Sorcerers' Books](Sorcerers-Books#the-ward-what-it-keeps-out).
* **The Witches' Road sings.** An original witchy ballad in a different arrangement at each stage: the entrance, the Parkour, the Copy, the boat race and the end. A resource pack can replace it. See [The Witches' Road](Witches-Road#the-roads-music).
* **The speedsters run up walls.** Quicksilver and Tommy each have **two separate keys**: **R** slows time (Tommy's Slow Time is new, in teal) and **G** is Super Speed; use one or both. Sprinting into a wall at super speed, they **run up it, along the ceiling, round an overhang and down the far wall**, with no fall damage after. New attacks: the **Dash Strike** (both, **Sneak + G**), Quicksilver's **Pebble Barrage** (**N**) and Tommy's **Sonic Boom** (**Sneak + V**). See [Quicksilver](Quicksilver#running-up-walls-and-across-ceilings) and [Tommy](Tommy).
* **Easy web swinging.** Spider-Man's **Web Assist** (on by default; **J** switches it) picks the anchor for you in trees, houses and cliffs, never runs you into the ground or the leaves, lets go at the right moment and sends the next web while you hold **G**, with a small white web showing where it will go; in open land it swings from the sky. See [Spider-Man](Spider-Man#web-assist-easy-swinging-33).
* **Smoother animations.** Beams, crackles, shields, the Hex and the HUD no longer flash, pop or jump: they flow and fade.
* **Update the server and every player together.** A 3.3 server and a 3.2 player (or the other way round) cannot play together. Worlds from 3.2 open in 3.3 as they were. (Billy's `barrierCooldown` was renamed `barrierLowerCooldown`, so a 3.2 server file gets the new default of 10.)

## What's new in 3.2

## What's new in 3.1

Four things Eilon asked for.

* **Build Magic for Wanda and Billy.** Press **Y** and build with the mouse, as in Axiom and WorldEdit but with your own magic: click two corners and right-click to fill, empty, change or paint them, double-click a whole tree or house, and hold **Y** for the Spell Wheel of eleven spells (Fill, Vanish, Change, Paint, Pull & Push, Shapes, Copy, Move, Repeat, Land, Grow). You see everything before it happens, and **Ctrl + Z** takes it back, even tomorrow. Wanda's magic has no limit; Billy's grows as he practises, from Apprentice to Wiccan. See [Build Magic](Build-Magic).
* **Every telekinesis works in the boss fights, and Billy moves blocks.** Billy tears blocks out of the ground and sets them down (**V**, **Sneak + V**). Wanda, Billy, the Phoenix and Magneto can take hold of Ultron, Thanos and Apocalypse, and the Wither, the Warden and the Elder Guardian too, as bosses: for a few seconds, then they break free. The blocks of a boss arena are only borrowed: the hole mends and the block crumbles. Billy and the Phoenix catch Thanos's sword, meteors and orbs and throw them back.
* **Magneto masters every metal.** **R** lifts anything with metal in it: Vision, Ultron, armour or a sword in any hand, arrows in flight, metal blocks, rails and doors, and a Sentry he throws is crushed. **Sneak + R** when R isn't holding anything is the **Magnetic Pull**: the metal around you, the ore in the rock included, flies into your inventory. The metal people build with (lanterns, rails, hoppers) comes only from where you face, so your own base behind you is safe. See [Magneto](Magneto).
* **Apocalypse never heals, and Wanda and the Phoenix hit him critically.** His health never comes back (his shield still knits). Every hit of Wanda's or the Phoenix's mind power is a **critical hit**: twice as strong on his shield and on him, with a burst and a crack you can hear. Everyone else still hurts him when his shield is down, but much less. See [Apocalypse](Apocalypse).
* **Update the server and every player together.** A 3.1 server and a 3.0 player (or the other way round) cannot play together. Worlds from 3.0 open in 3.1 as they were.

## What's new in 3.0

Five new heroes, all of them asked for by Eilon. The last three are villains, and bosses: their powers are won only by defeating them.

* **The Phoenix, Jean Grey.** Find a Phoenix Ember, hold right-click for three seconds, and read minds (**R**: a violet panel shows the items of whoever you look at, a player's whole inventory included; **Sneak + R** finds every mind around you through the walls), fly, lift things with your mind, take over a creature (**B**) or freeze everyone around you (**Sneak + B**), and hold **N** to charge the **Phoenix Burst**: a wave of cosmic fire that spreads in every direction. Two looks, from *X-Men: Apocalypse* and *Spider-Man: Brand New Day*. See [Phoenix](Phoenix).
* **Spider-Man, Peter Parker.** Get bitten by a **Radioactive Spider** (craft it or find it in a chest) and go by web: **R** wraps what you look at (**Sneak + R**: a burst of 24 strands in every direction), **G** zips you to the block you look at and on to the next (**Sneak + G**, held: swing on the web), jump at a wall and keep pushing to **climb it, and walk on the ceiling** (**V**), and a spider-sense warns you of danger before it hits. In the **Iron Spider** suit **B** unfolds four mechanical legs (**Sneak + B**, twice: Instant Kill), and **N** turns his eyes black for a short, strong, costly surge. Three suits, from *Homecoming*, *Infinity War* and *Brand New Day*. See [Spider-Man](Spider-Man).
* **Thanos, the Mad Titan, a boss.** Hold the **Tesseract** in one hand and **Loki's Scepter** in the other for five seconds, in open country in the Overworld, and he falls through a portal in the sky. The Infinity Stones come to him over time: he starts with the Power Stone and takes a new one every 90 seconds of fighting, **up to five, never the sixth**, and each brings new moves, so beat him fast and together (break his clench while he closes the Gauntlet). His powers are won only by defeating him: whoever did 3 percent of his health, and whoever struck the last blow, gets the **Infinity Gauntlet**; hold it for three seconds with no power and you are Thanos, with five stones on five keys. See [Thanos](Thanos).
* **Ultron, a boss.** Craft the **Ultron Program**, hold it in one hand and **Loki's Scepter** in the other and **Sneak + right-click** open, level ground: scrap rises out of it, and Ultron wakes among his robots, the **Sentries**, with more coming in waves. When he is down to 55 percent the ground under everyone tears loose and **rises 40 blocks into the sky**, as Sokovia did, and the fight goes on up there. Destroy his body and he **moves into a Sentry**, in a weaker body: he is gone for good only when no Sentry is left. His powers are won only by defeating him: every fighter who stayed a minute and hurt him or his Sentries gets **Ultron's Core**, and the Core with the Scepter, with no power, makes you Ultron (plasma from the fingers, anti-gravity flight, gravity, uploads, Sentries of your own). See [Ultron](Ultron).
* **Apocalypse (En Sabah Nur), a boss.** Plant the **Capstone** in the sand, by day under the open sky, with a Scarlet Witch or a Phoenix near, and he steps out of a violet portal. He fights with **sand** (lances, waves, spikes, boulders, tornadoes and a cocoon that swallows you), his portals and the Four Horsemen, inside a **golden shield that only the mind power of a Scarlet Witch or a Phoenix breaks**; when it shatters, everyone has ten seconds to hurt him. His powers are won only by defeating him: every fighter who stayed a minute and hurt him or his shield earns the **Essence of En Sabah Nur**; hold it for three seconds with no power and you become Apocalypse, with sand, levitation, a portal, a shield of your own and the power to make your friends stronger. See [Apocalypse](Apocalypse).
* **Update the server and every player together.** A player with an older version cannot join a 3.0 server, and a 3.0 player cannot join an older one. Worlds from 2.x open in 3.0 with every hero as they left them.

## Animations and effects

Every hero moves like the character on screen: each flier has a flight of his own (Thor even whirls Mjolnir on its strap while he hovers, with the handle really in his fist), every ability is acted out, and your own hands move in first person. Other players see all of it too. Flying leaves trails, rings and wind behind you, and Thor and Vision can break the sound barrier. The client options *Hero animations*, *Reduced motion* and *Flight effects* tone it down or turn it off, and *Hide heroes' armour* (on by default) stops armour from hiding the characters: they are in `config/enhanced-client.toml` and behind the *Config* button of Enhanced in the Mods screen, see [Server Configuration](Server-Configuration#client-options-animations-effects-and-looks). The details are in [docs/DESIGN.md](https://github.com/yuvalkolodkingal/enhanced/blob/main/docs/DESIGN.md).

## Download and install

Every push to `main` builds the mod, runs the in-game tests and publishes the jar on the [Releases](https://github.com/yuvalkolodkingal/enhanced/releases) page. The newest build is always marked *Latest*.

* **Minecraft** 1.21.1
* **Mod loader** NeoForge 21.1.x
* **Install it on** the client, and on the server too for multiplayer

Put the jar in the `mods` folder and start the game. The details are on [Installation](Installation). The jar contains a copyrighted song, so it is for personal use only: do not share it publicly.
