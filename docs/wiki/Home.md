# Enhanced

**Enhanced** is a Minecraft mod (NeoForge, Minecraft 1.21.1) where you can become one of the *Enhanced*: a hero from the Marvel films and shows, with the powers, the look and the colours you know from the screen. You can be **Magneto**, the Master of Magnetism; **Wanda Maximoff, the Scarlet Witch**; **Quicksilver**, who slows the world down around him (to *Sweet Dreams*) or just outruns it; **Vision**, the Mind Stone in a synthetic body (or **White Vision**); Wanda's sons **Tommy (Speed)** and **Billy (Wiccan)**; **Loki**, the God of Mischief; **Jean Grey, the Phoenix**, who reads minds and burns with cosmic fire; **Peter Parker, Spider-Man**, who goes everywhere on a web; or, if you are worthy, **Thor**, with **Mjolnir** and **Stormbreaker**. And three villains are bosses whose powers are won only by defeating them: **Thanos**, the Mad Titan, who gathers the Infinity Stones as he fights; **Ultron**, the AI of the Mind Stone, who lifts the ground into the sky; and En Sabah Nur, the first mutant, **Apocalypse**, whose golden shield only mind power breaks.

Original hero concepts by Eilon Kolodkin-Gal. This wiki is the player's guide. The full design, with every rule and number, is in [docs/DESIGN.md](https://github.com/yuvalkolodkingal/enhanced/blob/main/docs/DESIGN.md).

## What's new in 3.2

Eilon asked for these.

* **The Witches' Road has trials.** Open a Witches' Road Door with a Scarlet Witch Gene and, instead of choosing at once, **walk the Road**: a dimension of its own with three trials, each built for you alone. A **Parkour** over the void, a structure to **copy** block by block, and a long **ice-boat race**. Each success opens the next; at the end you choose to become Billy or Tommy. Falling never hurts, you can leave at any time, and your Gene is used up only at the end. See [The Witches' Road](Witches-Road).
* **Billy fires a beam, and has ten ranks.** Hold **R** for a continuous **Hex Beam** that you aim, stronger and longer-reaching as he ranks up; **Sneak + B** raises the **Hex Barrier**, a plate of blue magic that stops what comes at him from the front. His Build Magic ranks go from 4 up to **10** (Apprentice to Chaos Master), with a meter that grows to 32,768. See [Billy](Billy-and-the-Witches-Road) and [Build Magic](Build-Magic).
* **Wanda has an unlimited shield and an Instant level.** The Chaos Shield costs nothing and never breaks or runs out (a server can bring the old limits back). Press **Ctrl + I** in build mode and her spells, undos and redos appear all at once, with no waiting. See [Scarlet Witch](Scarlet-Witch) and [Build Magic](Build-Magic#the-instant-level-ctrl--i).
* **Two books of magic.** The **Book of the Vishanti** lets a sorcerer (Loki, Wanda or Billy) save places and teleport to them, and raise a **ward** round the base that no monster can enter, as big as the sorcerer is strong. The **Darkhold**, Agatha's book, gives a purple beam, poisons whoever uses magic on you, and has a hex mark and a mind glimpse. Both are crafted. See [The Sorcerers' Books](Sorcerers-Books) and [Items and Crafting](Items-and-Crafting).
* **Apocalypse is weaker.** One master knob, `apocalypse_boss.powerFactor` (0.65), cuts his damage and the number of his lances, Horsemen and tornadoes by about a third and slows his attacks; his shields start at 100 instead of 150, and his windows last 12 seconds. See [Apocalypse](Apocalypse#how-strong-he-is-the-master-factor).
* **Update the server and every player together.** A 3.2 server and a 3.1 player (or the other way round) cannot play together. Worlds from 3.1 open in 3.2 as they were.

## What's new in 3.1

Four things Eilon asked for.

* **Build Magic for Wanda and Billy.** Press **Y** and build with the mouse, as in Axiom and WorldEdit but with your own magic: click two corners and right-click to fill, empty, change or paint them, double-click a whole tree or house, and hold **Y** for the Spell Wheel of eleven spells (Fill, Vanish, Change, Paint, Pull & Push, Shapes, Copy, Move, Repeat, Land, Grow). You see everything before it happens, and **Ctrl + Z** takes it back, even tomorrow. Wanda's magic has no limit; Billy's grows as he practises, from Apprentice to Wiccan. See [Build Magic](Build-Magic).
* **Every telekinesis works in the boss fights, and Billy moves blocks.** Billy tears blocks out of the ground and sets them down (**V**, **Sneak + V**). Wanda, Billy, the Phoenix and Magneto can take hold of Ultron, Thanos and Apocalypse, and the Wither, the Warden and the Elder Guardian too, as bosses: for a few seconds, then they break free. The blocks of a boss arena are only borrowed: the hole mends and the block crumbles. Billy and the Phoenix catch Thanos's sword, meteors and orbs and throw them back.
* **Magneto masters every metal.** **R** lifts anything with metal in it: Vision, Ultron, armour or a sword in any hand, arrows in flight, metal blocks, rails and doors, and a Sentry he throws is crushed. **Sneak + R** when R isn't holding anything is the **Magnetic Pull**: the metal around you, the ore in the rock included, flies into your inventory. The metal people build with (lanterns, rails, hoppers) comes only from where you face, so your own base behind you is safe. See [Magneto](Magneto).
* **Apocalypse never heals, and Wanda and the Phoenix hit him critically.** His health never comes back (his shield still knits). Every hit of Wanda's or the Phoenix's mind power is a **critical hit**: twice as strong on his shield and on him, with a burst and a crack you can hear. Everyone else still hurts him when his shield is down, but much less. See [Apocalypse](Apocalypse).
* **Update the server and every player together.** A 3.1 server and a 3.0 player (or the other way round) cannot play together. Worlds from 3.0 open in 3.1 as they were.

## What's new in 3.0

Five new heroes, all of them asked for by Eilon. The last three are villains, and bosses: their powers are won only by defeating them.

* **The Phoenix, Jean Grey.** Find a Phoenix Ember, hold right-click for three seconds, and read minds (**R**: a violet panel shows the items of whoever you look at, a player's whole inventory included; **Sneak + R** finds every mind around you through the walls), fly, lift things with your mind, take over a creature (**B**) or freeze everyone around you (**Sneak + B**), and hold **N** to charge the **Phoenix Burst**: a wave of cosmic fire that spreads in every direction. Two looks, from *X-Men: Apocalypse* and *Spider-Man: Brand New Day*. See [Phoenix](Phoenix).
* **Spider-Man, Peter Parker.** Get bitten by a **Radioactive Spider** (craft it or find it in a chest) and go by web: **R** wraps what you look at (**Sneak + R**: a burst of 24 strands in every direction), **G** zips you to the block you look at and on to the next (**Sneak + G**, held: swing on the web), jump at a wall and keep pushing to **climb it, and walk on the ceiling** (**V**), and a spider-sense warns you of danger before it hits. In the **Iron Spider** suit **B** unfolds four mechanical legs (**Sneak + B**, twice: Instant Kill), and **N** turns his eyes black for a short, strong, costly surge. Three suits, from *Homecoming*, *Infinity War* and *Brand New Day*. See [Spider-Man](Spider-Man).
* **Thanos, the Mad Titan, a boss.** Hold the **Tesseract** in one hand and **Loki's Scepter** in the other for five seconds, in open country in the Overworld, and he falls through a portal in the sky. The Infinity Stones come to him over time: he starts with the Power Stone and takes a new one every 90 seconds of fighting, **up to five, never the sixth**, and each brings new moves, so beat him fast and together (break his clench while he closes the Gauntlet). His powers are won only by defeating him: whoever did 3 percent of his health, and whoever struck the last blow, gets the **Infinity Gauntlet**; hold it for three seconds with no power and you are Thanos, with five stones on five keys. See [Thanos](Thanos).
* **Ultron, a boss.** Craft the **Ultron Program**, hold it in one hand and **Loki's Scepter** in the other and **Sneak + right-click** open, level ground: scrap rises out of it, and Ultron wakes among his robots, the **Sentries**, with more coming in waves. When he is down to 55 percent the ground under everyone tears loose and **rises 40 blocks into the sky**, as Sokovia did, and the fight goes on up there. Destroy his body and he **moves into a Sentry**, in a weaker body: he is gone for good only when no Sentry is left. His powers are won only by defeating him: every fighter who stayed a minute and hurt him or his Sentries gets **Ultron's Core**, and the Core with the Scepter, with no power, makes you Ultron (plasma from the fingers, anti-gravity flight, gravity, uploads, Sentries of your own). See [Ultron](Ultron).
* **Apocalypse (En Sabah Nur), a boss.** Plant the **Capstone** in the sand, by day under the open sky, with a Scarlet Witch or a Phoenix near, and he steps out of a violet portal. He fights with **sand** (lances, waves, spikes, boulders, tornadoes and a cocoon that swallows you), his portals and the Four Horsemen, inside a **golden shield that only the mind power of a Scarlet Witch or a Phoenix breaks**; when it shatters, everyone has ten seconds to hurt him. His powers are won only by defeating him: every fighter who stayed a minute and hurt him or his shield earns the **Essence of En Sabah Nur**; hold it for three seconds with no power and you become Apocalypse, with sand, levitation, a portal, a shield of your own and the power to make your friends stronger. See [Apocalypse](Apocalypse).
* **Update the server and every player together.** A player with an older version cannot join a 3.0 server, and a 3.0 player cannot join an older one. Worlds from 2.x open in 3.0 with every hero as they left them.

## What's new in 2.3

* **Bigger Mjolnir and Stormbreaker**, as in the films, and **Stormbreaker is a fifth stronger** than Mjolnir in everything they share: blows, throws and the lightning beam. See [Thor](Thor).
* **The Chaos Shield stops blows up close too.** A blow that reaches a complete shield is stopped, wears the shield down and throws the attacker out. See [Scarlet Witch](Scarlet-Witch).
* **The glowing eyes sit on the eyes** of every look of Wanda and Thor.
* **Heroes' armour is hidden.** It still protects, but heroes are drawn without it, so you see the character. A client option brings it back. See [FAQ](FAQ#why-cant-i-see-my-armour).
* **Thor's Love and Thunder look, redrawn** to match the film: navy, gold and black armour, long ash-blond hair swept back from a bare forehead, a full beard, a red cape. And a new look, **Love and Thunder, helmet**, with the winged helmet from the New Asgard fight. See [Looks](Looks).
* **Vision can't leave Wanda's Hex**, unless he hurts her badly or defeats her. White Vision is held too, but never comes apart at the wall. See [Scarlet Witch](Scarlet-Witch#vision-cant-leave-your-hex).
* **The Vision of the Hex.** When Wanda raises her Hex, a Vision appears and fights at her side; look at a creature and press **Z** to send him at it.
* **The voices of the Infinity Stones.** The Space Stone sounds when the Tesseract is used, the Mind Stone when the Scepter is, and both hum quietly while you hold them.

## What's new in 2.2

* **Everyone moves like the character on screen.** New poses for every hero, a flight of his own for every flier, every ability acted out (other players see it too), and trails, rings and wind behind flying heroes. See *Animations and effects* below.
* **Thor's lightning beam.** Hold **V** and lightning pours from his hand, or from the head of Mjolnir or Stormbreaker: longer and twice as strong through Mjolnir or Stormbreaker, stronger yet as the God of Thunder. See [Thor](Thor).
* **Shatter Mjolnir.** **Sneak + R** and Mjolnir bursts into glowing pieces that strike his enemies, then fly back and make it whole in his hand again.
* **Pin someone with Mjolnir.** **Sneak + right-click** a creature or player and Mjolnir pins them down until its owner lifts it.
* **A harder Chaos Shield.** It takes 3/4 of a second to form, holds for 8 seconds, has 30 points of strength, shatters when those run out, and needs 6 to 15 seconds to recharge. See [Scarlet Witch](Scarlet-Witch).
* **The Tesseract awakens Wanda too.** Use it with a Scarlet Witch Gene in your other hand and you become the Scarlet Witch instead of Loki.
* **Loki's Scepter confuses.** A hit makes a creature forget its target and wander about, and gives a player Nausea and Slowness. See [Loki](Loki).

## Animations and effects

Every hero moves like the character on screen: each flier has a flight of his own (Thor even whirls Mjolnir on its strap while he hovers, with the handle really in his fist), every ability is acted out, and your own hands move in first person. Other players see all of it too. Flying leaves trails, rings and wind behind you, and Thor and Vision can break the sound barrier. The client options *Hero animations*, *Reduced motion* and *Flight effects* tone it down or turn it off, and *Hide heroes' armour* (on by default) stops armour from hiding the characters: they are in `config/enhanced-client.toml` and behind the *Config* button of Enhanced in the Mods screen, see [Server Configuration](Server-Configuration#client-options-animations-effects-and-looks). The details are in [docs/DESIGN.md](https://github.com/yuvalkolodkingal/enhanced/blob/main/docs/DESIGN.md).

## Download and install

Every push to `main` builds the mod, runs the in-game tests and publishes the jar on the [Releases](https://github.com/yuvalkolodkingal/enhanced/releases) page. The newest build is always marked *Latest*.

* **Minecraft** 1.21.1
* **Mod loader** NeoForge 21.1.x
* **Install it on** the client, and on the server too for multiplayer

Put the jar in the `mods` folder and start the game. The details are on [Installation](Installation). The jar contains a copyrighted song, so it is for personal use only: do not share it publicly.
