# Billy and the Witches' Road

Billy Maximoff (Wiccan), Wanda's other son. In *WandaVision* he hears every thought around him and freezes a squad of S.W.O.R.D. soldiers; in *Agatha All Along* his own chaos magic makes the Witches' Road real. In the mod, the Road is a door, and the door is the way to become Billy (or Tommy).

## The Witches' Road Door

**Crafting:**

```
A L A
T E T
A L A

A = Amethyst Shard
L = Soul Lantern
T = Dark Oak Trapdoor
E = Eye of Ender
```

It is a pair of weathered cellar doors set flat in the floor (you can stand on it), outlined in a faint blue-green glow, like the door in the basement in *Agatha All Along*. It is made of wood: an axe breaks it fastest, and pistons can't push it. Breaking it drops the door again.

### Seeking the Road: becoming Billy or Tommy

A player **with no power** right-clicks the door **holding a Scarlet Witch Gene**.

1. A ring of blue-green light runs around the door for two seconds.
2. The doors open onto a spiral stair among glowing roots, and the Road asks: **"Seekest thou the Road?"**
3. Choose **Billy: Wiccan** (blue magic, flight, telekinesis) or **Tommy: Speed** (super speed, like his uncle). See [Tommy](Tommy).
4. One gene is used up. Anyone else holding a gene can join the ritual and choose too.
5. The door closes again five seconds after it opens.

Without a gene the door won't open ("The Road only opens for witch blood"). A hero already "walks their own road".

### Walking the Road

The **Scarlet Witch** and **Billy** (whose magic made the Road) can travel it. Right-clicking a door without a gene opens it and takes them to the **next Witches' Road door** in the same dimension (in the order the doors were placed, going round), arriving on it in a column of light. A door that is gone is skipped. Whatever they hold with telekinesis stays behind, and the Hex wall is respected. Cooldown 3 seconds.

## Billy's abilities

Billy's magic is blue with a white-hot core, crackling and branching like a Tesla coil.

| # | Key | Ability | How it works |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **R** | Hex Bolt | A crackling blue arc from his hand to the first creature he looks at within 24 blocks: 6 damage, and it forks on to the two nearest creatures within 5 blocks of it (3 damage each). Everyone around sees the bolts. Cooldown half a second. |
| 2 | **G** (toggle) | Flight | Calm, weightless flight with open hands, like his mother's (speed 0.08). You can also double-tap **Space**. |
| 3 | **V** | Telekinesis | Wanda's telekinesis, in his blue: creatures, dropped items, minecarts, boats, primed TNT, falling blocks, **and projectiles in flight**: an arrow he catches hangs in front of him, pointing where he looks, and flies off at his command. He doesn't tear blocks out of the ground (that is Wanda's). Press again to launch, **Sneak** + the key to put down gently. |
| 4 | **B** | Freeze | Every creature and player within 12 blocks in a 90 degree cone in front of him, in his line of sight, is **spellbound** for 5 seconds: they can't move, jump or attack, can't pick a new target, and blue sparks circle their heads. The Ender Dragon and the Wither are too strong for it. Cooldown 12 seconds. |
| 5 | **N** (toggle) | The Noise | He hears every mind within 32 blocks: every creature glows through walls, **red** if it is hostile and about to attack, **blue** otherwise. Whispers crowd his head, louder the more minds are near. |

**The crown.** While he flies, and for three seconds after each spell, a glowing chrome crown forms on his head, in shades from ice-white to deep blue (the *Wiccan* look already has it).

## Looks

*Wiccan* (*Agatha All Along*), *Teen* (on the Witches' Road), *Halloween* (his *WandaVision* costume), *Wiccan (comics)*, and *Your own skin*. Press **H** to switch. See [Looks](Looks).

## Numbers you can change

Billy's flight speed, the Hex Bolt's damage, how long Freeze holds (5 seconds), and how far The Noise reaches (32 blocks) are the `billy.*` options on [Server Configuration](Server-Configuration).
