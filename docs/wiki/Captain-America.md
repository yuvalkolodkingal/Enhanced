# Captain America

The First Avenger (3.8): the super-soldier the **[Super Soldier Serum](Super-Soldier-Serum)** makes, with the **Vibranium Shield** that blocks everything from the front, flies from his hand, bounces from foe to foe and comes back to him. He is faster, stronger and tougher than anyone, he rallies his friends, and, as in *Avengers: Endgame*, he is **worthy of Mjolnir**.

Hebrew: [עברית](Captain-America-HE)

## The quick version

* **How:** use a **[Super Soldier Serum](Super-Soldier-Serum)** (in the lab of the Avengers Tower) and choose **Captain America**. You get a Vibranium Shield too.
* **R** Shield Throw, **G** Shield Slam, **V** Shield Charge, **B** Assemble, **N** Combat Combo. **Sneak** changes each of them: Ricochet Throw, Super-Soldier Leap, Shield Bash, Inspire, Flip Kick.
* **Right-click with the shield:** block. For him it stops **everything** from the front, explosions and beams too, at once, and sends arrows back.
* He doesn't fly: he runs, leaps and throws.

## Becoming Captain America

* Use a [Super Soldier Serum](Super-Soldier-Serum) and choose the **Captain America** card. Your old hero is replaced; if you have no Vibranium Shield, one goes into the hand that held the serum. You wake as **"The Captain"**, *The First Avenger*.
* Or **Choose a Hero** in the [Hero Creator](Hero-Creator) (with a shield), or `/enhanced power set <player> captain_america` (and a look: `classic`, `stealth` or `own`).

**Losing it:** the Mutant Cure, `/enhanced power set <player> none`, another serum choice, an [Arc Reactor](Iron-Man#becoming-iron-man-the-arc-reactors), [Eternity's](Eternity) Love, being sacrificed at Vormir, or choosing another hero. The shield stays yours: it is an item, and it still blocks like a shield.

## The body

The super-soldier's **Speed II, Strength II and Resistance I**, the mod's way: they are part of his body, not potion effects, so they show no icons, milk can't take them away and they come back when he logs in.

* **Speed II:** 40% faster.
* **Strength II:** every blow **+6**, a little faster to swing and with more knockback.
* **Resistance I:** every hit on him is **20% weaker** (not the void or `/kill`).
* **26 health** (13 hearts), a higher jump, and 4 more blocks of fall before it hurts.
* **Worthy of Mjolnir** (*Endgame*): he can lift a Mjolnir nobody owns and claim it, as Thor does. See [Thor](Thor#the-worthy).

His HUD says it: "Super-soldier: Speed II · Strength II · Resistance I".

## The Vibranium Shield

A round shield, red and silver rings round a blue centre with a white star, a leather strap behind. It is **unbreakable**, fire can't burn it, and it can't be crafted.

* **Anyone** can carry it and block with it like any shield.
* **For Captain America** it is much more: while he holds it up (right-click), it **blocks 100% of everything from the front**: blows, arrows, explosions, beams and the mod's powers (only the void and `/kill` get through). It comes up **at once** (no wait, as a normal shield has), an **axe never knocks it down**, **arrows, bolts, tridents, fireballs and the mod's shots bounce back** toward whoever shot them, and a creature that hits it from the front is pushed back. Every block rings with a deep vibranium sound and a silver flash.
* When he carries it but doesn't hold it, it hangs **on his back** (an elytra or a cape hides it).

**Where it comes from:**

* The serum's Captain America card, and **Choose a Hero → Captain America**, give one (if you have none).
* **Cap's closet** in the penthouse of the [Avengers Tower](Structures#avengers-tower): a Tech Case by the bed holds a Vibranium Shield, **for Captain America only**. Anyone else hears "This is Cap's shield." One for each Captain America.

## Abilities

| # | Key | Ability | How it works |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **R** | Shield Throw | Needs a shield in a hand. The shield flies from that hand where he looks (up to **24 blocks**), hits the first creature in its way for **8**, then **flies back to the hand it left**, through walls on the way home (as Mjolnir does). He catches it into the same hand (or his bag if the hand is full). While it flies, that hand is empty and he can't throw again. Any shield can be thrown; if he is gone when it comes back, it drops. **1 second** after the catch. **Sneak + R: Ricochet Throw** (only with the Vibranium Shield): on each hit the shield **bounces to the nearest other foe** within 8 blocks that it can see, up to **3 bounces** (8, 7, 6, 5 damage), and it can bounce off a wall once more toward a foe near the wall, then flies home. **3 seconds** after the catch. |
| 2 | **G** | Shield Slam | A short hop, and he drives the shield into the ground: a shockwave of **6** to every creature on the ground within **4 blocks**, knocked up. Without a shield, a fist slam at half. No fall damage on that landing. Cooldown 5 seconds. **Sneak + G: Super-Soldier Leap**: a long running leap forward and up, with a safe landing. Cooldown 2 seconds. |
| 3 | **V** | Shield Charge | He runs **8 blocks** in a moment behind his raised shield: every creature in his path takes **6** and is thrown aside; while he charges, his shield blocks from the front. A wall stops him (no harm to him). Cooldown 5 seconds. **Sneak + V: Shield Bash**: a bash at the creature right in front: **5** and **stunned** for 1.5 seconds (it can barely move and can't jump; a boss only takes the damage). Cooldown 3 seconds. |
| 4 | **B** | Assemble | He raises the shield and calls: he and every **ally** within **16 blocks** (players on his team, or every player when PvP is off; tamed pets; iron golems; villagers) get **Strength I and Speed I for 20 seconds**, in a ring of blue-white light. Cooldown 1 minute. **Sneak + B: Inspire**: he and his allies within 16 blocks heal **6** (3 hearts) and lose Weakness, Slowness and Mining Fatigue. Cooldown 30 seconds. |
| 5 | **N** | Combat Combo | Punches on the nearest creature in front (3.5 blocks): **4**, **4**, then a spinning kick of **7** that knocks it back. A blow every 8 ticks; after a second and a half, the combo starts again. **Sneak + N: Flip Kick**: a backflip kick: **5**, the creature is thrown up and back, and he lands about 3 blocks back. Cooldown 2 seconds. |

With PvP off, his abilities don't hurt players. Everything he pushes at the edge of the [Vormir Cliff](Vormir) counts as his push.

Press **H** to switch. See [Looks](Looks). The shield is drawn in first and third person: held, raised, on his back and in flight.

## The HUD

Next to the usual panel: the shield ("Shield: in hand", "on your back", "flying (2 bounces left)" or "none"), the combo ("Combo 2/3"), "Charging!", "Slam!", Assemble's seconds ("Assembled: 14s"), and the super-soldier's line.

## Against the others

* He is **worthy**: he can lift Mjolnir. A server can take that away (`captain_america.worthy`).
* His throw, slam, charge and combo can't keep [Wolverine](Wolverine) or [Deadpool](Deadpool) down: their [healing factor](Healing-Factor) gets them up again.
* [Magneto](Magneto) can't tear the Vibranium Shield from his hand: vibranium is not iron.

## Captain America in the Hero Creator

His abilities are in the [Hero Creator](Hero-Creator) (`captain.shield_throw`, `captain.shield_slam`, `captain.shield_charge`, `captain.assemble`, `captain.combo`, `captain.body`), and his five Sneak variants are cards of their own (`captain.ricochet`, `captain.super_leap`, `captain.shield_bash`, `captain.inspire`, `captain.flip_kick`). **The Super-Soldier** body makes a custom hero worthy of Mjolnir. Since 3.8.1 his Combat Combo and Flip Kick deal a damage of their own (`cap_combo`), so a custom hero's card strength scales them. A custom hero's Shield Throw needs a shield in hand: any shield flies and comes back, only the Vibranium Shield ricochets. See [Heroes' Abilities](Hero-Abilities#captain-america-6). The ready-made hero is *Captain America-like*, and *Captain America* is a run style.

## Numbers you can change

Every number of his body and abilities is a `captain_america.*` option, and the shield's are `vibranium_shield.*`. See [Server Configuration](Server-Configuration#captain-america-and-the-vibranium-shield).
