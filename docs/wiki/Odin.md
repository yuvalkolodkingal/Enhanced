# Odin

The Allfather (3.5), king of Asgard, as in the *Thor* films: the spear **Gungnir**, the **Odinforce**, his two ravens **Huginn and Muninn**, and the deep healing sleep of the **Odinsleep**. As a hero he fires golden bolts from Gungnir and throws it, raises a golden shield or heals his allies, banishes his enemies, sends his ravens to look for him, and commands the warriors of Asgard.

Hebrew: [עברית](Odin-HE)

## The quick version

* **How:** beat [Hela](Hela) in Asgard's throne room. Her first defeat wakes Odin and opens the **vault** under the throne room. Take **Gungnir** from its stand there, then, with no power, **sit on the Throne of Asgard holding your Gungnir**: you are crowned Odin.
* **R** fires Gungnir (Sneak: throw it), **G** (hold) raises the Odinforce Shield (Sneak: heal), **V** banishes (Sneak: a golden wave), **B** sends the ravens (Sneak: their report), **N** commands the warriors (Sneak: the Odinsleep).
* He doesn't fly. His Gungnir opens the Bifrost.

## Becoming Odin

1. **Odin sleeps.** Until Hela is beaten the first time, Odin lies in the Odinsleep in his chamber in the palace's east wing. You can't talk to him: Frigga answers for him. The **vault** under the throne room is closed by a wall of gold.
2. **Beat Hela.** Everyone rewarded in the fight (see [Hela](Hela#rewards)) gets the right to Gungnir. Her **first** defeat wakes Odin ("Odin wakes: the Allfather rises from the Odinsleep") and **opens the vault**.
3. **Take Gungnir.** Go down to the vault and right-click **Gungnir's Stand**: "You take Gungnir from its stand. It answers to you alone." Each worthy player takes **his own** Gungnir (the stand keeps showing one for the next). Without the right: "Only one who stood against Hela may take Gungnir."
4. **Sit on the Throne of Asgard** (right-click it) with **no power** and **your own Gungnir** in your hand. "Golden light pours from the throne. Stay, and hold Gungnir." Stay seated for **3 seconds** of golden light, and you are crowned: "You are crowned on the Throne of Asgard. Long may you reign, Allfather." The Asgardians in the hall bow to you. (With another power the throne refuses: "The throne will not crown one who bears another power." Sitting on it without Gungnir is just sitting.)

## Gungnir

The spear of the Allfather: long, gold, with a winged blade.

* **For anyone:** a spear that hits for **9**, reaches **one block further** than your arm, and never breaks or burns. Anyone can fight with somebody else's Gungnir.
* **Its owner opens the Bifrost with it:** right-click for the Bifrost screen, Sneak + right-click to where you look (any power, or none). Somebody else's Gungnir doesn't open it.
* **Bound to its owner**, like Hofund. If you lose yours, take a new one from the stand (or, as Odin, press **R** with none of your own anywhere): the old one crumbles to dust ("This Gungnir crumbles to dust: its bond is broken").
* It is in Asgard's never-lost list.

## The body

* **+12 health** (16 hearts), **+6 armour**, half the knockback, and **Regeneration I** while he is below half his health.
* He doesn't fly.

A shield in the way stops it. Needs Gungnir in his hand ("Gungnir is not in your hand"); with no Gungnir of his own anywhere, R calls a new one. Cooldown about a second. **Sneak + R: Gungnir Throw**: the spear flies up to 40 blocks, through two creatures, **16** damage each, and comes back to his hand through walls. **R** again calls it back early. Up to **12 seconds**; cooldown 10 seconds (half again as long if it was worn out or broken). **Sneak + G: Odinforce Heal**: heals him and every ally within 8 blocks (players who are not on another team, tamed animals, Asgardians) by **8**, and takes their harmful effects away. Cooldown 30 seconds. |
| 3 | **V** | Banish | The creature he looks at (up to 32 blocks), in golden light: a **monster is banished**, gone, with no drops. **Bosses**, the creatures of a boss fight and **named** creatures resist: they take **20** damage instead. Animals, allies and players are spared, and a spared one costs nothing. A player is banished only with **PvP on**: the Bifrost takes him home. Cooldown 20 seconds. **Sneak + V: Odinforce Wave**: a golden shock wave round him (8 blocks), **10** damage and a strong push. Cooldown 11 seconds. |

Cooldown 45 seconds. |
| 5 | **N** | Allfather's Command | He points at what he looks at (up to 48 blocks). **In Asgard**, every Einherjar and warrior within 64 blocks attacks it (or follows him, if he points at nothing). **Elsewhere**, four Einherjar appear in a golden flash and attack it. Cooldown 60 seconds. **Sneak + N: Odinsleep** (on/off): he kneels in a golden cocoon. He can't move, attack or use anything, takes much less damage (Resistance IV) and heals **5% of his health every second**. It ends when he is whole, when he presses Sneak + N again, or after **30 seconds**; then it waits 90 seconds. Not at full health: "You are not weary: the Odinsleep is for the wounded." |

### Odin's Bifrost

Odin opens the Bifrost with his Gungnir, as any owner does (use the spear): the same screen, the same cooldown, the same [circle](Asgard#the-bifrost-takes-everyone-in-its-circle). A banished player goes home by it too.

## Looks

Press **H** to switch. See [Looks](Looks).

| Look | Id | What it is |
|---|---|---|
| **The Allfather** (default) | `allfather` | Gold and copper armour, a great **red cape**, the gold **winged helmet**, and his **gold eyepatch**. |
| **The Wanderer** | `wanderer` | A long dark coat, no helmet, the eyepatch. |
| **Your own skin** | `own` | His powers in your own skin. |

He moves like a king: a regal stance, the spear held up for the blast and thrown overhand, kneeling in the Odinsleep and rising from it.

## The HUD

Gungnir in flight, or "no Gungnir: press to call it"; the ravens (how many they see, and the seconds left), the report's list ("Seen:"), and the Odinsleep ("sleeping, 64%").

## Odin in Asgard

As long as he sleeps, Odin lies in his chamber; once Hela has fallen he is awake and stands in the throne room. You can talk to him: he tells you of Gungnir and his ravens, and where the vault is. See [Asgardians](Asgardians).

The vault also keeps an empty place for something still to come.

## Odin in the Hero Creator

His six rows are in the [Hero Creator](Hero-Creator) (ids `odin.gungnir`, `odin.odinforce`, `odin.banish`, `odin.ravens`, `odin.command`, `odin.body`; see [Heroes' Abilities](Hero-Abilities#odin-6)). A custom hero fires the Gungnir Blast from his bare hand; his Odinforce shares the one shield with the other heroes' shields; Banish and the Command are world powers. The ready-made *Odin-like* hero starts with Gungnir, the Odinforce and the ravens.

## Numbers you can change

Every damage, cooldown and time of his abilities (the blast, the throw, the shield, the heal, Banish and whether it sends players home, the wave, the ravens, the Command, the Odinsleep) are the `odin.*` options on [Server Configuration](Server-Configuration#odin).
