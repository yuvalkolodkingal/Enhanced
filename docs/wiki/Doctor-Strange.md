# Doctor Strange

Stephen Strange, Master of the Mystic Arts and Sorcerer Supreme (3.6), as in *Doctor Strange*, *Avengers: Infinity War* and *Doctor Strange in the Multiverse of Madness*: spells drawn in orange **mandalas** of sparks, **Sling Ring** portals, the red **Cloak of Levitation**, and the **Eye of Agamotto** with the Time Stone inside. He lashes with a whip of sparks, shields his friends, binds his enemies, steps out of his body as a spirit, opens portals, folds the world into the Mirror Dimension and turns time back.

Hebrew: [עברית](Doctor-Strange-HE)

![Doctor Strange](images/058-doctor-strange.png)

## The quick version

* **How:** with **no power**, hold the **Eye of Agamotto** (right-click and keep holding) for **3 seconds**. The Eye is made from the [Time Stone](Infinity-Stones#the-time-stone-green-sorcerers-only) and 8 gold blocks.
* **R** the Eldritch Whip, **G** flight on the Cloak, **V** (hold) the Shield of the Seraphim, **B** the Crimson Bands, **N** a Sling-Ring Portal, **Y** the Eye: Slow Time. **Sneak** changes each of them: Whip Grapple, Seraphim Dome, Astral Projection, Mirror Dimension, Rewind.
* He is a **sorcerer**: the Book of the Vishanti and the Darkhold answer him, a Hex's Runes hold him, and he may hold and use the Time Stone.

## Becoming Doctor Strange: the Eye of Agamotto

![Doctor Strange and the Eye of Agamotto](images/059-doctor-strange-eye-of-agamotto.png)

1. **Get the Time Stone** from the top floor of a **Sanctum Sanctorum** ([Structures](Structures#the-sanctum-sanctorum)). Only a god, the Scarlet Witch, the Phoenix or a sorcerer can take it from its pedestal.
2. **Craft the Eye of Agamotto:** 8 gold blocks round the Time Stone. (Whoever crafts it must be able to hold the stone; he can then give the Eye to a friend.)
3. **With no power**, hold right-click on the Eye for **3 seconds**. The Eye opens, green rings turn round you, a mandala flashes, and you are **Doctor Strange**: "The Sorcerer Supreme". Everyone is told: "Eilon opened the Eye of Agamotto and became Doctor Strange!"
4. The Eye is **not used up**: it is **yours** now ("The Eye of Steve" on its tooltip). While you are Doctor Strange it makes nobody else Strange ("This Eye already has a Sorcerer Supreme"); other sorcerers can still use its time powers. If you lose your power, the Eye is nobody's again.

You also become Doctor Strange with **Choose a Hero** in the [Hero Creator](Hero-Creator), which gives you an Eye of your own and a Sling Ring, or with `/enhanced power set <player> doctor_strange`.

### The Eye for everyone else

| Who holds the Eye | Right-click | Sneak + right-click |
|---|---|---|
| A player with **no power** | hold 3 seconds: become Doctor Strange | the same |
| **Billy**, **Agatha**, **Loki**, the **Scarlet Witch**, a custom sorcerer | the Time Stone's **Slow Time** | the Time Stone's **Rewind** |
| **Doctor Strange** | Slow Time (as **Y**) | Rewind (as **Sneak + Y**) |
| Anyone else (Thor, the Phoenix, Spider-Man, Thanos...) | "The Eye stays closed. Only a sorcerer can open it." | the same |

So **Billy and Agatha get the Time Stone's powers** through the Eye. A sorcerer can also use the Eye **on a crop, a sapling or a baby animal**, as the Time Stone: it grows (Sneak: a crop grows back). The Eye recharges with the Time Stone: its sweep on the hotbar shows when. Inside a Hex whose Runes hold sorcerers: "The runes hold the Eye shut". Losing the Eye does **not** take Strange's power, only his **Y**.

**His body:** **+6 health** (13 hearts) and **+2 armour**, and his spells cost a fifth less hunger. The Cloak catches falls below 12 blocks.

### Astral Projection

* Your **body** stays where you stood, kneeling, its eyes closed. **You** are the spirit: pale blue and see-through, flying (even without G), **passing through walls**, and invisible to monsters (they lose you).
* The spirit can't hurt anyone, use items, open chests or pick things up.
* Up to **30 seconds**, and up to **64 blocks** from the body: "The cord to your body holds you here". The HUD shows the time and how far you are.
* **You come back** to your body when you press **Sneak + B** again, when the time is up ("Your body calls you back"), or when your body is hurt: then you take **half** of what it took.
* Not in a boss's arena ("Your spirit cannot leave your body in a boss's arena"). Cooldown 15 seconds.

### The Mirror Dimension

A bubble of the world, **14 blocks** round where you stand, for **20 seconds**: the world bends and turns like a kaleidoscope for everyone inside.

* **Nothing in the real world breaks** inside it: explosions hurt but break no blocks, fire goes out, and nobody but you can break or place blocks there. A safe place for a fight in the middle of a town.
* **Creatures you hurt inside can't leave**: the edge pushes them back (players too, while PvP is on). Everyone else may walk in and out.
* Your spells hit **20% harder** inside.
* Not in a boss's arena, on the Witches' Road or in the city of Asgard. Cooldown 60 seconds. *Reduced motion* calms the bending.

## The Sling Ring

A gold ring with an amethyst, for **every sorcerer** (Doctor Strange, Billy, Agatha, Loki, the Scarlet Witch, a custom sorcerer). Doctor Strange gets one when he awakens through Choose a Hero, and anyone can craft it: **4 gold ingots round an amethyst shard** (a plus shape). The Sanctum's library chests sometimes hold one.

* **Sneak + right-click**: **remember this place**. Give it a name, or leave it empty. The ring keeps up to **8 places**, in any world.
* **Right-click**: the ring's screen, with your places. Choose one and a **pair of portals** opens: one in front of you, its twin at the place, **even in another world** (the Nether, the End). Forget a place from the same screen.
* Cooldown 10 seconds ("The ring is gathering its strength"). **Never to or from Asgard** (only the Bifrost goes there; "A sling ring cannot reach the Realm Eternal.") and never the Witches' Road.
* For anyone who is not a sorcerer: "The ring is just a ring for you."

The **N** key needs no ring: that portal is Strange's own, in the same world.

## Looks

Press **H** to switch. See [Looks](Looks).

| Look | Id | What it is |
|---|---|---|
| **Sorcerer Supreme** (default) | `sorcerer_supreme` | *Doctor Strange* and *Infinity War*: a dark blue layered tunic with a wrapped belt, grey-brown leggings and boots, the **red Cloak of Levitation** with its high collar, the Eye on his chest, swept dark hair with grey temples and a goatee. |
| **Multiverse of Madness** | `multiverse` | The same red Cloak with a navy patch on it, and a tighter, tailored tunic. |
| **Defender Strange** | `defender` | Our own take on a Strange of another universe: a tied-back ponytail, a darker tunic and a dark red Cloak. |
| **Your own skin** | `own` | His powers in your own skin. |

**The Cloak** moves by itself: it billows when he flies, waves gently when he stands, and wraps round his shoulders when he falls. **The Eye** hangs on his chest and glows green while time is slowed. He holds his hands up in a spell's mudra while he casts, circles a hand for a portal, throws his arms wide for the dome and kneels for the Astral Projection. In first person you see your hands and the mandalas.

## The HUD

Next to the usual panel: the lash of the whip combo (`lash 2/3`), the Shield's and the Dome's seconds, the spirit's time and distance, the Mirror's and the portals' seconds, and a sixth line, **[Y]**, for the Eye: `no Eye`, `time slowed 7s`, `recharging 40%` or `ready`.

## Against the others

* **Bosses** (the Mad Titan, Ultron, Apocalypse, Hela): the Bands hold a boss only a second, and the Sling-Ring portals never take one. The Shield stops their shots, and their beams end at it.
* **Hydra soldiers** never see the Astral spirit.
* A **Hex's Runes** hold him (he is a sorcerer): no spells, no Eye.
* He doesn't break blocks.

## Doctor Strange in the Hero Creator

His six abilities are in the [Hero Creator](Hero-Creator) (`strange.whip`, `strange.shields`, `strange.bands`, `strange.portal`, `strange.eye`, `strange.body`), and every Sneak variant is a card of its own (`strange.whip_grapple`, `strange.seraphim_dome`, `strange.astral`, `strange.mirror`, `strange.eye_rewind`); see [Heroes' Abilities](Hero-Abilities#doctor-strange-6). His Eye is on **Y**, and works only with an Eye of Agamotto in the inventory. A custom hero flies like him with the flight style *Doctor Strange (the Cloak)*. The ready-made hero is *Doctor Strange-like*.

## Numbers you can change

Every number of his spells and his body is a `doctor_strange.*` option; the Eye's are `eye_of_agamotto.*` and the ring's `sling_ring.*`. See [Server Configuration](Server-Configuration#doctor-strange-the-eye-of-agamotto-and-the-sling-ring). The Eye's Slow Time and Rewind use the Time Stone's numbers (`time_stone.*`).
