# Looks

A **look** is one of a hero's outfits. Each look has its own skin, its own cape and its own colour for the hero's energy and magic. The abilities are the same in every look: a look changes how a hero appears, not what the hero can do.

Vision, Tommy, Billy, Loki and Thor have several looks. Magneto, the Scarlet Witch and Quicksilver have one suit each, which they put on with their helmet, crown or goggles.

## Switching looks

* Press **H** (the *Change Look* key) for the next look. **Sneak + H** goes back to the previous one. The looks cycle round, in the order of the lists below.
* The new look's name appears above your hotbar ("Look: ..."). A hero with only one look is told "This hero has only one look".
* Operators can set a look with a command: `/enhanced look <players> <look>`, or straight when giving a power: `/enhanced power set <players> <power> <look>`. The id of each look is in the lists below. See [Commands](Commands).
* The key can be rebound under *Options, Controls, Enhanced Powers*. See [Controls](Controls).

## Your own skin

Every look list of the newer heroes ends with **Your own skin**: the hero's powers and energy colours, in your own Minecraft skin. (Vision's two looks are both outfits.)
