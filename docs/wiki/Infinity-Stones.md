# The Infinity Stones

The six Infinity Stones of the films (3.6) are real items now: the **Space**, **Mind**, **Reality**, **Power**, **Time** and **Soul** Stones. Each one waits somewhere in the worlds, each has two powers, and only the right hands can hold them. Set them into a crafted **Infinity Gauntlet** to carry them together, turn the **Stone Wheel** to choose one, and with all six, **Snap**.

Hebrew: [עברית](Infinity-Stones-HE)

![The Infinity Stones](images/114-infinity-stones.png)

## The quick version

* **Where:** Reality in [Asgard](Asgard)'s vault; Mind inside **Loki's Scepter** (from a Hydra outpost); Space inside the **Tesseract** (Avengers Tower); Time in the **Sanctum Sanctorum**; Power in **the Orb**, in an **End tunnel**; Soul at the **Vormir Cliff** in the Nether, for a sacrifice ([Vormir](Vormir)): anyone can earn it there (3.6.1); one who may not hold it bare finds it on a pedestal by the pool, held for him.
* **Who:** only the gods (**Thor, Loki, Heimdall, Odin, Hela**), the **Scarlet Witch** and the **Phoenix** may hold a bare stone. A sorcerer may hold the Time Stone, and Vision the Mind Stone. Anyone else can't pick a stone up, and one in his bag **burns** him after 10 seconds.
* **Vessels:** **Loki's Scepter** (Mind), the **Tesseract** (Space) and the **Eye of Agamotto** (Time) give their stone's power to others. The Reality, Power and Soul Stones have **no vessel**: someone who is not a god can't use them at all.
* **Use** = right-click the stone; **Sneak + right-click** = its second power. Hold for the beams and the drain.
* **The Gauntlet:** craft it, click stones into it, **J** picks the next stone, holding **J** opens the **Stone Wheel**. With all six: Sneak and hold right-click for 3 seconds to **Snap**.

## The six stones

| Stone | Colour | Where it is | Use (right-click) | Sneak + use |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Reality Stone** | red | On a pedestal in [Asgard](Asgard)'s vault, under the throne room (after Hela's first defeat) | **Transmute**: the creature you look at turns to stone. While you hold it, shots at you turn into **bubbles** | **Illusion**: you vanish and a red copy of you walks away |
| **Mind Stone** | yellow | Inside **Loki's Scepter**: craft the Scepter alone | Hold: the **Mind Beam** (Vision's beam), which confuses | **Mind Command**: the creature you look at fights for you |
| **Time Stone** | green | On a pedestal at the top of a **Sanctum Sanctorum** | **Slow Time** round you (on/off) | **Rewind**: back to where you were 5 seconds ago |
| **Power Stone** | purple | Inside **the Orb**, in the vault chest of an **End tunnel** | Hold: the **Purple Beam**, with explosions where it hits | The **Power Bolt** (Thanos's blast) |
| **Space Stone** | blue | Inside the **Tesseract**, on the roof of **Avengers Tower**: craft the Tesseract alone | **Portal Step**: step through a portal to where you look | The **Realm Picker**: the Overworld, the Nether, the End or Asgard |
| **Soul Stone** | orange | The **Vormir Cliff** in the Nether, given for a sacrifice ([Vormir](Vormir)) | Hold: the **Soul Drain** (Thanos's) | **Soul Sight**: every soul near you glows through walls |

The four places, and how to find them with `/locate`, are on [Structures](Structures#the-stone-sites-36).

## Who may hold a stone

The stones are too strong for most hands. The **Bearers** may hold every bare stone: the gods **Thor**, **Loki**, **Heimdall**, **Odin** and **Hela**, the **Scarlet Witch** and the **Phoenix** (and a [custom hero](Hero-Creator) with one of their "god's" abilities). Two more may hold one stone each:

* **A sorcerer** may hold the **Time Stone**: Billy, Agatha, Doctor Strange and a custom sorcerer.
* **Vision** may hold the **Mind Stone**: it is his own.

The Time Stone **answers only a sorcerer**. Thor, Heimdall, Odin, Hela and the Phoenix may hold it, but it stays quiet in their hand: "You are no sorcerer: the Time Stone will not answer you."

| Who | May hold | May use |
|---|---|---|
| **Loki**, the **Scarlet Witch** | all six | all six |
| **Thor**, **Heimdall**, **Odin**, **Hela**, the **Phoenix** | all six | all but the Time Stone |
| **Billy**, **Agatha**, **Doctor Strange** | the Time Stone | the Time Stone |
| **Vision** | the Mind Stone | the Mind Stone |
| **Everyone else** (Magneto, Spider-Man, Thanos, the Hulk, no power...) | none | none, but the vessels work for them |
| A player in **Creative** | all six | all but the Time Stone (unless a sorcerer) |

**Non-gods can't use the Reality, Power or Soul Stone at all.** These three have no vessel. Only a Bearer, or one who wields a filled Gauntlet, ever uses them.

### When a stone is too strong for you

* **On the ground** a stone **slips away** from someone who may not hold it: a spark, a little hop, and "The Power Stone slips away from your hand."
* **In your inventory** (from a chest, a crafting table, a friend's hand) it starts to **burn**: a red countdown on the screen, "The Power Stone burns! 7…", and a tick every second. After **10 seconds** (three times faster in your hand) it **zaps** you for 2 damage (it never kills you), throws you back a little, and **jumps out** in front of you: "The Power Stone is too strong for you!"
* **Safe places:** a chest, a barrel, a shulker box, the ender chest, the **Gauntlet** and the **Orb** hold stones safely, for anyone. A bundle in your inventory does not: the stone burns through it.
* A Bearer may **give** a stone to anyone. Then it is their 10 seconds.

## The vessels

![The Scepter and the Tesseract](images/115-artifacts-scepter-tesseract.png)

The vessels let other heroes, and players with no power, use three of the stones. They never burn anyone.

| Vessel | Stone | Who can use it | How |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Loki's Scepter** | Mind | **Anyone** | **Sneak + hold right-click**, with nothing in your other hand: the **Mind Beam**. (Its other uses are as before: see [Loki](Loki).) |
| **The Tesseract** | Space | **Anyone** | **Sneak + right-click**: the **Realm Picker**. (Its plain use is as before.) |
| **The Eye of Agamotto** | Time | **A sorcerer** (Billy, Agatha, Loki, the Scarlet Witch, Doctor Strange) | Right-click: **Slow Time**; Sneak + right-click: **Rewind**. Held for 3 seconds by a player with **no power**, it makes him [Doctor Strange](Doctor-Strange#becoming-doctor-strange-the-eye-of-agamotto). Anyone else: "The Eye stays closed. Only a sorcerer can open it." |

**Taking the stone out and putting it back** (crafting table):

| Craft | Gives |
|---|---|
| Loki's Scepter alone | the **Mind Stone** and an **Empty Scepter** (just a weapon now) |
| An Empty Scepter and the Mind Stone | Loki's Scepter again |
| The Tesseract alone | the **Space Stone** and an **Empty Tesseract** (a clear, hollow cube) |
| An Empty Tesseract and the Space Stone | the Tesseract again |
| 8 gold blocks round the **Time Stone** | the **Eye of Agamotto** |
| The Eye of Agamotto alone | the Time Stone (the gold is lost) |

Someone has to hold the Time Stone to craft the Eye, so a god, the Scarlet Witch, the Phoenix or a sorcerer makes it, and can give it to a friend with no power. The Eye shares the Time Stone's cooldown: its sweep on the hotbar shows when it is ready again.

## What each stone does

![The Space Stone in the inventory](images/113-ultron-inventory-space-stone.png)

Every stone has its own cooldown, shared by its two uses, and costs a little hunger (with an empty food bar it does nothing). A hand held by Billy's Freeze, a body turned to stone, the Time Lock or Mjolnir's pin can't use a stone ("The stones will not answer while you are held"). A switch that is on (Slow Time) can always be turned off. Only one beam or drain at a time.

### The Reality Stone (red)

* **Reality Bubbles** (while you hold it bare, or it is the Gauntlet's chosen stone): arrows, tridents, bolts and other shots that fly at you within 4 blocks **turn into bubbles**, as round Thanos in *Infinity War*. Each bubble costs a little hunger. In a boss's arena at most two a second, and they cost more. (Thor's thrown Mjolnir and the Mad Titan's own weapons are too real to change.)
* **Transmute** (use): the creature you look at (up to 20 blocks) **turns to stone** for 3 seconds. Bosses shake it off. Cooldown 12 seconds.
* **Illusion** (Sneak + use): you turn invisible for 6 seconds, and a **red copy** of you walks away; the monsters chase it. Cooldown 20 seconds.

### The Mind Stone (yellow)

* **Mind Beam** (hold): Vision's yellow beam (up to 32 blocks), **4 damage** twice a second, and it **confuses** what it hits. Up to 6 seconds, then 3 seconds of rest (1 second if you let go early).
* **Mind Command** (Sneak + use): the creature you look at (up to 16 blocks) **fights for you** for 20 seconds: it follows you, attacks what you attack and what attacks you, and never turns on you; its eyes flash blue. A [Hydra soldier](Hydra-Soldiers) becomes a traitor to Hydra. A **player** (with PvP on) is only dizzy, slow and weak for 6 seconds. Bosses, and minds that are too strong, resist: "This mind is too strong for the stone." Cooldown 30 seconds.

### The Time Stone (green; sorcerers only)

* **Slow Time** (use, on/off): "Time slows around you." Every creature within 12 blocks moves **three times slower** (bosses half as much); green rings turn round your wrist. Up to 10 seconds, then 10 seconds of rest. It stops when the stone leaves you.
* **Rewind** (Sneak + use): you go back to where you were **5 seconds** ago, with that health if it was higher, as Thanos's Rewind. Cooldown 30 seconds.
* **On a crop or a sapling** (use while you look at it): it **grows**; Sneak on a crop: it grows **back**. On a baby animal: it grows up. Every half second.
* A sorcerer held by a Hex's Runes can't use it: "The Hex Runes hold the Time Stone still."

### The Power Stone (purple)

* **Purple Beam** (hold): a thick purple beam (up to 32 blocks), **4 damage** twice a second, and an **explosion** where it hits every half second (6 damage round it). It kicks back. After **3 seconds** it **overheats**: "The Power Stone burns too hot! Let it cool." (7 seconds; 3 if you let go first). It breaks blocks **only** if the server turns on `power_stone.terrainDamage` and the game rule `mobGriefing` is on, and never in a protected place.
* **Power Bolt** (Sneak + use): Thanos's violet blast (up to 28 blocks), **12** damage to the creature it hits and 6 round it. Cooldown 3.5 seconds.

### The Space Stone (blue)

* **Portal Step** (use): a blue portal, and you step to the block you look at (up to 64 blocks) or right behind the creature you look at. Cooldown 3 seconds.
* **Realm Picker** (Sneak + use; also the Tesseract's Sneak + use): a screen of four windows. Click one, or press **1** to **4**:
  * **The Overworld**: your bed or respawn anchor, else the world's spawn.
  * **The Nether**: where you are, divided by 8, on safe ground (from the End or Asgard, where you last were in the Nether).
  * **The End**: the main island, outside the exit portal.
  * **Asgard**: Heimdall's Observatory. The Space Stone is **the one way into Asgard other than the Bifrost** (a server can turn it off with `space_stone.reachesAsgard`).
  
  You go alone, land with 2 seconds of Resistance, and the stone rests 30 seconds. A grey window says why it can't be chosen (you are there already, Asgard is closed...). Not in a boss's arena, on the Witches' Road, inside a Hex that holds you, or while you are pinned.

### The Soul Stone (orange)

* **Soul Drain** (hold): Thanos's orange chain to the creature you look at (up to 16 blocks): **6 damage a second**, and you get half of it back as health (bosses lose half as much). 2 seconds of rest when it ends.
* **Soul Sight** (Sneak + use): every living soul within 32 blocks **glows** through walls for 8 seconds. Cooldown 20 seconds.

### The HUD

While you carry stones, little gems sit **to the right of the hotbar**, one per stone, each with its cooldown sweep. Lines under them show the Reality Stone's bubbles ("×3 bubbles"), Slow Time and the Soul Sight, and a heat bar for the two beams. The burning countdown is red.

## The Infinity Gauntlet

There are two kinds of Infinity Gauntlet now:

* **Thanos's Gauntlet** (the Titan's): the reward of the [Thanos](Thanos#the-infinity-gauntlet) fight, and every Gauntlet from an older world. It is unchanged: hold it for 3 seconds with no power and you become Thanos. Its five glowing stones are the Titan's own; they can't be taken out ("These are the Titan's own stones.").
* **The Infinity Gauntlet**, crafted: **six empty sockets** for real stones.

**Crafting:** gold blocks all round, a **nether star** in the middle and a **netherite ingot** at the top middle (`G N G / G C G / G G G`). A server can switch the recipe off (`gauntlet.craftable`).

**Setting the stones:**

* **In:** in your inventory, pick up a stone and **right-click it onto the Gauntlet** (or the Gauntlet onto the stone), as with a bundle. Anyone may put a stone in: the Gauntlet holds it safely.
* **Out:** **right-click the Gauntlet** in a slot with nothing on the cursor: the **chosen stone** comes out. Only someone who may hold that stone can take it out ("The stones will not leave the Gauntlet for you.").
* The sockets light up on the item and on your hand, and everyone sees your Gauntlet on your arm.

**Wielding it:** holding a Gauntlet **with stones in it** in either hand works only for a **Bearer**, the hero **Thanos** and the **Hulk** (as a Hulk, not as Bruce Banner). Anyone else: "The Gauntlet is too heavy for your hand." (it doesn't burn).

**Using it:** right-click uses the **chosen stone**, and Sneak + right-click its second power, exactly as the bare stone. The Time Stone still answers only a sorcerer. The hero Thanos has **his five stones on his keys** as always; with the **Mind Stone** in a Gauntlet he also has its beam and its Command (the sixth stone he never had; a server can switch that off with `gauntlet.mindForThanos`).

### The Stone Wheel (J)

* **Tap J**: the **next stone** of the Gauntlet in your hand: "Stone: Space Stone".
* **Hold J**: the **Stone Wheel** opens round the crosshair, with the Gauntlet's stones. Let go on one, or click it, to choose it.

J is also Spider-Man's Web Assist key; each answers only for its own player. You can change it in *Options, Controls, Enhanced Powers* ("Stone Wheel (Infinity Gauntlet)").

### The Snap

With **all six stones** in a crafted Gauntlet, held by someone who may wield it:

1. **Sneak and hold right-click** for **3 seconds**. The fingers close and the six stones light one after the other. (A quick tap instead uses the chosen stone's Sneak power.)
2. **Snap!** A white flash. **Half of the hostile monsters** within 64 blocks **turn to dust**: exactly half, rounded down. They drop nothing. "Snap! 5 turned to dust."
3. **Never** players, pets, villagers, animals, named or tamed creatures, and never a boss or a boss's helpers.
4. **The cost:** the Snap leaves its wielder at **2 health** with **Weakness II** for 30 seconds. Thanos pays nothing; the [Hulk](Hulk#the-gauntlet-and-the-snap)'s arm burns instead. In Creative it costs nothing.
5. **One Snap a day:** the Gauntlet can snap again only after one Minecraft day (20 minutes). The HUD shows "Snap ready: Sneak + hold use", or "Snap again in 19:59".

Not in a boss's arena ("Not here."), on the Witches' Road, or in Asgard. Your first Snap gives the advancement **Half of Everything**.

## How many stones: the ledger

The four stones found in places (Reality, Time, Power and Soul) are counted. A server chooses how (`stones.uniqueness`):

* **Once per player** (`PLAYER`, the default): every player may take each of them **once**. Two brothers can each have a full set; a pedestal still shows its stone to the next player. Try a second time and: "You have already taken the Time Stone from a place like this."
* **Once per world** (`WORLD`): one of each in the whole world. When someone has taken it, every other place is empty: the pedestal is bare, the chest holds a note, "The Time Stone is gone."
* **Unlimited** (`UNLIMITED`): every place gives its stone every time (for Creative servers).

The Soul Stone has its own choice, `soul_stone.onePer`, with the same three answers. A Soul Stone earned by one who may not hold it bare (3.6.1) counts as his from the moment it appears on the pedestal by the pool: it is stamped with his name when it is taken, whoever takes it (he, or a Bearer for him). The Mind and Space Stones are not counted: they are as rare as Loki's Scepter and the Tesseract.

**A lost stone comes back** (`stones.returnLost`): a stone that falls into the void, is destroyed by `/kill`, or is lost with a sacrificed player's things can be taken again (in player mode by the one who lost it; in world mode its places fill again).

## The stones in the Hero Creator

A [custom hero](Hero-Creator#the-stone-rows-36) may put a stone on a key: the six **stone rows** (`stone.reality`, `stone.mind`, `stone.time`, `stone.power`, `stone.space`, `stone.soul`). A stone row works **only while your hero carries that stone** and may use it (bare, in a Gauntlet he may wield, or in its vessel). See [Heroes' Abilities](Hero-Abilities#the-infinity-stones-36).

## The Mad Titan

The [Thanos boss](Thanos) is unchanged: his stones are phases of his fight, not items, and he still gives Thanos's Gauntlet. But from his second stone on, he goes first for a fighter who **carries a real stone**: "You carry something that is mine."

## Numbers you can change

Who may hold the stones (`stones.customBearers`, `visionHoldsMind`, `sorcerersHoldTime`), the burn (`graceSeconds`, `handGraceFactor`, `rejectDamage`), the ledger (`uniqueness`, `returnLost`), the Gauntlet and the Snap (`[gauntlet]`), and every number of every stone (`[reality_stone]`, `[mind_stone]`, `[time_stone]`, `[power_stone]`, `[space_stone]`, and the Soul Stone's in `[soul_stone]`) are on [Server Configuration](Server-Configuration#the-infinity-stones-36).
