# Falcon

Sam Wilson (3.8), the Falcon of *The Winter Soldier* and *Endgame*: a soldier whose power is his **Exo-Wings**. With them on his back he flies, glides like an elytra, **dives** onto his foes from the sky, shoots up like a rocket, wraps his wings round himself as a **shield**, and sends **Redwing**, his little drone, to find the monsters and zap them. On his wrists he has **energy darts** and a small **rocket**.

Hebrew: [עברית](Falcon-HE)

## The quick version

* **How:** take the **Falcon Exo-Wings** from the top of the [Avengers Tower](Structures#avengers-tower), carry them, then use a **[Super Soldier Serum](Super-Soldier-Serum)** and choose **Falcon**. **Wear the wings on your chest.**
* **R** Wrist Blasters, **G** Flight, **V** Dive, **B** Wing Shield (hold), **N** Redwing. **Sneak** changes each of them: Wrist Rocket, Glide, Boost Climb, Wing Slash, Redwing Strike.
* **Everything but the wrist weapons needs the wings on your chest**: "Put on your Exo-Wings!"
* With the wings on, he takes **no fall damage**, and flying into a wall doesn't hurt.

## Becoming Falcon

1. Climb the [Avengers Tower](Structures#avengers-tower) to the **observation deck**, its highest room (the door says **OBSERVATION deck**). Between two end rods stands a glass **Tech Case** with the wings turning inside and the sign "FALCON / Exo-Wings". **Right-click it**: the **Falcon Exo-Wings** are yours. One pair for each player, so you and your brother both get yours.
2. Take a [Super Soldier Serum](Super-Soldier-Serum) from the tower's lab. With the wings **anywhere in your inventory**, use the serum: the **Falcon** card is open (without the wings it is locked). Choose it: you are Falcon ("Wings out!").
3. **Put the wings on your chest slot**, and fly.

Or **Choose a Hero** in the [Hero Creator](Hero-Creator) (it gives a pair, on your chest if it is free), or `/enhanced power set <player> falcon` (and a look: `endgame`, `winter_soldier` or `own`).

**Losing it:** the Mutant Cure, `/enhanced power set <player> none`, another serum choice, an [Arc Reactor](Iron-Man#becoming-iron-man-the-arc-reactors), [Eternity's](Eternity) Love, being sacrificed at Vormir, or choosing another hero. The wings stay an item: on anyone else's back they are a chest plate with folded wings.

## The Exo-Wings

A back pack with two jet nozzles and two wings of **segmented metal feathers**, five panels a wing. They are worn on the **chest slot** and give **6 armour** (an iron chestplate's, and a little toughness). They can't be crafted. They wear like an elytra while he glides and when he is hit; mend them with **iron ingots on an anvil** ("Your Exo-Wings are broken. Mend them with iron on an anvil."). Broken wings don't fly.

The wings move: **folded** on his back on the ground, **spread** and beating gently while he hovers, **wide and flat** when he glides, **swept back** for the dive, **wrapped round the front** for the shield and **flung out** for the slash. The nozzles flame while he flies. In first person you see the tips of the wings at the edges of your screen while you fly.

On a player who is **not Falcon**, they are just a chest plate with folded wings: no flight.

## Flight and the glide

* **G: Flight** (or double-tap Space): with the wings on, he hovers and flies (speed 0.10), the wings spread and the nozzles roar.
* **Sneak + G: Glide** (on/off): the wings are his **elytra**: wide and flat, he glides like an elytra flyer (jumping in the air starts it too, and firework rockets push him). When he **looks up**, the nozzles give him a small boost every 2 seconds. **Sneak + G** again: back to hovering. From the ground, he hops and spreads his wings at the top.
* **No fall damage** while he wears working wings, and gliding into a wall doesn't hurt him.

## Abilities

| # | Key | Ability | How it works |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **R** | Wrist Blasters | A pair of bright red **energy darts**, left then right: **4** each, 40 blocks, aimed at the crosshair. They are energy, not bullets: they never run out. A pair every 6 ticks. Works without the wings. **Sneak + R: Wrist Rocket**: one small rocket: **8** and a small boom (it never breaks blocks). Then **R** waits 4 seconds. |
| 2 | **G** | Flight | See above. **Sneak + G: Glide**. |
| 3 | **V** | Dive | Only **in the air, 4 blocks or more above the ground** ("Dive from the air!"): he folds his wings and **dives** at the creature he looks at (up to 32 blocks; he follows it) or at the spot: on impact **10** to it and **3** to the creatures right next to it, knocked back; no harm to him, and he bounces back up and hovers. Cooldown 5 seconds. **Sneak + V: Boost Climb**: the nozzles roar and he shoots **24 blocks straight up** in a second (from the ground too), and hovers at the top (a ceiling stops him). Cooldown 3 seconds. |
| 4 | **B** (hold) | Wing Shield | The wings **wrap in front of him** (up to 5 seconds): arrows and shots from the front **bounce back** (and become his), blows from the front lose **70%**, explosions from the front **50%**. In the air he hovers still behind it; on the ground he walks slowly. Then **B** waits 3 seconds. **Sneak + B: Wing Slash**: he spins with his wings out: **7** to every creature within 3 blocks, knocked back. Cooldown 4 seconds. |
| 5 | **N** | Redwing | His drone **launches** from the wing pack: a small red-and-black drone with a red eye that flies over his right shoulder. It **marks every monster within 32 blocks** (they **glow red** through walls, for him alone) and **zaps** the nearest marked one it can see within 16 blocks once a second (**2**). It stays out **1 minute**, or until **N** again (it flies back into the pack), or until the wings come off. Redwing has **12 health**: if it is shot down, it falls back into the pack with sparks, and "Redwing needs repairs." for 30 seconds. **Sneak + N: Redwing Strike**: Redwing (launched first if it is in) **rams** the creature he looks at, up to 32 blocks: **8** and Slowness II for 3 seconds, then flies back to his shoulder. Then **N** waits 10 seconds for the next strike. |

**Without the wings on your chest**, every wing move (**G**, **V**, **B** and their Sneak moves) and Redwing (**N**: it lives in the wing pack) say "Put on your Exo-Wings!". Only the wrist weapons (**R**, **Sneak + R**) work without them.

**The body:** **24 health** (12 hearts) and 10% faster. With PvP off, his abilities don't hurt players.

## The HUD

Next to the usual panel: the wings ("Wings: 92%", "not on" or "broken") and "Gliding", the Wing Shield's seconds, and Redwing's time and health, or "Redwing repairs 21".

## Falcon in the Hero Creator

His abilities are in the [Hero Creator](Hero-Creator) (`falcon.blasters`, `falcon.dive`, `falcon.wing_shield`, `falcon.redwing`, `falcon.body`), and his four Sneak variants are cards of their own (`falcon.wrist_rocket`, `falcon.boost_climb`, `falcon.wing_slash`, `falcon.redwing_strike`). A custom hero with his cards **needs no wings item**: Falcon's wings are drawn on him, and his wing moves work. He flies with the **Flight** power (*Falcon (the wings)* is a flight style); to glide like an elytra he needs **Falcon's Body** and the Exo-Wings on his chest. The **Wing Shield** is a shield (it can't go with another hero's shield). See [Heroes' Abilities](Hero-Abilities#falcon-5). The ready-made hero is *Falcon-like*, and *Falcon* is a run style.

## The advancement

**Wings out** ("Get the Falcon Exo-Wings"), in the **Avengers** tab.

Every number of his body, the wings, the darts, the dive, the shield and Redwing is a `falcon.*` option. See [Server Configuration](Server-Configuration#falcon).
