# The Multiverse

Eilon's idea (3.19): the world is no longer one universe. Every place belongs to a **universe**, known by its number as in the comics, and there are **as many universes as you like**, as in the infinite dimensions of Minecraft's April Fools update and in *What If...?*

Hebrew: [עברית](Multiverse-HE)

## The universes

| Universe | What it is |
|---|---|
| **Earth-616**, the main universe | Where a new world starts (the Overworld; the Nether, the End and the mod's realms belong to it). Every structure of the game and of the mod is here, **except the Mutant School**. |
| **Earth-10005**, the X-Men's universe | A normal world of large biomes with the game's own structures and the **Mutant School**, the only universe that has it now (with its lab: Wolverine, Deadpool, Cerebro). |
| **Earth-2149**, the zombie universe | The same land as 616, overrun. **Zombies with helmets** walk by day and by night (the sun does not burn them), **four times as many** as a normal night, and **every village is an abandoned village**. Every other structure of the game is there. |
| **What If... the Gamma Bomb Went Off?** | Every creature is a Hulk of its kind (below). |
| **What If... Hydra Won?** | Hydra's grey city, its people, and its two rulers (below). |
| **What If... Ultron Won?** | A dead world of ruins, bones and Ultron's Sentries (below). |
| **Random universes** | As many as you like, made from a name or a number as in Minecraft's 20w14&infin; (below). |

Outside 616 there are none of the mod's structures, so none of the Infinity Stones' sites: see [the stones](#the-infinity-stones-of-the-other-universes).

## Getting there

Every way opens the **Multiverse screen**: seven windows, Earth-616, Earth-10005, Earth-2149, the Gamma Bomb, Hydra Won, Ultron Won and a random universe. Click one, or press 1 to 7, or **type any name in the box under the windows and press Enter**: you go to the universe that name makes.

* **The Darkhold's Dream Walking**: hold the Darkhold (as one who may read it), look straight down at your feet, and **Sneak + use**. You fall asleep and wake in the other universe.
* **The Sling Ring of a cosmic hero**: as **Billy the Demiurge** or **Cosmic Spider-Man** (the Ultimate versions), hold the Sling Ring, look straight down at your feet, and **Sneak + use**: a pair of portals opens, here and in the other universe, for anyone to cross while it lasts.
* **Reed Richards' Multiverse Bridge** (below).
* Operators: `/enhanced multiverse go <universe>`.

In 616, 10005 and 2149 you land **at the same place** you left (they are the same Earth); coming from far away, where you last were in that universe, or at the spawn (in the two cities, on a street near the Citadel). In a random universe you land in the middle of its land (in a cave world, on its floor).

The way home: any of the ways above, the bridge's portals while they stand, or a bed: dying with no bed in that universe brings you back to 616's spawn. A Nether portal leads to 616's Nether, and back to 616.

## Random universes (as in 20w14&infin;)

The seventh window is a new random universe each time. To go to **one universe in particular**, **type its name** on the screen (or rename an item at an anvil and hold it): **a number** names that universe ("48213"), and **any words** make a universe of their own, always the same one ("Captain Carter", "my base"), as the books did in Minecraft's April Fools update 20w14&infin;. "616", "10005" and "2149" name those universes.

The name is the seed of everything in its universe:

* **The shape of the land**: the Overworld's, **amplified** mountains, **floating islands**, or **one great cave**.
* **What the land is made of**: the stone may be terracotta, end stone, quartz, prismarine, wool, glowstone, sponge... and the grass may be mycelium, nylium, snow, sand or wool.
* **What fills the seas**: water, lava, ice, slime, honey, magma or glass.
* **The biomes**: the game's own, **all one biome**, or **every biome turned into another** (deserts where forests were...).
* **The colour of the sky's haze and the fog.**
* **The creatures**: in many universes some kinds come as other kinds (the cows may be pandas, the zombies blazes).

Its title on the screen says what it is best known for: "What If... the World Were Made of Clay?".

(The random universes share four dimensions, one per shape of land (`enhanced:what_if`, `what_if_amplified`, `what_if_islands`, `what_if_caves`), each cut into squares 100,000 blocks wide: each square is a universe. `/enhanced multiverse` says which one you are in.)

## What If... the Gamma Bomb Went Off?

Bruce Banner's gamma bomb went off over the whole world. **Every creature came out of the green light a Hulk of its kind**: half again to more than twice its size, four times as tough, two and a half times as strong, faster, thick-skinned and hard to knock back. They glow with green gamma sparks, and the air itself is green. Players stay as they are.

## What If... Hydra Won?

Hydra rules a grey industrial city: blocks of flats with Hydra's red stripes, factories with smoking chimneys, small fenced **Hydra compounds** with a bunker and a red flag, Hydra skull statues in the squares, and the smog over everything. In the middle (the city block at 0, 0) stands **the Citadel**, Hydra's black tower.

* **The people** walk the streets. Speak to one (use) and they tell you how they suffer. Hydra's loudspeakers repeat their slogans.
* **Hydra soldiers** patrol round you and fight you.
* **The rulers**: come near the Citadel and they come out of it, each with a boss bar:
  * **Red Skull** (Johann Schmidt, who took Erskine's first serum): his Luger, a blast of the **Tesseract's** blue power every few seconds, and at half health he calls his men ("Cut off one head, two more shall take its place!").
  * **Captain Hydra** (Steve Rogers, whose past Kobik, the living Cosmic Cube, rewrote so he had always served Hydra): hand to hand, he leaps at you, his shield stops shots from the front, and he rallies the soldiers near him.
* **Become them**: Red Skull drops **Schmidt's Serum** and Captain Hydra a **Shard of Kobik**. Using one makes you that character (Captain America's powers, in his look); Change Look keeps you in it, and only becoming another hero takes you out of it.
* **The liberation**: when both have fallen, **every Hydra soldier is gone**, the people are happy (and tell you so), the smog lifts, and **the city turns back into an ordinary one**, chunk by chunk around you: the red becomes white, the iron bars and fires go, the yards become grass.

## What If... Ultron Won?

As in the series: Ultron took the six Infinity Stones and ended all life. The cities lie in ruins, broken towers with holes blown through their walls, cracked streets with rubble and craters, cobwebs, and **the bones of the people** where they fell. Nothing lives here; no creature comes to be. **Fleets of Ultron's Sentries** fly over the ruins and hunt whoever is left, and now and then you hear Ultron's voice.

## Reed Richards' Multiverse Bridge

```
N N N
N   N
N N N

N = Block of Netherite (eight of them, nothing in the middle)
```

1. **Set the core down.** It raises a great upright **ring**, 11 blocks wide and high, round the place above it, across the way you looked. (Make room first: blocks in the way are left, and a ring with more than two missing blocks will not work.)
2. **Give it power**: a **redstone signal** on the core (a lever, a redstone block beside it).
3. **Stand in the middle** of the ring, on the core, look down and **use the core**: the Multiverse screen opens. A renamed item in your hand tunes it to the universe its name names.
4. The ring **gathers its energy** for three seconds, filling with blue light. Stay in the middle: then it sends you, **and everyone standing in the ring**, and holds the way open as a pair of portals for a while, so the others can follow and you can come back.

Breaking the core takes the ring down; the core drops itself.

## The Infinity Stones of the other universes

The other universes have no stone sites, so their stones lie in **chests**: a chest of the game's loot (in a village, a dungeon, a temple, a mineshaft...) opened there for the first time sometimes holds **a stone of that universe that nobody has found yet**.

* **Each universe has one of each stone.** Copies may wait in several chests, but the **first one a player takes is the stone**: every other copy of it **fades away** as soon as anyone touches it.
* **A stone works only in the universe it came from.** Taken to another universe it is just a pretty rock (the same for a stone in a Gauntlet). 616's own stones work only in 616.
* `/enhanced multiverse reset_stones` (operators) lets every stone of the other universes be found again.
