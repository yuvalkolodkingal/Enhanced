# Controls

Every hero has five abilities. They are bound to the keys **R, G, V, B, N**, in that order: ability 1 is **R**, ability 2 is **G**, ability 3 is **V**, ability 4 is **B** and ability 5 is **N**. The same keys work for every hero, and each key triggers the ability with the same number in that hero's list. Keys work only when you are a hero, no screen is open and you are not a Spectator.

**Press, hold, toggle.** *Press* abilities fire once per key press. *Hold* abilities (the Chaos Beam, the Shrapnel Storm, the Chaos Shield, Billy's Hex Beam and Hex Barrier, the Mind Stone Beam, Thor's Lightning Beam, the Phoenix Burst, Spider-Man's Web Swing, Thanos's Soul Drain, Ultron's Repulsor Fire, Apocalypse's Shield, Agatha's Purple Beam and Witch's Shield, Cyclops's Optic Blast) work for as long as you keep the key down (the Chaos Shield never runs out in 3.2, unless a server turns that off; in 3.3 Billy's Hex Barrier has no time limit either, and only drops when it shatters; Agatha's shield lasts 10 seconds at most). *Toggle* abilities switch on with one press and off with the next.

The abilities, their cooldowns and costs are explained on each hero's page. The Scarlet Witch and Billy also have **Y**, for [Build Magic](Build-Magic). The two [books of the sorcerers](#the-sorcerers-books-and-the-road) are used, not bound to a key. A [custom hero](Hero-Creator) has the same five keys, with the powers you chose on them; since 3.4 each key also has a **Sneak +** box, a hero's ability brings its Sneak variant with it (Thor's Mjolnir Throw on **R** puts Shatter Mjolnir on **Sneak + R**), and a custom hero with Billy's Build Magic uses **Y** for it. Sneak + a key whose Sneak box is empty does what the key does. See [Hero Creator](Hero-Creator#6-put-them-on-the-keys-plain-or-sneak).

## Change Look: H

**H** changes your look, if your hero has several (**Sneak + H** goes back). It is bound as *Change Look* and works for Vision, Tommy, Billy, Loki, Thor, the Phoenix, Spider-Man, Thanos, Ultron, Apocalypse, Agatha, Cyclops and Professor X (for him it switches between the young and the old Charles). Magneto, the Scarlet Witch and Quicksilver have one suit each, and the key tells you "This hero has only one look". The looks of every hero are on [Looks](Looks).

## Vision, That One!: Z

The Scarlet Witch has one more key. **Z** is her order to the Vision her Hex raises (see [Scarlet Witch](Scarlet-Witch#the-vision-of-the-hex)). It works only while she has a Hex:

* **Look at a creature and press Z**: Vision goes for that one.
* **Look at nothing and press Z**: he comes back to your side.
* **Sneak + Z**: he holds his post where he is, or follows you again. The Hex Control screen (**N**) has the same choice as a button.

## Build Magic: Y

The Scarlet Witch and Billy have one more key: **Y**, for [Build Magic](Build-Magic). It is the sixth line of your hero panel (`[Y] Build Magic  OFF`). It does nothing for any other hero.

* **Tap Y**: build mode on or off.
* **Hold Y** (a third of a second): the **Spell Wheel**, a ring of eleven pictures. Out of build mode it turns build mode on first.

In the wheel (the game does not pause):

| Input | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **Mouse onto a picture, then let go of Y** | Choose that spell; the wheel closes. |
| **Let go in the middle** | The wheel stays open for clicking. |
| **Click a picture** | Choose that spell and stay open (to pick a style); click the chosen one to close. |
| **1** to **9**, **0** | Choose the first nine spells and the tenth (Land); the wheel closes (also while Y is still held). Grow, the eleventh, has no number. |
| **Mouse wheel** | Step from spell to spell (down is clockwise). |
| **Esc** | Close the wheel. (A tap of **Y** closes it too, and build mode stays on.) |

The wheel also has the style row, **Mix** (the dice), your nine hotbar slots, **Undo** and **Redo** (and, for the Scarlet Witch, **Instant** under them), the door that starts or stops building, and **Blocks**: a page of block pictures with a search box and the last 16 you used. See [Build Magic](Build-Magic#the-spell-wheel).

In build mode the mouse works like this (the strip at the bottom right always tells you what the buttons do right now):

| Input | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **Left click** | *Where*: the first and second corner of a box. Double click: the whole tree or house. Click the sky: let go (or a corner in the air). A left click never breaks a block. |
| **Right click** | *Do it*: cast what the ghost shows. Hold it for a big spell (one second for more than 64 blocks removed or replaced, two for a million) and for Paint, Land and Grow. With nothing ready it is plain Minecraft. |
| **Scroll** | The main number: push the side of the box you look at, size, copies, layers. **Ctrl + scroll**: 8 at a time. |
| **Alt + scroll** | Which kind: style, shape, turn, gap. |
| **Middle click** | Take that exact block as your material. |

**The Ctrl shortcuts** (their names in the Controls screen start with "Build Magic"):

| Keys | Does | Works |
|---|---|---|
| **Ctrl + Z** / **Ctrl + Y** | Undo / redo | in build mode **and out of it** (for the Scarlet Witch and Billy only; for every other hero Ctrl + Z is what it was) |
| **Ctrl + I** | **Instant** on or off: spells, undos and redos appear all at once (a gold badge "⚡ Instant") | in build mode, for the Scarlet Witch only (Billy is told it is her magic alone) |
| **Ctrl + R** / **Ctrl + F** | Turn a ghost a quarter / flip it | in build mode |
| **Ctrl + C** | Copy the box (choose Copy) | in build mode |
| **Ctrl + X** | Pick the box up (choose Move) | in build mode |
| **Ctrl + V** | Bring the pocket's ghost back | in build mode |
| **Delete** | Vanish what is in the box | in build mode |
| **Left Alt** (held) | The second knob, with the scroll wheel | in build mode |

**No Sneak is used in build mode**, and every key of your hero keeps its power (fly with **G**, fight with **R** and **B**, Sneak + **V** takes a block). Plain **Z** is still Wanda's order to her Vision.

Things you may notice:

* The game's Controls screen marks these Ctrl keys, and the keys they share (Sprint, Swap Item With Offhand, the toolbar keys, and **R**, **V**, **Z**, **Y** of the mod), in **red**, with the note "This key is also used for...". That is only the screen being careful: each key still goes to the right place.
* **Left Control is still Sprint**: holding Ctrl while you walk forward sprints, as in vanilla Minecraft.
* **Xaero's Minimap** also uses **Y**. Rebind one of the two.

(3.3: a player with **no power** who **holds use on the Darkhold for 3 seconds** becomes [Agatha](Agatha).)

| Darkhold | (nothing to press) | While you hold it, magic used on you poisons the one who used it. |
| Darkhold, with no power | **Hold use** for 3 seconds | You become [Agatha](Agatha). |

## Thor's beam, shatter and pin, step by step

**The lightning beam (hold V)**

1. Look at what you want to hit.
2. Press **V** and keep holding it. Lightning shoots from your hand (or from the head of Mjolnir or Stormbreaker, if you hold one) and hits the first creature it meets, again and again, for as long as you hold the key.
3. Let go of **V** to stop. With Mjolnir or Stormbreaker in hand the beam reaches farther and hits harder, and as the God of Thunder (**B**) harder still. It uses a little food, and it stops when your hunger bar is empty.

**Shattering Mjolnir (Sneak + R)**

1. Hold Mjolnir in your hand, and hold **Sneak** (Shift).
2. Press **R**. Mjolnir bursts into glowing pieces that fly at the enemies near you, hit them and fly back. If nobody is near, they fly out in a ring and come straight back.
3. When the last piece is home, Mjolnir is whole in your hand again. Press **R** while it is in pieces to call them home at once. You can shatter it again after 15 seconds. (Stormbreaker doesn't shatter: Sneak + **R** just throws it.)

**Pinning someone down (Sneak + right-click)**

1. Hold Mjolnir in your hand, and hold **Sneak**.
2. Right-click a creature or player. Mjolnir lies down on their chest and they can't move, jump, fly or teleport.
3. To lift it again, right-click them again without Sneak, or press **R** to call it back. Only Mjolnir's owner can, and it lets go by itself after a minute. Bosses can't be pinned. See [Thor](Thor).

## The Phoenix's mind and fire, step by step

**Reading a mind (R)**

1. Press **R**. "Mind Reading: ON" appears above your hotbar.
2. Look at a creature or player, up to 24 blocks away with nothing solid between you. A violet panel appears beside your crosshair with what they hold and wear, their mood and their health, and, for a player, their whole inventory.
3. Press **R** again to switch it off. A player you read is told "You feel a presence in your mind". Magneto's helmet, a Loki's illusion and the biggest bosses show only a line of why they can't be read.

**Mind Sense (Sneak + R) and Mind Control (B)**

1. **Sneak + R:** a ring of violet light spreads out to 48 blocks and every mind it passes glows on your screen through the walls for 6 seconds (players white, monsters pink, the rest violet). The action bar says how many it found. Wait 20 seconds before the next.
2. **B**, looking at a creature within 10 blocks: it is yours for 15 seconds and fights for you. A second one frees the first. **Sneak + B:** everything living within 16 blocks, through walls, is frozen for 3 seconds (players only with PvP on). Wait 20 seconds after Mind Control, 60 after the field.

**The Phoenix Burst (hold N)**

1. Hold **N**. You rise in flames to hover a block and a half above the ground, and a firebird forms behind you. You walk slowly while you charge, and you can be hurt.
2. Let go to unleash it: a wave of fire spreads in every direction (8 to 24 blocks, the more you charged the bigger and stronger). Three seconds is a full charge and it fires by itself. Let go within the first 0.6 seconds and the Phoenix slips away.
3. It costs food (up to 6 points), leaves you weak and slow for 5 seconds, and needs 90 seconds to rest. See [Phoenix](Phoenix).

## Spider-Man's webs, walls and legs, step by step

**Webbing something (R)**

1. Look at a creature or a block, up to 24 blocks away, and press **R**. A silver strand flies from your wrist and a splat sticks to the wall.
2. A creature is wrapped for 6 seconds: it can barely move and can't jump. A block gets a patch of web in front of it. The web blocks vanish by themselves after 10 seconds.
3. **Sneak + R** is the Web Burst: 24 strands fly out in every direction, wrapping the hostile creatures near you (villagers, animals and pets are spared), and then you wait 12 seconds. You can't be webbed yourself.

**Zipping and swinging (G)**

1. Look at a block, up to 40 blocks away, and press **G**. The web pulls you there, then it vanishes and you stick to the wall or ceiling you came to. Press **G** again for the next one.
2. To swing, hold **Sneak**, press **G** and **keep holding it**. With **Web Assist** (on by default) the web finds its own anchor ahead and above (trees, houses, cliffs), never runs you into the ground or the leaves, lets go at the right moment and sends the next web while you keep holding **G**: **W** pushes the swing, **S** brakes, **A**/**D** push sideways, and a small white web shows where the next one will go. **G** alone in mid-air swings too. Without Web Assist (the **J** key) it is the manual swing: aimed with the crosshair, **W** reels the web in, **S** lets it out. Let go of **G** and you fly off with your speed. See [Web Assist](Spider-Man#web-assist-easy-swinging-33).
3. **Space** during a zip cancels it (you keep your speed). A swing also ends if you stay on the ground for half a second, after 15 seconds, or if you are held.

**Climbing (V, on by default)**

1. Jump at a wall and keep pushing into it, or stand against it and hold **Space**. You stick. (Walking into a wall on the ground does nothing.)
2. Look along the wall and press **W** to go that way; look at the wall or up and **W** climbs; look down and **W** climbs down. **A** and **D** go sideways. Hold **Space** with your head against a ceiling to walk upside down.
3. A new press of **Space** leaps you off the wall. **V** turns the climbing off.

**The legs, Instant Kill and the Black Eyes (B, N)**

1. In the **Iron Spider** look, **B** unfolds four mechanical legs from your back, and **B** again folds them. They guard you, plant on walls, and you never take fall damage.
2. **Sneak + B** with the legs out, then **Sneak + B** again within 3 seconds to confirm: Instant Kill. The legs strike every threat around you for 10 seconds at most, then you wait 30 seconds.
3. **N** (any look; 6 food points needed) turns your eyes black for up to 30 seconds: much faster and stronger, and organic webs. **N** again calms it. When it runs out you crash for 10 seconds. See [Spider-Man](Spider-Man).

## The HUD

The top-left corner of the screen always shows your hero, each ability with its key, its cooldown, whether a toggle is on, and your resources (for example the iron shards Magneto has stored). The Scarlet Witch and Billy have a **sixth line**, `[Y] Build Magic  ON/OFF` (Billy's also shows his magic meter, `✦ 372/512`), and Magneto's first line shows `pull 4.9s` while the Magnetic Pull recharges. Billy's Hex Beam line shows its damage and reach (`6.0 dmg/s, 20 blocks`), and the Scarlet Witch's Chaos Shield line shows "∞ unlimited". **3.3:** while Billy's Hex Barrier is up its line shows a bar and its health (`41/60`) and a big bar sits under the crosshair; a sorcerer held by the Hex Runes sees a red line above the action bar, "The Hex Runes hold your powers"; Professor X's screen gets blue edges and a line with whose mind he is in while he steers a body (and the one he steers gets violet edges and "Professor X controls your body!"), and Cerebro's list; a custom hero's panel is drawn in his own colours with his name. **3.4:** a custom hero's panel also has a `[⇧R]` line under a key for its Sneak box, and shows whether each of his heroes' switches is on and his shields' strength.

## Rebinding keys

All nineteen keys are in the game's key settings: **Options, Controls, Key Binds, Enhanced Powers**. They are named *Ability 1* to *Ability 5*, *Change Look*, *Vision, That One! (Scarlet Witch)*, *Web Assist (Spider-Man)* (**J**, 3.3) and eleven *Build Magic* keys (the first is *Build Magic (Scarlet Witch, Billy)*, which is **Y**; the last is *Build Magic: Instant (Scarlet Witch)*, **Ctrl + I**). Rebinding changes the key, not the ability order: *Ability 3* always triggers the third ability in your hero's list.

## Sprinting heroes

Quicksilver and Tommy each have two separate powers in 3.3. **G** turns **Super Speed** on or off: with it on, **sprinting** is super speed (Tommy's is on from the start). **R** turns the slowing of time on or off (**Quicksilver Time**, **Tommy's Slow Time**): the world slows down around him whether he sprints or not. Use one, or both. While either is on, Quicksilver also mines, attacks, swims and uses items faster without sprinting; only the running speed and the step-up need a sprint.

**Running up walls:** with super speed on, sprint into a wall (holding **W**) and he runs up it, along a ceiling, round an overhang and down the far wall; keep **W** held and keep sprinting, or he slows and drops off; **Space** leaps off. Quicksilver needs **Wall & Water Run** on (**B**); Tommy's **Wall Run** is on unless **Sneak + R** turns it off. See [Quicksilver](Quicksilver#running-up-walls-and-across-ceilings).
