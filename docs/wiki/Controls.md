# Controls

Every hero has five abilities. They are bound to the keys **R, G, V, B, N**, in that order: ability 1 is **R**, ability 2 is **G**, ability 3 is **V**, ability 4 is **B** and ability 5 is **N**. The same keys work for every hero, and each key triggers the ability with the same number in that hero's list. Keys work only when you are a hero, no screen is open and you are not a Spectator.

| [Vision](Vision) | Mind Stone Beam (hold) | Flight (toggle) | Phase through walls (toggle) | Density (toggle) | Human Disguise (toggle) |
| [Tommy](Tommy) | Super Speed on/off (sprint) | Disarm Blitz | Whirlwind | Vibrate Through Walls | Molecular Acceleration |
| [Billy](Billy-and-the-Witches-Road) | Hex Bolt | Flight (toggle) | Telekinesis | Freeze | The Noise (toggle) |

**Press, hold, toggle.** *Press* abilities fire once per key press. *Hold* abilities (the Chaos Beam, the Shrapnel Storm, the Chaos Shield, the Mind Stone Beam, Thor's Lightning Beam) work for as long as you keep the key down (the Chaos Shield also drops by itself after 8 seconds, or when it breaks, and then has to recharge). *Toggle* abilities switch on with one press and off with the next.

The abilities, their cooldowns and costs are explained on each hero's page.

## Change Look: H

**H** changes your look, if your hero has several (**Sneak + H** goes back). It is bound as *Change Look* and works for Vision, Tommy, Billy, Loki and Thor. Magneto, the Scarlet Witch and Quicksilver have one suit each, and the key tells you "This hero has only one look". The looks of every hero are on [Looks](Looks).

## Vision, That One!: Z

The Scarlet Witch has one more key. **Z** is her order to the Vision her Hex raises (see [Scarlet Witch](Scarlet-Witch#the-vision-of-the-hex)). It works only while she has a Hex:

* **Look at a creature and press Z**: Vision goes for that one.
* **Look at nothing and press Z**: he comes back to your side.
* **Sneak + Z**: he holds his post where he is, or follows you again. The Hex Control screen (**N**) has the same choice as a button.

## Flight

Flying works like Creative mode: **double-tap Space**, or press the hero's flight key. Either one turns flight on, and the same again lands you.

| Hero | Flight key |
|---|---|
| Magneto | **N** (Magnetic Flight) |
| Scarlet Witch | **G** (Enhanced Flight) |
| Vision | **G** (Flight) |
| Billy | **G** (Flight) |
| Thor | **G**, and only with Mjolnir or Stormbreaker in hand |

Quicksilver, Tommy and Loki do not fly: Quicksilver and Tommy run, and Loki walks (and vanishes).

## Sneak changes some keys

Sneak is the usual Minecraft sneak key (Shift by default).

| Combination | What it does |
|---|---|
| Sneak + **R** as Magneto | Sets the metal you hold down gently, instead of throwing it. |
| Sneak + **V** as the Scarlet Witch or Billy | Sets what you hold with telekinesis down gently. |
| Sneak + **N** as the Scarlet Witch | Dissolves your Hex. |
| Sneak + **Z** as the Scarlet Witch | Your Hex's Vision holds his post, or follows you again. |
| Sneak + **R** as Quicksilver | Turns Super Speed on or off, instead of Quicksilver Time. |
| Sneak + **R** as Loki | Throws a fan of three daggers. |
| Sneak + **R** as Thor, with Mjolnir in hand | Shatters Mjolnir into fragments that strike his enemies (with Stormbreaker it throws it, as R does). |
| Sneak + right-click on a creature or player, with Mjolnir in hand | Pins them down under Mjolnir. Right-click them again, without Sneak, to lift it (only its owner can). |
| Sneak + **N** as Thor | Opens the Bifrost to the spot you look at. |
| Sneak + **H** | Previous look. |

With telekinesis, pressing the key again **launches** what you hold.

## Thor's beam, shatter and pin, step by step

**The lightning beam (hold V)**

1. Look at what you want to hit.
2. Press **V** and keep holding it. Lightning shoots from your hand (or from the head of Mjolnir or Stormbreaker, if you hold one) and hits the first creature it meets, again and again, for as long as you hold the key.
3. Let go of **V** to stop. With Mjolnir or Stormbreaker in hand the beam reaches farther and hits harder, and as the God of Thunder (**B**) harder still. It uses a little food, and it stops when your hunger bar is empty.

**Shattering Mjolnir (Sneak + R)**

1. Hold Mjolnir in your hand, and hold **Sneak** (Shift).
2. Press **R**. Mjolnir bursts into glowing pieces that fly at the enemies near you, hit them and fly back. If nobody is near, they fly out in a ring and come straight back.
3. When the last piece is home, Mjolnir is whole in your hand again. Press **R** while it is in pieces to call them home at once. You can shatter it again after 15 seconds. (Stormbreaker doesn't shatter: Sneak + **R** just throws it.)

**Pinning someone down (Sneak + right-click)**

1. Hold Mjolnir in your hand, and hold **Sneak**.
2. Right-click a creature or player. Mjolnir lies down on their chest and they can't move, jump, fly or teleport.
3. To lift it again, right-click them again without Sneak, or press **R** to call it back. Only Mjolnir's owner can, and it lets go by itself after a minute. Bosses can't be pinned. See [Thor](Thor).

## The HUD

The top-left corner of the screen always shows your hero, each ability with its key, its cooldown, whether a toggle is on, and your resources (for example the iron shards Magneto has stored).

## Rebinding keys

All seven keys are in the game's key settings: **Options, Controls, Key Binds, Enhanced Powers**. They are named *Ability 1* to *Ability 5*, *Change Look* and *Vision, That One! (Scarlet Witch)*. Rebinding changes the key, not the ability order: *Ability 3* always triggers the third ability in your hero's list.

## Sprinting heroes

Tommy's Super Speed starts when you **sprint**. His key (**R**) turns the ability on or off: with it off, you sprint like anyone else.

Quicksilver is different. **R** turns Quicksilver Time on or off, and the world slows down around him whether he sprints or not. **Sneak + R** turns Super Speed on or off instead, which speeds up only him (the two modes exclude each other). In both modes he also mines, attacks, swims and uses items faster without sprinting, and only the running speed and the step-up need a sprint. See [Quicksilver](Quicksilver).
