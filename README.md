# Enhanced

**[Join the official Discord](https://discord.gg/dKNCxwM5JE)** for news, help, bug reports and ideas.

A Minecraft mod where you can become one of the *Enhanced*:
**Magneto**, the Master of Magnetism; **Wanda Maximoff, the Scarlet Witch**;
**Quicksilver**, who slows the world down around him (to his own song) or just outruns it;
**Vision**, the Mind Stone in a synthetic body (or **White Vision**);
Wanda's sons **Tommy (Speed)** and **Billy (Wiccan)**; **Loki**, the God of Mischief;
**Jean Grey, the Phoenix**, who reads minds and burns with cosmic fire;
**Peter Parker, Spider-Man**, who goes everywhere on a web;
or, if you are worthy, **Thor**, with **Mjolnir** and **Stormbreaker**;
**Agatha Harkness**, whom the Darkhold makes; **Cyclops**, whose eyes only his ruby-quartz glasses hold back;
**Professor X**, young or old, who hears every mind and takes over bodies; **Doctor Strange**, whom the Eye of Agamotto makes;
**the Hulk**, whom a gamma beam makes; or, from the X-Men's secret lab under the Mutant School, **Wolverine** and **Deadpool**,
whose healing factor gets them back up from anything but the strongest powers. In the Avengers Tower wait the **Avengers**:
a **Super Soldier Serum** makes you **Captain America**, **Red Hulk** or **Falcon**, and an **Arc Reactor** makes you **Iron Man**,
with six suits; and in the End, **Eternity** grants one wish, which can make you **Love**, Thor's daughter. The six **Infinity Stones** wait in the worlds,
and the **Stone Locator** points the way to them. In the woods, **Agatha**, her son **Nicky** and **Rio, the Green Witch**,
live happily together, and when you die, Rio lets you choose where to start again. In Creative mode the **Hero Creator**
turns you into any hero, or into a hero of your own.
Three villains are bosses, and their powers are won only by beating them: **Thanos**, the Mad Titan,
who gathers up to five Infinity Stones as he fights; **Ultron**, the AI of the Mind Stone, who lifts the ground
into the sky and whose Core makes you Ultron; and En Sabah Nur, **Apocalypse**, a boss you beat to earn his Essence.

Every power's look and colour follows the MCU films and shows, and Vision, Tommy, Billy, Loki
(in his Asgardian clothes, the TVA's or the God of Stories'), Thor, the Phoenix, Spider-Man, Thanos, Ultron, Apocalypse, Agatha, Cyclops, Professor X, Heimdall, Odin, Hela, Doctor Strange, the Hulk, Wolverine, Deadpool, Captain America, Red Hulk, Falcon and Love each have several looks
(press **H**; for Iron Man, **H** puts on his suits).

## What's new in 3.19.0

* **The Multiverse.** Every place belongs to a universe: **Earth-616**, the main one, where a new world starts (every structure but the Mutant School); **Earth-10005**, the X-Men's, the only universe with the **Mutant School** now; **Earth-2149**, the zombies', where helmeted zombies walk by day and night, four times as many as a normal night, and every village is abandoned; and **endless What If universes**, as in the April Fools infinite dimensions: a renamed item names one by a number or by any words, and each asks its own question (What If... Gravity Failed? the Dead Rose? Everyone Was Quicksilver?...). See [The Multiverse](docs/wiki/Multiverse.md).
* **Three ways between them**, each with a Multiverse screen: the **Darkhold's Dream Walking** (Sneak + use, looking straight down), the **Sling Ring of a cosmic hero** (Billy the Demiurge, Cosmic Spider-Man: portals to the other universe), and **Reed Richards' Multiverse Bridge** (8 netherite blocks round an empty middle): a great ring that, powered by redstone, sends whoever stands in its middle.
* **The other universes' Infinity Stones** lie in chests, one of each per universe (the first copy taken is the stone, the others fade away), and a stone works only in its own universe.
* **Ultimate Wanda, the Sorceress Supreme** (needs Ultimate, as Billy the Demiurge does: Sneak + G as the Scarlet Witch). Twenty hearts, a netherite set's protection, every power eight times as strong, twice the healing, a faster flight, no recharge on anything. **H** switches her menu (the Scarlet Witch's keys, or her own), **Sneak + H** her hair (the films' red or the comics' black). Her own menu: **R** takes over the universe she stands in (its sky turns red for everyone in it; any number of universes at once; Sneak + R lets them all go), **B** lights runes in the skies of her universes that take the powers of every player under them, each one making her stronger (Sneak + B: her Chaos Shield), **V** takes over a body from 30 blocks like Professor X, with no time limit, any telepath's too, while a shield guards her own body (Sneak + V: reading minds as Jean Grey does, in red). She comes back a second after she dies, with all her health; nothing freezes her; the Hex Runes do not hold her and every Hex lets her through.
* **What If universes, more of them.** Gamma Bomb, Hydra Won and Ultron Won, and random universes made from any name, each different. Survivors talk in their own words and live in bunkers; a freed Hydra world stays free; Earth-2149 has a zombie of every creature, hunting on sight. Travel between universes has its own animations.
* **Ultimate Wanda grows.** Banishment to the **Nexus** between the universes, **red portals**, her own red **Eye of Agamotto** (N in her menu: it reveals the hidden, holds her foes, burns the dead and demons; Sneak: the All-Seeing Eye, 50 blocks), a **Chaos Shield nothing gets through**, a **Hex with no size limit**, a giant chaos beam (Sneak + R in the Scarlet Witch's menu) and the **Chaos Energy Eruption** (B in her Multiverse menu). She wears the Sorceress Supreme look (Sneak + H: ginger or dark hair), and her red sky has real runes in it.
* **Jean Grey, much stronger.** A bigger Phoenix Burst and firebird; telekinesis in orange with no lightning; **all her energy orange**, her force field (Sneak + G) a sphere of **burning orange fire**; she heals five times as fast as a player, fire and lava never hurt her, and her body is armoured like diamond. **Body takeover** (B at a player or at her own host): she steers the body for as long as she likes, at any distance; the Scarlet Witch, Billy and Spider-Man are immune. **Matter Manipulation** (Sneak + N, or the Build Magic key): the Scarlet Witch's Build Magic in her orange, unlimited for now (`phoenix.matterUnlimited`). A new **Brand New Day** look: a near-black green leather jacket over a yellowish olive hoodie and hood.
* **Red Skull and Captain Hydra** are heroes of their own, with Hydra's suit and shield and Hail Hydra.
* **Billy the Demiurge's Hex Barrier** never wears down and stops everything.
* **The Darkhold and the Book of the Vishanti** have new looks and pages you can read (K).
* Fixes: no crash placing the Horton Manor's signs on 26.3, the Hydra Shield renders on 26.3, the What If looks on 1.21.4 and newer, the Bridge is in the creative tab and arriving no longer freezes.
* The server and every player need 3.19 (network protocol 22).

## What's new in 3.18.3

* **The Nova Flame can be survived.** A full blast of the Human Torch's Nova Flame does 14 damage (it was 24) and burns 4 seconds, 8 at full charge (it was 6 and 12): a player at full health lives through it, and armour and tougher heroes take much less. (New setting names `jim.novaFlameDamage` and `jim.novaFlameBurnSeconds`, so every server takes the new numbers.)
* **Professor X uses the body he takes over.** In a player's body, his ability keys are that body's own powers (its hero's, with Sneak), aimed where he makes it look; his take-over key still brings him back. His right click uses what the body holds: places a block, opens a door, eats, drinks, draws and shoots a bow.
* **The Hulk heals faster:** 1.5 health a second (it was 0.35), from 3 seconds after a hurt (it was 5). Setting `hulk.regenerationPerSecond`.
* **Jumping in slowed time is slow too.** A player inside another's slowed time moved at full speed as soon as he jumped; he is slow in the air now as on the ground.
* The network protocol is 19: players need 3.18.3 on both sides.

* **The Cloak of Levitation's collar** turns with the head, so the head no longer comes through it when he turns or looks up.

## What's new in 3.18.1

* **A level system for witches.** Each of the three spellbooks now starts its caster at level 1 and grows to level 5 by experience: each level opens one more spell on a fixed key and makes the spells stronger, and level 5 is exactly the whole book of before (never stronger than the real hero: every ability of Agatha's or Strange's at most at its strength 5). Experience comes from spells that hit hostile creatures, kills, bosses (400), the book's practice and a daily study at the book's home lectern (60), with a coven bonus near another witch and a cap of 600 an hour; death costs none. Empty keys get small cantrips in the book's colour by role (Mini Shield, Blink Step, Mend, Repel, Spark, Witch Light). The Green witch gains a new spell at level 5, **Root Grasp** (B). A level line and experience bar on the HUD, a title with particles on each level-up (and a word to the whole server at level 5), and the **Book of Spells** screen when you use your book (the Ethereal witch: a button on her hat's screen). Existing casters start at level 5 and nothing changes for them. Settings in `[spellbook_levels]`; commands `/enhanced spellbook xp <players> add|set <n>` and `/enhanced spellbook level <players> <book> <1-5>`.

* **The witches are heroes of their own.** The Ethereal Witch, the Eldritch Sorcerer and the Green Witch are now in the Hero Creator's **Choose a Hero** list after the mod's heroes (a card each, with her book): Become! makes you that witch at once, at your level in her book (level 1 if you never began it), with the creator's usual checks; she keeps your own skin, and her levels work as with the book. Operators can also use `/enhanced power set <players> ethereal_witch|eldritch_sorcerer|green_witch`, and `/enhanced power get` names a witch by her own name. Every spell of the three witches has its own symbol in the drawn HUD styles (Comic, Stack, Dial, Scroll, Cluster), in her colours: her book's spells, their Sneak forms, the Root Grasp and every cantrip (a small sparkle marks a cantrip); a witch of the Demiurge's gift has symbols for her cantrips too.
* **Agatha can drain Cosmic Spider-Man.** Her Power Drain works on him though he is no sorcerer: she gains as from any drain, and it takes 25 of his cosmic power (setting `spider_man.cosmic.agathaDrain`), which he must gather again; drained to nothing, he is plain Spider-Man again.
* **Flying heroes slow down in slowed time.** A hero flying inside another's Quicksilver Time, slow time or Time Stone zone now flies at its speed too (Slowness never touched flight).
* **Billy the Demiurge (needs Ultimate, which is off by default).** Billy's ultimate version, the second after Cosmic Spider-Man: Sneak + N turns Billy into the Demiurge and back. He wears the Demiurge's starry suit with orange shoulders, a red scarf and a red cape; when he flies or uses a power he changes, with a burst of stars, into the star form: a red hood comes up, the cape grows into a great red cloak that fans out round him to the ground and flares wider as he flies, Billy's crown shines over him brighter and bluer, and rings of blue light turn at his hands; he is back to rest when he stops. He has twenty hearts, a netherite set's protection and heals faster; his Hex Barrier (Sneak + B) wraps his whole body; all his powers hit twice as hard and are drawn in a much brighter blue with small stars in the energy. **Magic Types:** H (Sneak + H: back) puts another witch's or sorcerer's magic on his keys, the Scarlet Witch's, Doctor Strange's, Agatha's or Loki's, and back to his own; he keeps the Demiurge's look and their effects turn his blue with stars, and his panel shows which magic he has. **The gift of magic:** Sneak + G opens a screen where he picks a player who has no hero and the magic to give from the Hero Creator's catalogue (magic only: the spellbooks' magic, flight, and every ability of the Scarlet Witch, Billy, Agatha, Doctor Strange and Loki); that player becomes a caster of it in his own skin, until the Demiurge takes it back. Without Ultimate none of it works, and a Demiurge whose Ultimate is taken away is plain Billy again at once. Settings in `[billy.demiurge]`; operators also have `/enhanced demiurge on|off|type|gift|take`.
* **Slowed time runs at a tenth of the normal speed, smoothly, on every version.** Quicksilver Time, Tommy's slow time, the Time Stone's Slow Time (the Infinity Gauntlet, the stone bearers, Doctor Strange's Eye of Agamotto) and a custom hero's Time power (Slow Time) now slow everything in their zone to 10% (was 20%, and a third for the Time Stone): creatures, items, falling blocks, TNT, boats, every vanilla projectile (arrows, tridents, snowballs, potions, fireballs, wither skulls, shulker bullets, llama spit, fireworks, wind charges) and the mod's own (missiles, orbs, bolts, bullets, darts, the thrown shield, Jim Hammond's fireballs, the spellbooks' shots). Creatures glide and move their limbs in slow motion instead of stepping and standing, and on 26.3 they no longer freeze for good. Other players in the zone keep about a tenth of their walking speed. Everything keeps its full strength when it hits, and its full speed once it leaves the zone. ## Quicksilver's song

Since 3.5 Quicksilver's Walkman plays his own original theme, *Silver Hours*, and Speed's plays
*Westview Blur*. Since 3.8.4 *Sweet Dreams (Are Made of This)* is no longer built into the mod. A
player who wants it puts their own copy in `config/enhanced/music/` as `sweet_dreams.mp3` (or `.ogg`
or `.wav`) and sets the client option `quicksilverSong = "sweet_dreams"`; without the file,
Quicksilver plays *Silver Hours* and the game says once where to put it.

To play a different song, start the game once (this creates the folder `config/enhanced/music/` in
your Minecraft folder) and put an `.mp3`, `.ogg` or `.wav` file there (`quicksilver.*`, `speed.*`,
`witches_road.*`, or any name for Quicksilver). A server can give every player its own songs from
`<world>/serverconfig/enhanced/music/` (`/enhanced music reload|list`). See [Music](docs/wiki/Music.md).

## Building

You need Java 21. The copy of the mod for Minecraft 26.3 is compiled with Java 25 (into Java 21 class files);
Gradle downloads a Java 25 by itself if none is installed.

GitHub Actions (`.github/workflows/build.yml`) runs the same build and the GameTests on every
push and pull request, and publishes a release for every push to `main` (or a `v*` tag).

```sh
./gradlew build              # the mod jar ends up in build/libs/
./gradlew runClient          # play in a development client
./gradlew runGameTestServer  # run the automated in-game tests
```

To install, copy `build/libs/enhanced-<version>.jar` into the `mods` folder of a NeoForge
1.21.1, 1.21.4, 1.21.11 or 26.3 instance (the jar holds a copy of the mod for each; see [docs/MULTIVERSION.md](docs/MULTIVERSION.md)).

How the heroes and bosses of 3.0 hang on the shared code (the plug-ins, the event priorities, the
boss framework and its rules) is in [docs/HEROES-AND-BOSSES.md](docs/HEROES-AND-BOSSES.md).
