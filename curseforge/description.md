# Enhanced

**[Join the official Discord](https://discord.gg/dKNCxwM5JE)** for news, help, bug reports and ideas.

**Become a hero of the Marvel films and shows, in Minecraft**, with the powers, the looks and the colours you know from the screen.

## The heroes

* **Magneto**, the Master of Magnetism; **Wanda Maximoff, the Scarlet Witch**, with her Hex, her runes and Build Magic; **Quicksilver**, who slows the world down (to his own song) or just outruns it
* **Vision** (or **White Vision**), the Mind Stone in a synthetic body, with his own mind palace; Wanda's sons **Tommy (Speed)** and **Billy (Wiccan)**
* **Loki**, **Thor** (if you are worthy) with **Mjolnir** and **Stormbreaker**, and in **Asgard**: **Heimdall**, **Odin** and **Hela**
* **Jean Grey, the Phoenix**; **Spider-Man**; **Agatha Harkness**; **Cyclops**; **Professor X**; **Doctor Strange**; **the Hulk**
* From the X-Men's secret lab: **Wolverine** and **Deadpool**, who get back up from almost anything
* From the **Avengers Tower**: **Captain America**, **Red Hulk**, **Falcon**, and **Iron Man** with six suits, from the Mark 1 to the nanotech Mark 85 and the Hulkbuster
* **Love**, Thor's daughter, a wish away from **Eternity** in the End

## Bosses

Win their powers by beating them: **Thanos**, who gathers the Infinity Stones as he fights; **Ultron**, who lifts the ground into the sky (and **Nano Ultron**); **Apocalypse**, whose golden shield only mind power breaks; and **Hela**.

## The world

* The six **Infinity Stones** to find, the **Infinity Gauntlet** and the **Snap**, and a **Stone Locator** that points the way
* Structures: **Avengers Tower**, the **Mutant School** and its lab with **Cerebro**, **Hydra outposts**, the **Sanctum Sanctorum**, **Vormir**, **Agatha's Cottage**, Mjolnir's craters, the **Shrine of the Cosmic Entities**
* Realms: **Asgard**, the **Witches' Road** with its trials, **Eternity's realm** and **Vision's mind**
* When you die, **Rio** lets you choose where to start again

## Hero Creator

In Creative mode, become any hero, or make your own: pick from more than 80 hero abilities and 30 powers, set your keys, colours, skin, cape and aura.

## Install

Minecraft **1.21.1, 1.21.4, 1.21.11 or 26.3** (the same jar), with **NeoForge** 21.1, 21.4, 21.11 or 26.3. Put the jar in the `mods` folder of the client and, for multiplayer, of the server too. **Lithium** causes visual bugs with Enhanced, so remove it.
